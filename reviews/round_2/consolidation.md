## The two trades in v3
1. **Service bargain.** Four Arab parties asked for a "no existing right lost" protection. Bennett, Yesh Atid and Yisrael Beiteinu want benefits tied to service, which in their view targets Haredi draft evaders.
   - Resolution: the protection applies to the **civil-national track (Arab citizens) this term**. Benefits tied to service and phased implementation by track (Yisrael Beiteinu's nice-to-have) apply generally.
   - In return, Ra'am votes **for** the whole Service Law (Yisrael Beiteinu) and endorses the Arab civil track (Yashar). Ra'am said in round 1 it accepts a Haredi service law, and in round 2 that the Arab track matches Abbas's position.
   - **Risk to check in round 3:** whether Ra'am accepts a binding vote on the Haredi track, since Ra'am has sometimes aligned with the Haredi parties.
2. **Settlement bargain.** The Democrats get "no new outpost legalization during the term". Bennett gets "planning and construction in existing communities **will continue** (יימשכו) under law and government decisions". Each had said it could accept the other's point.

## Correction to one reviewer's arithmetic
Bennett's reviewer wrote that the Joint List is no longer arithmetically needed. That is true only if the whole Joint List, including Balad, abstains.
- Zionist parties 48 + Ra'am 7 = 55, against 53. If Balad's ~3 votes against, the count is 56 versus 55.
- The government therefore still depends on Joint List *non-opposition*.
- Balad has committed to abstain on investiture and budgets (round 2).

## Not adopted
- A "human shields" line (Bennett): likely to cost the Arab parties' tolerance; Bennett said it was droppable.
- "...and the states of the region" (Yisrael Beiteinu): it would turn the reservation clause into a regional-normalization framing; Yisrael Beiteinu said it was droppable.
- "Renewal of dialogue with the Palestinian leadership" (Hadash): not acceptable to Yisrael Beiteinu or Yashar; Hadash said it was not decisive.
- Budget authority for the Deputy Minister (Ra'am): conflicts with Bennett's subordination requirement.
- A Joint List Finance Committee seat (Ta'al): Ta'al said it would drop it.
