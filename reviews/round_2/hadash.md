# Review of draft v2, as Hadash (חד"ש / الجبهة)

## 1. Verdict: **ACCEPT WITH CHANGES** (one small must-change; this is a convergence round)

## 2. Gains vs concessions ledger

### Is the v2 structure (Annex 1, unilateral safety net) better or worse for Hadash? **Better, on balance.**

**Why it is better**
- It is exactly our model and our promise: a safety net from outside, "no blank check" (dossier §3, Jabareen 10–13.10.2026), and the 1992 Rabin precedent (§1b).
- We vote yes on nothing.
- We sign neither clause 4 ("Jewish and democratic"), nor the "disarm Hamas" pledge (cl. 9), nor administrative detention, nor the Shin Bet role (cl. 10, 16). Annex 1 expressly disclaims clause 4 and every Basic Law.
- Our leverage is intact, and it is real:
  - If Hadash and Ta'al (about 9 seats) voted **against** together with the right, 53 + 9 = 62 would beat 55.
  - So the government lives only because we do not oppose it.
  - The safety net is conditioned on Chapter D, and we judge that condition ourselves.
- Framing Chapter D as a commitment "to all Arab citizens, not a price for votes" is good. The gains belong to our public and cannot be dismissed as a bribe.

**Why it is worse**
- Ra'am receives the visible levers:
  - a Deputy Minister (cl. 22);
  - the 60-day breach mechanism (cl. 25);
  - a seat on the monitoring committee (cl. 24).
- Ra'am will claim Chapter D as its own.
- This is partly offset because the Arab local authority heads (where Hadash is strong) sit on the committee.

### Round-1 must-changes

- **M2 (Gaza symmetry): met.**
  - Cl. 7 mourns civilians "ישראלים ופלסטינים כאחד, לרבות ברצועת עזה / الإسرائيليين والفلسطينيين على حد سواء، بما في ذلك في غزة".
  - Cl. 9 commits to consolidating the end of the war, returning hostages and bodies, and humanitarian aid and reconstruction.
- **M1 (political horizon): partly met.**
  - "העם הפלסטיני / الشعب الفلسطيني" now appears in a government text, together with civil and security coordination with the Palestinian Authority (cl. 15).
  - There is no "אופק מדיני" (political horizon) and no negotiation pledge.
  - In round 1 we said we could live without "occupation" if "the Palestinian people" appears. We keep our word.
- **M3 (belligerent occupation / תפיסה לוחמתית): dropped.**
  - The Arabic ordering «الضفة الغربية (يهودا والسامرة في القانون الإسرائيلي)» was adopted. Accepted.

### Net gains
- Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir and Smotrich are out.
- Crime: NIS 1bn a year.
- Decision 550 successor of about NIS 30bn, plus a youth plan (our demand 3, cl. 17).
- Kaminitz frozen for existing homes; fines and orders cancelled (cl. 18).
- No demolitions or forced relocation in the Negev villages (cl. 19).
- Full Arabic in government services (cl. 6).
- No annexation, no outpost money, no coalition funds; outposts evacuated (cl. 13–14).
- "Jewish terror" to be fought with equal tools (cl. 10).
- An Oct 7 commission that also covers the conduct of the war (cl. 11).
- The al-Aqsa status quo (cl. 15a).
- Judicial independence protected (cl. 3).

### Net concessions
We sign none of these and only tolerate them:
- the Service Law (6a);
- administrative detention (cl. 10);
- the Shin Bet role (cl. 16);
- "freedom of action throughout the area" (cl. 15).

### Net judgment
**Clearly positive.** Demands 1–3 are fully met. Demand 4 is met minimally ("the Palestinian people", coordination with the Palestinian Authority, no annexation).

## 3. Must-change (only one)

**Clause 6a and Annex 1: no loss of existing rights.** "State benefits are tied to service" in a general rule can work as a sanction on Arab citizens who do not serve. That breaks our "no coercion" red line (dossier §2, INN 13.10). It also echoes "no citizenship without loyalty".

- Add to the end of 6a:
  > "No citizen will lose any existing right, allowance or benefit for not serving; service adds benefits and does not condition existing ones (HE: אף אזרח לא ייפגע בזכות או בהטבה קיימת בשל אי־שירות; AR: لا يُحرم أي مواطن من حق أو امتياز قائم بسبب عدم الخدمة)."
- In Annex 1, change the last sentence to:
  > "Their declaration does not constitute endorsement of clause 4, clause 6a, or of any Basic Law."

**Flip:** with this change the verdict is **ACCEPT**. Without it, Hadash still issues the safety net, but publicly fights clause 6a and votes against the Service Law.

## 4. Nice-to-have
1. **Annex 1:** add "Hadash maintains its principled opposition to administrative detention and to the involvement of the Shin Bet in civilian policing", so that "noting clause 10" is not read as endorsing those tools.
2. **Representation:**
   - Cl. 20: the Knesset committee chair and the Arab deputy Speaker are to come from the Joint List (Ra'am already gets a committee in cl. 22).
   - Cl. 24: the Joint List receives the quarterly report and may attend the monitoring committee as an observer.
3. **Political horizon:** cl. 15 should add "and will encourage a renewal of dialogue with the Palestinian leadership". If rejected, there is no change to the verdict.

## 5. Trade space
- We drop "belligerent occupation" and "political horizon" for this term.
- We tolerate Ra'am's Deputy Minister and the Shin Bet clause.
- We accept "freedom of action" (cl. 15).
- In exchange, we ask for the 6a guarantee and the deputy Speaker or committee chair going to the Joint List.

## 6. Promise check

**Kept**
- Replace Netanyahu (Ynet, about 25.10).
- No joining and no blank check (§3).
- All four demands (N12, 11.10): crime; development, demolitions and Kaminitz; youth (via cl. 17); a minimal Palestinian-people and Palestinian Authority track.
- No endorsement of the Nation-State Law (§1b).
- Condemnation of harm to all civilians, including Gaza (§1b, 2023 line).
- Settler terror and outposts.

**Strained**
- No "occupation" or "political horizon" wording. This is mitigated: Hadash signs nothing, and Annex 1 is our own text.
- Clause 6a, if it is not fixed.
