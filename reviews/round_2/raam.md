# Ra'am review of draft v2 (round 2)

## 1. Verdict

**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.** There is one small clarifying must-change, and with it Ra'am signs.

All three of our round-1 must-changes were met:

| Round-1 demand | v2 | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A role in the fight against crime | Cl. 22: Ra'am nominee as Deputy Minister for crime and violence in Arab society, outside the cabinet. Cl. 16: the crime plan is led by that Deputy Minister. Ra'am also chairs a committee. | **Met.** Dropping the "coalition member" label is acceptable. |
| Negev | Cl. 19: "no demolition of inhabited homes and no forced relocation" (دون هدم بيوت مأهولة ودون تهجير قسري). | **Met** |
| Al-Aqsa | Cl. 15a: status quo; the Arabic text reads المسجد الأقصى / الحرم القدسي الشريف. | **Met** |

## 2. Gains vs concessions

**New gains since v1**

- **We are the only party to the agreement (Chapter E).** This is better for us than v1, which lumped us with Hadash and Ta'al. It is the 2021 model, and on paper it is stronger than 2021.
- **Crime plan run by our own nominee (cl. 16, 22),** with Shin Bet tools against criminal organizations under judicial oversight. Our voters have demanded this since the record of 252 murders in 2025.
- **Kaminitz (cl. 18):** fines and administrative demolition orders on existing homes are cancelled, explicitly including the Triangle and the Negev. That is better than 2021's freeze.
- **Monitoring with Arab mayors (cl. 24),** a 60-day remedy period, and a release clause (cl. 25).
- **Symbolic gains:**
  - Mourning for civilians "Israelis and Palestinians alike, including in Gaza" (cl. 7).
  - "The Palestinian people" (cl. 15).
  - A Nation-State Law disclaimer (cl. 4).
  - Arabic in government services (cl. 6).
  - Jewish terror fought "with the same tools", including administrative detention (cl. 10).
  - No coalition funds for settlements (cl. 13).

**Compromises made for other parties, judged by Ra'am**

- **Service Law (cl. 6a).** For Arab citizens the service is civilian, in their own communities, with no security link, and grows through incentives, not sanctions. Ra'am keeps a free vote (cl. 21).
  - This matches Abbas's own July–August 2026 position: yes to civic service for Arabs, with no military or security ties (dossier §1b, §4e).
  - **The one problem** is the general bullet "state benefits are tied to service". Read against our Negev families, it could cut existing allowances. That is a sanction in all but name. See the must-change below.
- **"Hamas, a terrorist organization under Israeli law, will be disarmed and will not rule Gaza" (cl. 9).**
  - **Acceptable.** It describes Israeli law and is a goal of the government acting "in coordination with the international framework". It is not a personal Ra'am pledge to "destroy" Hamas, which Abbas refused on Kan on 28 Jul 2026.
  - "Disarmament" (نزع السلاح) is the framework's own term. The same clause includes ending the war, the hostages, aid and reconstruction.
  - We can defend it: "We condemned the crimes of October 7" (Abbas, Kan, 28 Jul 2026).
- **Security freedom of action (cl. 15) and the existing-communities status quo (cl. 13):** tolerable. They come with no annexation, outpost evacuation and reserved positions (cl. 3).

**Net:** clearly positive. The visible gains are larger than in 2021, and the concessions are descriptive or carry a free vote.

## 3. Must-change (only one)

**Cl. 6a, after "state benefits are tied to service":**

> "Additional benefits will be granted for service; **no existing social-security right, allowance or civil service will be reduced or denied for non-service** (HE: לא תישלל ולא תופחת אף זכות או קצבה קיימת בשל אי-שירות; AR: لا يُسلب ولا يُنتقص أي حق أو مخصص قائم بسبب عدم الخدمة)."

This is consistent with "incentives, not sanctions" and with cl. 6, which bars conditioning civil rights. It costs the Zionist side nothing on the IDF and Haredi track, because the tie to benefits stays in place as a positive incentive.

**If this is fixed: ACCEPT.**

## 4. Nice-to-have (not conditions)

- **Cl. 15a, Hebrew:** "הר הבית / אל-אקצא" ("Temple Mount / al-Aqsa"), for parity with the Arabic text.
- **Cl. 18:** define "new" construction as built after the government is sworn in.
- **Cl. 22:** state that the Deputy Minister sits in the Ministry of National Security with authority over the cl. 16 budget.

## 5. Trade space

- If Yisrael Beiteinu rejects "the Palestinian people" (cl. 15), Ra'am can live with "the Palestinians" (הפלסטינים / الفلسطينيين). That is a Hadash and Ta'al demand more than ours.
- We accept everything else in v2 as is, including cl. 9 and cl. 15.
