# Round 2 review: Yashar! (ישר! עם איזנקוט) on draft v2

## 1. Verdict
**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.** One decisive, low-cost change remains. If it is made, our verdict is **ACCEPT**.

| # | Demand | v2 | Status |

## 2. Gains and concessions ledger
**Gains:**
- Eisenkot becomes PM and Netanyahu is out.
- A state commission at the first government meeting.
- All four of our rules are in the text: Jewish and democratic (cl. 4), the Declaration of Independence (cl. 4), service for all (cl. 6a), and Hamas as a terror organisation that is disarmed (cl. 9).
- Administrative detention for Jewish terror (cl. 10), as we pledged on 21 Oct.
- Smotrich's powers return to the IDF (cl. 13).
- Illegal outposts and farms evacuated; settlement only by law and government decision (cl. 13–14). This matches our "settlement must be legal" position.
- No annexation and no Palestinian state.
- Judicial independence (cl. 3).
- War on crime in Arab society, including the Shin Bet against criminal organisations (cl. 16).
- Hostages, bodies and the end of the war (cl. 9).
- The Joint List gives only a unilateral safety net, so no Joint List party is inside the government.

**Concessions:**
- The government relies on Ra'am's votes.
- A Ra'am nominee becomes Deputy Minister (cl. 22). This is outside the cabinet; a deputy minister is not a member of the government. But the public will read it as "Ra'am in the government", against our 26.9 line.
- "The Palestinian people" (cl. 15). It appears only in the reservation of positions, which we can live with.
- Symmetric mourning, "including in Gaza" (cl. 7). It is humane, cl. 8 still separates out the massacre, and it is defensible.
- Ra'am keeps a free vote on the service law.
- The Annex lets Balad join a safety-net declaration. Likud will use it as an attack line, but it is unilateral.

**Net:** clearly positive. The single real exposure is cl. 21–22. We can defend a Ra'am role only if Ra'am visibly accepts all four rules. Our test was always "whoever accepts the rules can be a partner" (dossier §3). We never said "no Arabs".

## 3. Must-change (one, decisive)
**Cl. 21: Ra'am and service (rule 3).** Replace "Otherwise it keeps freedom of vote, including on the Service Law." with:
> "Ra'am supports the civil-national service track for Arab citizens set out in clause 6a (HE: מסלול השירות האזרחי-לאומי לאזרחים הערבים; AR: مسار الخدمة المدنية للمواطنين العرب). On the other provisions of the Service Law, and on all other matters, it keeps freedom of vote."

*Why this is decisive and cheap:*
- Abbas has already said publicly that he backs civilian service for Arab citizens (PNN, 27.7.2026).
- Without this sentence, the Deputy Minister role contradicts Eisenkot's 26.9 statement: «אני לא רואה כרגע אף מפלגה… שמקבלת את הכללים האלו – כולל מנסור עבאס».
- With it, the statement becomes true and dated: then no party accepted the rules; now Ra'am does.

## 4. Nice-to-have
1. Cl. 6a: use our name for the law, "חוק שירות ממלכתי לכל", and add "whoever serves is rewarded" (מי שמשרת, מתוגמל).
2. Cl. 22: the Deputy Minister should be a professional figure with a law-enforcement background (e.g. the nominee Ra'am itself floated, a former police major-general).
3. Annex 1: state explicitly that the government is not a party to it and assumes no obligations under it.

## 5. Trade space
- We accept "the Palestinian people" in the reservation clause, the Gaza mourning sentence, the Kaminitz and Negev freezes, the al-Aqsa/Temple Mount status quo, and the Ra'am deputy-minister role.
- All of these are conditional on the cl. 21 fix.
- If Yisrael Beiteinu cannot accept "the Palestinian people", we would accept "the Palestinians" (HE: הפלסטינים; AR: الفلسطينيون) instead. We do not require it.
