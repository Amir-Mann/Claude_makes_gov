## 2. Gains and concessions ledger

**Gains**
- Netanyahu out.
- The Service Law with "no exemption quotas" (our 2026 platform almost verbatim).
- The state commission at the first meeting, with a wider scope.
- "Jewish and democratic", with the Nation-State Law **not repealed**.
- Hamas named as a terror organization and disarmed.
- Full security freedom of action (cl. 15).
- No Palestinian state, no "כיבוש" (occupation), no "Nakba".
- Area C vision preserved (cl. 3).
- Shin Bet against crime.
- Enforcement against new illegal building.
- No coalition funds (we pledged to cut them).
- The Decision 550 successor is *our* 2021 plan.
- The Joint List is reduced to a unilateral annex: no Joint List signature on the government's framework and no dependence on Balad.
- Ra'am signs "Jewish and democratic" and "Hamas, a terror organization".

**Concessions**
- Relying on Ra'am's votes, which breaks "לא נסתמך" (we won't rely on them, 26 Apr). This is the core cost, unchanged.
- An executive post for Ra'am (cl. 22). This sits uneasily with Bennett's "No" (4 Sep) to governing with Ra'am again.
- No sovereignty for 4 years.
- Kaminitz freeze and no Negev demolitions in villages under recognition.
- Administrative detention for Jewish terror suspects. Bennett signed and then reversed such an order as Defense Minister in Feb 2020 ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/in-about-face-bennett-orders-jewish-terror-suspect-be-freed/)), so it is uncomfortable but defensible under "law and judicial review" and "a small extremist minority".

**Net:** clearly positive. v2 is better for us than v1: the Joint List is moved out of the core, and the draft law and Hamas disarmament are in.

## 3. The compromises the coordinator asked about

- **Cl. 7, mourning civilians including in Gaza: acceptable.**
  - It mourns *innocent* civilians, and is immediately followed by cl. 8–9, which name Hamas as the perpetrator and a terror organization.
  - We would have liked "Hamas, which hid behind them, bears responsibility", but it is not decisive. Pushing it would cost Ra'am.
- **Cl. 15, "the Palestinian people": acceptable.**
  - It appears only where *positions are reserved* on a future settlement. Nothing grants self-determination or statehood.
  - Bennett's own Stability Plan *is* a settlement with the Palestinians, namely autonomy.
  - It sits next to "full security freedom of action" and "no final-status decision".
- **Cl. 4, Nation-State Law reservation: acceptable.** It is neutral and nothing is repealed. Bennett himself sought a Druze fix in 2018.
- **Cl. 22, Ra'am deputy minister: the decisive point.**
  - A Ra'am nominee as Deputy Minister fighting Arab crime is sellable, especially if it is Segalovitz: a Jewish former police major-general and former Yesh Atid MK.
  - But it must read as an appointee working under the government's own minister, not as "Ra'am inside the government".

## 4. Must-change (one)

**Cl. 22.** Replace with:

> "Ra'am's nominee, **with the approval of the Prime Minister**, will serve as Deputy Minister for the fight against crime and violence in Arab society, **under the responsible minister and the government's policy**, outside the cabinet and without a vote in the security cabinet (HE: סגן שר בכפיפות לשר הממונה, מחוץ לממשלה ולקבינט; AR: نائب وزير تابع للوزير المسؤول، خارج الحكومة والكابينت). Ra'am will also chair one Knesset committee."

If this is adopted, the verdict flips to **ACCEPT**.

## 5. Nice-to-haves (not conditions)

1. Cl. 13: "planning and construction … **will continue** in accordance with the law and government decisions" (HE: יימשכו).
2. Cl. 7: add "and condemn the use of civilians as human shields" (HE: שימוש באזרחים כמגן אנושי; AR: استخدام المدنيين دروعًا بشرية).
3. Cl. 10: "dismantling of violent hubs" → "dismantling of illegal violent hubs" (HE: מוקדי אלימות בלתי חוקיים), so that it reads as aimed at outposts, not communities.

- **Would drop:** nice-to-haves 1–3 if they endanger Ra'am's signature.
