## 3. Must-change (one, decisive)

**Clause 14, second and third sentences.** v1 said "No outpost will be retroactively legalized during the term". v2 limits that ban to private land and adds "any other regularization… by government decision".
- This reopens the door to "הסדרה" (legalization) of the 100+ outposts Golan promised to dismantle.
- Legalizing outposts is on our deal-breaker list ([dossier §6](../../research/democrats.md)).
- We sit in the cabinet as a minority, so "by government decision" offers us no protection.

Replace with:
> "No new outpost will be legalized during the term (HE: לא יוכשר במהלך הקדנציה אף מאחז בלתי מורשה; AR: لن تُشرعَن أي بؤرة استيطانية خلال الولاية). Only steps already decided by a government decision before 27 October 2026 may proceed, under law and judicial review; none on private Palestinian land."

This keeps Bennett's protection for existing communities and past legal decisions. It blocks fresh legalizations in this term. Then **ACCEPT**.

## 4. Nice-to-haves
1. **Clause 5:** add "on the basis of civic Jewish-Arab partnership" (HE: שותפות אזרחית יהודית-ערבית; AR: شراكة مدنية يهودية-عربية). This is Golan's own phrase from 26 Sep.
2. **Clause 14:** publish quarterly progress on the evacuation timetable, as clause 24 does for Chapter D.

## 5. Trade space
- We will accept YB's objection to "Palestinian people", if it comes, provided "the Palestinians" (הפלסטינים) remains.
- We will not reopen the "partnership" wording.
- We accept the judiciary "broad consent" clause and the Service Law's tie between service and benefits. Meretz may grumble about the latter.
