# Ta'al: review of draft v2 (round 2)

## 1. Verdict

**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES** (one cheap, decisive item). If it is adopted, the verdict is **ACCEPT**.

## 2. Gains vs concessions ledger

The ledger is written from Ta'al's worldview (dossier `research/taal.md`).

**Round-1 must-changes: all substantially met**
- **Clause 4 (the reservation):** met in full. "Describes the existing law… no party is taken to endorse… Basic Law: Israel – the Nation-State… Each party keeps its freedom to act lawfully on it." Annex 1 adds: "does not constitute endorsement of clause 4 or of any Basic Law." This is stronger than we asked.
- **Clause 15 ("Palestinian people", העם הפלסטיני / الشعب الفلسطيني):** met, together with civil and security coordination with the PA. "Positions… are fully reserved" is weaker than "will not close off a settlement". Tibi already said this is "not a peace government" (JPost, 22 Oct 2026), so we accept it.
- **Clause 18 (Kaminitz):** met for the core demand. Enforcement against existing inhabited homes is frozen immediately; administrative fines and orders are cancelled within 6 months; the Triangle and the Negev are named. With clause 19 (no demolition or forced relocation in the villages), this is the strongest demolitions text any Israeli government has offered. Enforcement against *new* building continues; we can live with that.

**New gains in v2**
- Mourning "Israelis and Palestinians alike, including in Gaza" (cl. 7).
- An end to the war and the return of hostages (cl. 9).
- The status quo at al-Aqsa / al-Haram al-Sharif (cl. 15a).
- No coalition funds and no money for outposts (cl. 13).
- Jewish terror fought "with the same tools" as Palestinian terror (cl. 10).
- A new Knesset Speaker in the first week, i.e. Ohana is out (cl. 23). This is a campaign promise kept.
- Arabic in government services (cl. 6).
- Targets for teachers, medical residencies and youth (cl. 17).
- Arab mayors on the monitoring committee (cl. 24).

**Is the new structure (Annex 1) better or worse for Ta'al? Better on balance.**
- *Better:*
  - It is exactly Tibi's doctrine of a "safety net from outside" without joining the coalition (§2–3 of the dossier).
  - We sign no text that contains "Jewish and democratic", "disarm Hamas" or the Service Law.
  - We have no duty to vote yes, not even on investiture or budgets.
  - Chapter D as "a commitment to all Arab citizens, not a price for votes" means we never have to defend a budget as a bribe.
- *Worse:*
  - The institutional credit goes to Ra'am: the deputy minister, a committee chair, a seat on the monitoring committee, and the clause 25 enforcement lever.
  - "An Arab deputy Speaker" (cl. 20) could also go to Ra'am, although a deputy Speaker post was part of the Joint List deal (ToI, 19 Aug 2026).
  - Voters will ask what the largest Arab list got in its own name.

**Concessions.** Ta'al gives only an undertaking not to bring the government down while Chapter D is implemented. It concedes nothing in words.

**Net:** clearly positive. The text delivers everything we campaigned for except a political horizon, at almost zero ideological cost. The one gap is visible standing for the Joint List itself.

## 3. Must-change (one)

**Clause 20 plus clause 24: give the Joint List visible standing.** Replace "an Arab deputy Speaker" in clause 20 with:
> "a deputy Speaker from each of the two Arab lists, Ra'am and the Joint List (סגן יו"ר הכנסת מכל אחת מהרשימות הערביות / نائب لرئيس الكنيست من كل من القائمتين العربيتين)"

Add to clause 24:
> "Representatives of the factions that made the declaration in Annex 1 will take part in the monitoring committee and receive its reports."

Neither costs the Zionist parties a minister, a vote or a word of principle.

## 4. Nice-to-have (at most 3)

- **Clause 6a:** add
  > "During the term, no existing right or benefit will be withdrawn from citizens who do not serve."

  Otherwise "benefits tied to service" can be read as a sanction on Arab youth, which contradicts "incentives, not sanctions".
- **A Joint List seat on the Finance Committee** (ועדת הכספים / لجنة المالية).
- **Clause 15:** add "and will avoid steps that close off a future political settlement". This is Hadash's priority more than ours.

## 5. Trade space

- We drop the Finance Committee seat and the clause 15 addition if the must-change is adopted.
- We accept the Service Law's existence, the "disarm" clause and administrative detention (we do not sign them).
- We would accept a monitoring role with observer status only, rather than full membership.

## 6. Promise check

**Kept:**
- Topple Netanyahu without letting him remain caretaker.
- Outside support only.
- No acceptance of the Nation-State Law (cl. 4 and Annex).
- Crime plan, demolitions, Kaminitz, master plans, Negev, local-authority budgets.
- No vote for settlements.
- Ohana replaced.
- Teachers and doctors.

**Partly kept:**
- Deputy Speaker (needs the must-change).
- Finance Committee (not delivered).

**Accepted in advance as not delivered:** a political horizon.
