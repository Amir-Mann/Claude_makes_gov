# Round 2 review — BALAD (التجمع الوطني الديمقراطي / בל"ד), non-signatory; candidate for Annex 1

## 1. Verdict: ACCEPT WITH CHANGES (one change, needed only for Balad to join Annex 1)

**Round-1 must-changes**
1. **Clause 7, symmetry on civilians: met verbatim.** "Israelis and Palestinians alike, including in Gaza" (الإسرائيليين والفلسطينيين على حد سواء، بما في ذلك في غزة).
2. **Clause 9, end of the war: met.** "consolidate the end of the war and prevent its resumption" now appears, together with hostages and bodies, aid and reconstruction.
3. **Clause 13, money: met.** "no allocations to unauthorized outposts and no coalition funds".
   - **Arabic place name: acceptable.** «الضفة الغربية (يهودا والسامرة في القانون الإسرائيلي)» puts our name first and attributes the other name to Israeli law, not to us.

**Votes**
- **Investiture: Balad abstains.** Under v2 our abstention is now decisive: if Hadash and Ta'al abstain, then 55 vs 53 + 3 against would fail. We abstain because toppling the "government of bloodshed" is our mandate.
- **Budgets: Balad abstains** while clause 13 (no outpost or coalition money) and the Chapter D funding are honoured in each budget law.
  - If either is breached, we vote against.
  - Abstaining is not voting for: we still vote for no war or settlement budget.

**Annex 1:** Balad joins, if the change below is made.

## 2. Ledger from Balad's point of view

**Gains (new in v2 in bold)**
- Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir and Smotrich out.
- NIS 1bn a year against crime.
- A successor to Decision 550 worth at least NIS 30bn.
- **Kaminitz fines and demolition orders cancelled for existing homes.**
- **No demolitions and no forced relocation (تهجير قسري) in the Negev villages.**
- **Government services fully available in Arabic.**
- **The al-Aqsa / al-Haram al-Sharif status quo** (المسجد الأقصى / الحرم القدسي الشريف).
- **"The Palestinian people" (الشعب الفلسطيني) named.**
- **Coordination with the PA.**
- **A Nation-State Law disclaimer.**
- Jewish terror named (الإرهاب اليهودي), **with the same tools used against Palestinian terror**.
- Outposts evacuated; no annexation.
- **Gaza's dead mourned; the end of the war consolidated.**
- Arab mayors on the monitoring committee.

**Concessions** (in a text we do not sign)
- Hamas "a terrorist organization under Israeli law … disarmed" (clause 9).
- A Service Law tying benefits to service (6a).
- The Shin Bet in crime-fighting (16).
- Administrative detention as a tool (10). We oppose it in principle for everyone.
- IDF "freedom of operational action" throughout the West Bank (15).
- No "occupation" (الاحتلال).

**Net:** for an outside, non-signing faction, the gains clearly outweigh the concessions, which are other parties' words. As written, though, Annex 1 would make us "note in particular" all of Chapter C, including clause 15, and all of Chapter D, including the Shin Bet. That is the only problem.

## 3. Must-change (decisive only for Balad joining Annex 1)

**Annex 1, last two sentences.** Replace "note in particular Chapters B (clauses 7, 10), C and D" with:
"note in particular clauses 7, 10, 12–14, 15a and 16–20."

Replace the final sentence with:
"Their declaration does not constitute endorsement of any clause not listed above, nor of any Basic Law."
(AR: «لا يشكّل هذا الإعلان تأييداً لأي بند غير مذكور أعلاه، ولا لأي قانون أساس»)

If this is fixed, the verdict is ACCEPT and Balad signs Annex 1. Hadash and Ta'al lose nothing by the change. If it is not fixed, Balad still abstains on investiture and budgets, but stays outside Annex 1 and says so publicly without attacking Hadash or Ta'al.

## 4. Nice-to-have (not decisive)
- Clause 6a: add "No Arab citizen will lose any existing right or benefit for not serving during this term" (AR: «لن يُحرم أي مواطن عربي من حق أو امتياز قائم بسبب عدم الخدمة»). This makes "incentives, not sanctions" unambiguous.
- Clause 16: "the Shin Bet *only* against criminal organizations, never for political surveillance of Arab citizens."

## 5. Trade space
- We drop the Section 7A demand and the word "occupation".
- We accept "military control" and Hadash's Arabic ordering of the place names.
- We will not oppose the Service Law in public campaigns; we vote against it freely.

## 6. Promise check
**Kept**
- Not in a Zionist coalition.
- No vote for war or settlement budgets.
- Netanyahu toppled.
- Crime plan, an end to demolitions, the Negev.
- No affirmation of a "Jewish state" by Balad (dossier §3, §4a). Annex 1 explicitly disclaims clause 4.

**Strained**
- Abstaining on budgets breaks our record of voting against every budget (dossier §1b).
- The Shin Bet and Service Law clauses exist in a text our partners tolerate.

**To voters:**
«أسقطنا حكومة الدم دون أن نوقّع على يهودية الدولة أو نصوّت لميزانية حرب أو استيطان، وانتزعنا وقف الهدم والاعتراف بقرى النقب وخطة ممولة ضد الجريمة.»
«امتناعنا أداة لحماية شعبنا، لا تنازل عن مشروع دولة جميع مواطنيها.»

English: "We toppled the government of bloodshed without signing the state's Jewishness or voting for a war or settlement budget, and we won a halt to demolitions, recognition of the Negev villages and a funded crime plan. Our abstention is a tool to protect our people, not a surrender of the state-of-all-its-citizens project."
