## 2. Lines we would refuse as written

**Part D, budget sentence, in both languages:**
- Arabic: «لن يتغيّب التجمع عن التصويت على الميزانية إلا إذا…»
- Hebrew: "בל"ד תיעדר מההצבעות על התקציב רק אם…"

The sentence drops our consequence. As written, a budget that funds annexation or leaves Part B unfunded would only mean Balad turns up, not that it votes no. In round 3 we stated that Balad then votes **against**.

Add at the end:
- Arabic: «…وموّلت الجزء ب؛ وإلا يصوّت نوابه ضدها.»
- Hebrew: "…והוא יממן את חלק ב'; אחרת יצביעו חבריה נגד."

**TV statement (c): «منذ بداية الحرب» is acceptable.**
- The wording is symmetric and names no perpetrator.
- It ties our condemnation to neither side's framing.
- Our base reads it as the war on Gaza; Israeli viewers can read it as including 7 October.
- Hadash's 7 October condemnation stays in Jabareen's own remarks, which Balad does not read.

We do not need the date removed. The rest of (c) is as we asked: the platform's state wording, and our closing line on disqualification and service.
