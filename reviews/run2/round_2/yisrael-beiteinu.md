# Round 2 (run 2): Yisrael Beiteinu on v5

*Simulated negotiating team.* Items marked **[BK]** are background knowledge. The search quota was used up, so they were not re-verified in this run.

## 1. The real question: new elections with Netanyahu as caretaker, or S2+?

**The answer now is new elections.** That is not a bluff. The record also shows that this is not his last word.

**The record cuts both ways**
- **2019.** He refused Netanyahu and the Haredim and forced repeat elections, twice. He also refused a Gantz minority government in late 2019.
- **March 2020 [BK].** After the third election, he backed a Gantz-led government that would rest on outside Joint List support. It died because Hendel, Hauser and Levy-Abekasis refused, not because of him.
- **2021.** He sat in a government that rested on Ra'am.

So Liberman does bend toward Arab-supported structures. He does it **only after at least one repeat election has proved there is no Zionist majority**, never 48 hours after a campaign pledge built specifically to block exactly this. The 15 Oct pledge, "גם לא בממשלת מיעוט לתקופת מעבר", was aimed at S2 itself.

**Why elections are rational for him now**
1. **His word is his brand.** His voters' story since 2019 is "Liberman says no and means it." Breaking that within a week costs more than any portfolio.
2. **Bill 3 changes the price of elections.** If the defendant law passes in this Knesset, Netanyahu is a lame-duck caretaker who cannot be tasked again. The March 2027 election then becomes a contest over who leads the Zionist camp. YB, holding a draft law and clean hands, is well placed in that contest.
3. **Bennett will not move first while we hold.** Bennett's round-1 file says exactly that. Neither of us gains by breaking first.

**What we keep open, honestly**
- After the next election, if it again produces no Zionist 61 and Likud again keeps Netanyahu, the 2020 and 2021 precedent comes back on the table.
- We do not say this publicly, and we will not sign anything that pre-commits us now.

## 2. P1: "Knesset first". Which of the four bills?

We vote for **all four**. Voting the same way as Ra'am and the Joint List on bills, with nothing signed, is acceptable. These are our bills; whoever votes for them is supporting us.

| Bill | Vote | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Replace the Speaker | For | Our own 17 Oct pledge (replace Ohana). |
| 2. State commission into 7 Oct | For | Our "first decision". |
| 3. Defendant law | For, and we co-sponsor | Our pledge "through all three readings". |
| 4. Service for All (v4 clause 6a) | For | Only with automatic personal sanctions in (d), and with "the principle of equal burden applies to all citizens". We vote against any quota amendment. |

**Additional demand:** a fixed dissolution date (March 2027) passed together with bill 3. The Knesset legislates, then goes to the voters.

## 3. P2: S2+ (if P1 fails)

**We reject it.**
- The open letter to the National Committee changes the envelope, not the arithmetic. Investiture passes 55–53 only with Ra'am's yes.
- Under S2+, YB does not join. If it is put to a vote, we vote **against**.

**On the elements, for the record, so they can be reused in any later government**
- **Segalovitz as coordinator.** A former police major-general, appointed by the Minister of Public Security and outside the government, is **not** our objection in itself. Our objection is the dependence. Within S4a we could accept a professional of his record in the post. We will not accept it as Ra'am's price.
- **Horizon sentence.** We reject "will take no step that precludes a future settlement", because it would bind our Areas A and B / Jordan option. Acceptable wording: "Israel will maintain security coordination in Judea and Samaria. Long-term positions are reserved under clause 3." In Hebrew: «ישראל תקיים תיאום ביטחוני ביהודה ושומרון; העמדות לטווח ארוך שמורות כאמור בסעיף 3». We do not write the PA's survival into the text: we regard it as a terror-supporting body.
- **Letters to the Arab parties.** Whether they suffice is for those parties to judge. Any such letter keeps YB out. Our Annex 2 line stays.
