# Balad — run 2, round 2 (draft v5)

## The scenario changes our calculation

The alternative is now Netanyahu as caretaker until March 2027 or later. Ben-Gvir would still be in charge of the police in our towns.
Our voters turned out at about 80% to end that. Voting against is therefore a much more expensive card than it was in round 1, and we
say openly that we are moving.

## P1 — "Knesset first": how Balad's 3 MKs vote

| Bill | Vote | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Replace the Speaker | **For** | Abu Shehadeh called for removing Ohana on 28 Oct. |
| 2. State commission into 7 Oct | **For**, if the mandate covers "the conduct of the war"; otherwise **absent** | It must not become a tribunal against Arab citizens (see Cassif and Abu Shehadeh). |
| 3. Defendant law | **For** | See below. |
| 4. Service for All | **Against** | Our platform reads: "We reject military and 'civil' service schemes, all forms of conscription." This includes 6a(c) "voluntary with incentives". |

**Bill 3, knowing it may produce S4a without us: yes, we vote for it.**
- Our mandate is to remove Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir and Smotrich, not to be in government. We never wanted to be.
- Opposition to a Likud–Yashar–Bennett government is our natural place. We would lead an Arab opposition of 19 seats with clean hands.
- A government without Netanyahu and **without Ben-Gvir and Smotrich** is a gain for our people even if it is right-of-centre.
- We record one reservation. We support this bill as **a rule about tasking a government**, not about who may stand for election. Balad will never vote to widen grounds for disqualifying lists or candidates.

## P2 — "S2+", the public letter to the National Committee of Arab Local Authority Heads

**The form suits Balad better than round 1 did.** S2 was our first preference: Balad signs nothing with a Zionist government. A
commitment addressed to our society's elected local leadership, not to the parties, is a "safety net for our people"
(«شبكة أمان لأهلنا وناسنا وشعبنا»), not a deal.

**What the letter must contain for Balad's 3 MKs to be absent:**
1. **Anti-disqualification line (non-negotiable):**
   > "The government will not initiate or support legislation to widen the grounds for disqualifying lists or candidates."
   >
   > «لن تبادر الحكومة أو تدعم تشريعًا لتوسيع أسباب شطب القوائم والمرشحين».

   Yesh Atid and the Democrats accept this. Yashar wants to delete it, from the party that backed disqualifying our chair.
   If Yashar deletes it, we cannot explain our absence to our voters.
2. **Chapter D with dates:**
   - an immediate Kaminitz freeze, and the amendment within 6 months;
   - no demolition of inhabited homes in the Negev, with relocation only "agreed with the residents" («بموافقة السكان»);
   - NIS 1bn a year for the crime plan;
   - the Decision 550 successor in the first budget.
3. **The crime post.** We have no veto on Segalovitz as coordinator. Balad records its objection to the Shin Bet having any role in
   civilian policing.
4. **The al-Aqsa breach clause.**

**The horizon sentence:** it is weak. It omits "occupation" and "the Palestinian people". We will not hold the government hostage on
it; that fight belongs to Hadash. Our public answer will say «الاحتلال» and «الشعب الفلسطيني» in our own words.

**What we drop from round 1:**
- We **no longer require** "Zionist, statist" to be removed from the government's own guidelines. It is their self-description, in a text we do not sign.
- Lieberman's "we do not count on the Joint List" can be his speech, as long as it is **not written into any government document**.

**What does not move:**
- No Balad signature on any text.
- **Absent, not abstain**, on investiture and budgets.
- Absence on each budget is conditional on no annexation or outpost money (clause 13) and on Chapter D being funded. If either fails, Balad votes **against**.
- Against the Service Law.

**Investiture vote under S2+:** **all 3 absent**, in coordination with the Joint List.
- If the anti-disqualification line is missing, we vote **against**.
- Arithmetic: 55 for, 53 against, if Bennett and Yisrael Beiteinu vote yes. If they vote no, the government falls anyway (41 for, 67 against), and our vote makes no difference.

**A list split is still possible.** If Hadash abstains in the hall while we are absent, the effect is the same.
