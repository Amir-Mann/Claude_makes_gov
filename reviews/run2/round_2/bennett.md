# Round 2 review: Bennett 2026 (7 of Together's 12)

*Simulation. This does not speak for any real party. The basis is `research/bennett.md` and round 1.*

## 1. The real question: new elections under caretaker Netanyahu, or S2+?

**Answer: Bennett chooses new elections, and his default does not move.**

His 2021 record is the strongest argument against us, and we face it squarely. In 2021 he said: "I had an option to go to fifth, sixth, seventh elections and tear Israel apart" (JPost, 4 Sep 2026). He chose Ra'am then. Three things are different now, and each one decides the case:
1. **In 2021 the prize was the premiership.** In S2+ the PM is Eisenkot. Breaking the pledge a second time, for someone else's government, is a bad trade by any politician's arithmetic.
2. **The pledge was written after 7 October, specifically to close the 2021 door.** The words were: "אין מנדט לממשלה **שנתמכת** במפלגות ערביות" (no mandate for a government *supported* by Arab parties). S2+ lives on Ra'am's yes votes (55 to 53) and on the Joint List, including Balad, staying away. Without them it is 55 against 65. This is exactly the abstention model he rejected in February.
3. **He would be first and alone.** Liberman refuses, so S2+ has 41 seats without both of us. Bennett will not pay the price of breaking his word for a government that still cannot be formed.

**The cost, stated honestly:** Netanyahu stays caretaker until about March 2027, and our voters will hold that against us. Our answer: "Likud chose Netanyahu over a government. I offered them one without him." We run again on a clean record.

**A conditional path (our estimate: about 20%).** Bennett could move only if all four of these hold:
- (a) P1's defendant law has passed, and Likud still blocks a government without Netanyahu. Likud, not the Arab parties, would then visibly be the obstacle.
- (b) Liberman moves at the same time, in one joint announcement.
- (c) There is a rotation with Bennett in it. The premiership is the only prize large enough, as in 2021.
- (d) Nothing is signed with, or addressed to, any Arab faction.

If any one of these is missing, it is elections.

## 4. TV statement

**T2 is confirmed, only if a government forms.**

**(a) Lines we need in the joint reading:**
- «ממשלה ציונית של ברית המשרתים» (a Zionist government of the alliance of those who serve)
- «חמאס הוא ארגון טרור והוא יפורק מנשקו» (Hamas is a terror organization and will be disarmed)
- «לא תקום מדינה פלסטינית בקדנציה הזאת ולא בשום קונסטלציה מטעמנו» (no Palestinian state will be established in this term, nor in any constellation by our hand)
- «ועדת חקירה ממלכתית תוך 14 יום» (a state commission of inquiry within 14 days)
- «נאכוף את החוק נגד קומץ אלים, ונגד בנייה בלתי חוקית – יהודית ופלסטינית» (we will enforce the law against a violent handful, and against illegal construction, Jewish and Palestinian)

**Lines we will not say:**
- "with the support of the Arab parties";
- "including in Gaza" in a joint mourning line;
- "occupation", "the Palestinian people".

**(b) Arab statements:** made separately and on their own platforms, not on our stage, and never introduced as "our partners".
