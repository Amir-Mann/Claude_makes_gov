## P2: S2+ if P1 fails

**The letter.** It is our bridge and we stand behind it.
- The PM-designate publishes Chapter D, with numbers and dates, the crime post and the al-Aqsa breach clause.
- It is an open public letter to the **National Committee of Arab Local Authority Heads**.
- **It is not addressed to any faction, and it does not name Ra'am or the Joint List.** That is the whole point.
- So, honestly:
  - It does **not** meet Ra'am's demand for a matching letter to Ra'am.
  - It does **not** meet Hadash's demand for a signed letter naming the Joint List.
  - It **does** meet Ta'al's "safety net for our society", because the commitments run to citizens, are public, and are monitored quarterly.
- Every faction answers publicly. We do not negotiate what they answer.

**Horizon sentence (in the letter only):** accepted as worded.
- "הממשלה תקיים תיאום עם הרשות הפלסטינית ולא תנקוט צעד המונע הסדר עתידי."
- It is consistent with "no annexation" and "separation".
- We reject "העם הפלסטיני" and "renew a diplomatic process": Eisenkot said "בממשלה שנקים לא תקום מדינה פלסטינית".

**Segalovitz.** Acceptable as **government coordinator with powers delegated by the Minister of Public Security**, outside the government. Two conditions:
- he states the four rules publicly and in his own name before he is appointed, including "חמאס ארגון טרור שיש לפרקו";
- he is appointed by the government, not under a Ra'am agreement.

He is a former police major-general, so this is the professional appointment we promised. "Deputy-minister powers" and a seat on the ministerial committee: **no**.

**Our own choice: S2+ or new elections under Netanyahu as caretaker.**
- **Yashar prefers S2+.** Eisenkot said on election night that another election "would be a mistake".
- A Netanyahu caretaker government until spring 2027 or later is "the October 7 government" we promised to send home.
- S2+ keeps every pledge we actually made:
  - Ra'am is not in the government;
  - nothing is signed with any Arab faction;
  - partners accept the four rules.
- But S2+ needs **both** Bennett and Lieberman. Without either, it is 48 against 53. We will not present a government that fails on the floor, and we will not cross into S3.
- So the real Yashar move is pressure, not a breach:
  - Eisenkot asks Herzog for the mandate on the recommendations;
  - he publishes the letter and the guidelines;
  - he tells Bennett and Lieberman publicly that the choice is S2+ or a caretaker Netanyahu: "Choose."
- If they choose elections, we go to elections as the Zionist alternative with clean hands, and we do not blame Ra'am.

**Investiture vote:** for (21) under S4a or S2+. Yashar does not join S3 without the four rules.
