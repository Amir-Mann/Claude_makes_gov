# Hadash: round 2 response to draft v5 (run 2)

*Simulated negotiating team of Hadash, about 6 of the Joint List's 12 MKs. This is a simulation and does not speak for the real party.*

## 1. P1, "Knesset first": how we vote on the four bills

| Bill | Hadash vote | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Replace the Speaker | **For** | This is a must-have shared with Ta'al. The Joint List's votes are counted openly. |
| 2. State commission law (7 Oct) | **For**, if its mandate covers the conduct of the war, including the humanitarian policy in Gaza. **Abstain** if the mandate stops at 7 Oct. | We want accountability for the "government of blood", not just a review of military failures. |
| 3. "Defendant law" | **For** | See below. |
| 4. Service for All | **Against** | Jabareen: "We will not accept any coercion… related to national service." |

**Bill 3, even if it produces a unity government without us: yes, we still vote for it.**
- We promised to end Netanyahu's rule, not to preserve our own leverage.
- A unity government without Netanyahu is worse than a change government, but better than Netanyahu as caretaker until 2027. We would lead the opposition with clean hands.

## 2. P2, "S2+": does an open letter to the National Committee of Arab Local Authority Heads meet our demand?

**Partly. We move, on three conditions.**

We drop the demand that the letter name the Joint List. A public letter to Arab society's own elected bodies fits "a safety net for our society, not for the government". Our counterpart becomes **our own published reply**, which sets out our conditions and when our commitment lapses.

**Conditions:**
1. **Addressees.** The letter is also addressed to the High Follow-Up Committee (لجنة المتابعة العليا). The Local Authority Heads alone do not represent the political public.
2. **Horizon sentence.** The mediator's version is not enough. Our floor:
   > "…will maintain and strengthen coordination with the Palestinian Authority, will take no step that precludes a future settlement **between Israel and the Palestinian people**, and **will respond positively to regional and international efforts toward a political settlement**."
   >
   > «…ولن تتخذ أي خطوة تحول دون تسوية مستقبلية بين إسرائيل والشعب الفلسطيني، وستتجاوب إيجابيًا مع الجهود الإقليمية والدولية للتوصل إلى تسوية سياسية».

   - We give up "renew a diplomatic process with the Palestinian leadership".
   - We do **not** give up "the Palestinian people". It costs Yashar four words, and Yesh Atid and Ra'am already accept it.
3. **Content.** The letter carries:
   - Chapter D with dates;
   - Kaminitz frozen on the day of swearing-in;
   - no forced relocation in the Negev without the residents' agreement;
   - clauses 13[A] or [C] and 14;
   - the al-Aqsa trigger;
   - a monitoring committee in which the Joint List sits as an equal member.

**How Hadash votes under S2+ if the conditions are met:**
- **Investiture:** abstain, present in the hall.
- **Our reply** promises that we will not support no-confidence or dissolution motions while the letter is being implemented. The commitment lapses 60 days after a breach that is not remedied.
- **Budgets:** we abstain if they contain no settlement or war additions and fund Chapter D.

**If the conditions are not met:** we abstain once on investiture, so as not to keep Netanyahu as caretaker, and give no safety net.

**Segalovitz as coordinator.** This is acceptable, but not as Ra'am's patronage:
- he reports to the monitoring committee;
- his budget is in the letter;
- no Shin Bet role in civilian policing.

We prefer this to a Ra'am deputy minister.
