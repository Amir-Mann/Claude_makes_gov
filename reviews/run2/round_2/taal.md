# Ta'al (Ahmad Tibi): run 2, round 2 response to v5

*Simulation exercise; speaks for no real party.*

We accept the mediator's scenario: Netanyahu keeps Likud, so the real alternative to a deal is elections with him as caretaker. Tibi promised voters «We will not allow Netanyahu to be caretaker prime minister». That pushes Ta'al toward a deal. It does not push us toward signing anything about identity.

## P1: "Knesset first"

| Bill | Ta'al vote | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Replace the Speaker | **For** | This was our first campaign promise: "remove Ohana… a Kahanist and racist arena." |
| 2. 7 Oct state commission | **For** | It puts the responsibility of Netanyahu's government on record. |
| 3. Defendant law | **For** | This is Tibi's own demand (JPost, 22 Oct). |
| 4. Service for All (v4 6a) | **Against** as written. Abstain only if the conditions below are met. | See below. |

**Bill 4 conditions for an abstention:**
- 6a(b) must state that no benefit in land, housing, employment or higher education is conditioned on service.
- 6a(c) must state: "imposes no obligation and sets no targets for citizens not liable." In Arabic: «لا يفرض أي واجب ولا يضع أهدافاً على غير الملزمين».
- The Haredi draft itself is not our fight. Arab youth are.

**Bill 3 if it produces S4a:** yes, we still vote for it, knowing it may produce a Likud-without-Netanyahu government without us.
- We cannot vote against a law we demanded.
- Our first goal is "to bring down this government". Tibi said: «we are choosing between the bad and the worse».
- A Likud government without Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir and Smotrich is "less bad", and we would oppose it from the opposition.

**Recommendation to the President:** Ta'al supports a unified Joint List recommendation of Eisenkot, as in 2019 and 2020, if he asks for it. Without a mandate in his hands, the Knesset-first track has no clock.

## P2: S2+ and the open letter to the National Committee of Arab Local Authority Heads

**Does it meet "a safety net for our society"? Mostly yes.**
- Tibi's phrase was «safety net for you, for our society», not "for our party".
- A public, dated commitment to Arab society is a stronger political asset than a private letter to a faction.
- It is also something Balad can live with.

Ta'al accepts the open letter in place of Annex 1 [A] if it contains:
1. **Chapter D with numbers and dates.**
   - NIS 1bn a year for crime.
   - The Decision 550 successor, at no less than about NIS 30bn.
   - Restoration of the cuts to local authorities in the first budget.
   - Published targets for placing Arab teachers and for residency slots.
2. **Kaminitz.** Enforcement against existing homes is frozen immediately. Within 6 months, the administrative enforcement against them is repealed, *naming Tayibe, Tira, Qalansawe, the Triangle and the Negev*. "No forced relocation" in the Negev.
3. **The Joint List as an equal member of the monitoring committee**, with the right to publish its own assessment.
4. **Clause 5 plus the anti-disqualification line** (Balad's demand), and **deletion of the public "won't count on them" line**. A government can stay silent about us; it cannot humiliate us in writing.
5. **Presidium:** clause 20 [A], with the government parties not blocking the Joint List's deputy Speaker. That post was promised to Ta'al in the list deal.
6. **Horizon sentence.** We accept the mediator's text with three words restored: "…will take no step that precludes a future settlement **between Israel and the Palestinian people**." In Arabic: «بين إسرائيل والشعب الفلسطيني».
   - Ta'al drops "renew a diplomatic process" as a condition. Hadash may keep it.
   - "The Palestinian people" is our floor. Bennett called it the line he cannot read, but it sits in a letter he does not sign.

**Crime post (Segalovitz as a coordinator with delegated powers): acceptable.**
- He is a professional rather than a politician, and that helps our society.
- Ra'am will claim the credit. Our answer is the monitoring committee seat and the letter's addressee: the Local Authority Heads, who are often our people.

**Investiture under S2+ with this letter:**
- Ta'al **abstains, present in the hall**, as a unified Joint List decision. Voters must see us end Netanyahu's rule.
- **Safety net:** no vote for no-confidence or dissolution while the letter is implemented.
- **Budgets:** abstain if Chapter D is funded and there are no new settlement lines. Otherwise we vote against.

**Without the letter, or without item 6:** Ta'al is absent and offers no standing safety net. We **never vote against** an Eisenkot investiture whose alternative is Netanyahu as caretaker.

Our public Joint List answer letter records our standing reservations: the occupation, settlements as illegal, no administrative detention, no compulsory service.
