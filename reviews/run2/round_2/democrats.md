# The Democrats (Golan): round 2 review of draft v5

Simulated negotiating team; this does not speak for the real party.

## Bottom line
- We accept the mediator's sequence: **P1 first, then P2 (S2+)**. Under S2+ all 8 Democrats MKs vote **FOR** investiture.
- Ranking: **S3 > S1 > S2+ > elections under a Netanyahu caretaker**. S4a puts us in opposition, and we would still not block it (bill 3).
- S3 is dead under the vetoes. We stop asking for it.

## P1: "Knesset first"
**We vote FOR all four bills.**
1. **Replace the Speaker:** yes.
2. **7 Oct state commission law:** yes. It is our pledge.
3. **Defendant law:** yes, even if it opens S4a without us. Ending Netanyahu's rule comes before our seats, and we would lead the opposition.
4. **Service for All (v4 6a):** yes. It is our platform: "שוויון מלא בשירות… וגיוס כלל האוכלוסייה" (full equality in service… and conscription of the whole population). Clause (c), a voluntary track for those not liable, stays as written.

**Condition: a clock.** If Likud has not replaced Netanyahu by the end of the first 28-day mandate, the change bloc moves to P2. Yashar should say so publicly.

## P2: S2+
Acceptable, with four conditions.

**(1) The letter must bind.**
- The open letter must be **adopted as a government decision at the first cabinet meeting**, not only signed by the PM-designate.
- Our wording: "These commitments are government policy. The government will report on them quarterly to the National Committee **and to the Knesset factions that respond to this letter**" («ולסיעות הכנסת שישיבו למכתב זה»).
- This names no faction, and it gives Ra'am and the Joint List a formal standing.
- Our view: this goes a long way toward Ra'am's "matching letter" and Ta'al's "safety net". It meets Hadash's "names the Joint List" demand only in substance, not formally. We support adding the horizon sentence as drafted.

**(2) The crime post sits under Golan.**
- Segalovitz as government coordinator with powers **delegated by the Minister of Public Security**: yes.
- This works only if **the Minister is Golan**. Yashar (round 1) has now said that is possible.
- **Our trade:** we drop 3[B] and accept 3[A], provided these words are added: "Until the committee reports (within 6 months), the government will not use the powers created by these laws. **Restoring** the earlier arrangements does not require the broad consent mentioned in clause 3."
- We already accept 9[A] and 15[A], which meets Liberman's stated price.
- **Deal-breaker:** Golan barred in writing from security portfolios.

**(3) Clause 13, a bridge to Bennett.** His [B] is our deal-breaker, and our [A] is his. We offer **[A′]**:
> "No new communities will be established. In existing communities, planning and construction will take place **only within the boundaries of plans approved before 27 Oct 2026**, by government decision."
> «לא יוקמו יישובים חדשים. ביישובים קיימים התכנון והבנייה ייעשו רק בתוך גבולות תוכניות שאושרו לפני 27.10.2026, בהחלטת ממשלה.»

- This allows new government decisions inside approved boundaries, and nothing outside them.
- We drop the E1 freeze from the text.
- Clause 14 stays as in run 1. **Bennett's "regularize outposts on state land" is a deal-breaker for us**: it legalizes the outposts we promised to dismantle. The only exception is what the government had decided before 27 Oct, as in run 1.

**(4) Clause 10 keeps "Jewish terror" (טרור יהודי) and our bridge line:** "בנחישות זהה לזו שבה נלחמת המדינה בכל טרור" (with the same determination with which the State fights all terror).

**Verdict:** SIGN WITH RESERVATION under S2+. The reservation from round 1 stays, with "judicial laws: repeal" replaced by "judicial laws: restoration, as per clause 3".
