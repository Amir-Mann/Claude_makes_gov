# Round 2: Yesh Atid (Lapid), 5 of Together's 12 MKs

Simulation only. Our sources are `research/yesh-atid.md` and our round-1 file.

## 1. P1, "Knesset first": we vote for all four bills, all 5 MKs

| Bill | Vote | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Replace the Speaker | **For** | — |
| 2. State commission of inquiry law | **For** | Our day-one pledge of 26 Apr 2026. |
| 3. Defendant law | **For** | We have pushed for it since 2023. It applies to the future and is general in form, which answers the "personal law" charge. |
| 4. Service for All (v4 6a) | **For** | It is our platform: "אין מכסות פטור". We want 6a(d) in our round-1 wording, the suspension of funding and benefits. |

**Voting alongside Ra'am and the Joint List with nothing signed:** acceptable to us, without qualification. 

**If bill 3 opens S4a, a unity government with Likud without Netanyahu:** we join on three conditions:
- Netanyahu holds no role, no rotation and no "pardon-and-stay";
- the commission law (bill 2) is already passed or is in the guidelines;
- the Service Law (bill 4) passes in the first year.

We will not let Likud trade the commission for unity. If it tries, we go to the opposition.

**Our addition to P1:** a fifth bill, held in reserve, to dissolve the Knesset with a fixed date. If neither P1 nor P2 produces a government by the end of the formation period, elections come on a date the Knesset sets, not one Netanyahu chooses as caretaker.

## 2. P2, "S2+": Yesh Atid says yes

- **Investiture:** all 5 MKs vote **for**.
- **The open letter to the Arab local authority heads:** acceptable. It is the 2021 Chapter D, which we built.
- **The horizon sentence:** acceptable. "Coordination with the PA… no step that precludes a future settlement" is fully consistent with Lapid's "two states for two peoples" (UN, 2022) and his "separation" line. We would not add "renew a diplomatic process" over Bennett's objection.
- **Segalovitz as crime coordinator, with delegated powers and outside the government:**
  - We support it. He is a former Yesh Atid MK and police major-general.
  - It is a professional appointment, not a "coalition with Ra'am", so Lapid's AP undertaking stands.

**The honest problem:**
- S2+ without Bennett's 7 and Lieberman's 7 has 34 Zionist seats plus Ra'am's 7, which is 41. That is dead.
- If Bennett stays out, a Yesh Atid split adds nothing: our 5 are already counted in the 48. That is why we will **not** split over S2+.
- We fight inside Together instead, publicly:

  «נתניהו כראש ממשלת מעבר עד 2027 ואולי יותר — זה המחיר האמיתי של "לא נסתמך". ועדת חקירה לא תקום מממשלת מעבר.»
  ("Netanyahu as caretaker PM until 2027, and maybe longer, is the real price of 'we won't rely on them'. No caretaker government will set up a commission of inquiry.")

- We will remind Bennett that in 2021 he chose a government with Ra'am over a fifth election.

**Forecast:** Bennett says no in November and might move in January. Lieberman, as in 2019, does not move.
