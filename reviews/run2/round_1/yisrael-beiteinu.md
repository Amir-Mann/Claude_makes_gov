# Round 1 (run 2): Yisrael Beiteinu review of draft v4

*Simulated negotiating team. Sources: `research/yisrael-beiteinu.md`.*

## 1. Structure: the honest answer

**Our ranking: S4a > S4b > opposition > S2 > S1 > S3.**
- S4a: unity with Likud without Netanyahu, about 65–74 seats.
- S4b: new elections.

**Is there any give in "no minority government, even transitionally"? No.**
- The wording was "לא נתמוך בשום ממשלת מיעוט, גם לא בממשלת מיעוט לתקופת מעבר" (15 Oct).
- On 19 Oct he added "לא ניתן להישען על ח"כים מרע"ם ומהרשימה המשותפת".
- That was 12 days before the vote, and 5 weeks after we voted to disqualify both lists.

**S2 is a minority government with better packaging.**
- The arithmetic gives it away. If the Arab lists are absent, the vote is 48 for and 53 against, and the government **fails**.
- Investiture passes only if Ra'am votes **yes** (55 against 53) and the Joint List stays away.
- So "a government that wins while the Arab lists are absent" does not exist. Ra'am's yes votes are load-bearing in every change structure.
- Liberman cannot tell his voters "we signed nothing" when his government lives on Mansour Abbas's hand.

**Our seats are decisive.** Without our 7, the Zionist change bloc is 41 and no change government exists at all. That gives us a veto. We use it here because this is the promise our voters re-elected us on.

**What Liberman actually prefers**
1. **Unity with Likud without Netanyahu.** This is the purpose of the election-night call: "האיש הזה חייב לרדת מהבמה… חייב להעיף אותו", and "הליכוד בלי נתניהו יכול להיות שותף משמעותי".
2. **New elections**, if Likud keeps Netanyahu. We would run on clean hands, with double envelopes still to count, against an Eisenkot who broke his "Zionist majority" line.

**The only narrow thing we would do with Arab votes in the room** is the Knesset agenda, not a government:
- replacing Speaker Ohana;
- a defendant law;
- a dissolution bill with a date.

Ra'am may vote for our bills. We do not depend on it, and we sign nothing with it.

**Investiture vote**
- S1, S2 and S3: YB does **not join**. If the government is presented anyway, YB votes **against**.
- S4a: **for**.

## 2. Clause by clause

These picks serve a Zionist-majority government (S4a). They are not consent to S1, S2 or S3.

- **2.** [S2 wording] "ממשלה ציונית, ממלכתית" is the minimum. We add "with a Zionist majority in the Knesset".
- **3.** Pick [A]. The PM term limit and a constitution process are our own pledges (Maariv, 27 Oct). Reject [B].
- **4.** Pick [A]. The government parties affirm it in the first person. We are open to balancing it with the Declaration of Independence.
- **5.** Must change to: "Arab citizens of Israel are equal citizens, with equal rights and equal duties" / «אזרחים שווים, בשוויון זכויות ובשוויון חובות».
  - Delete "their elected representatives are a legitimate part…". We do not sign a certificate for lists we voted to disqualify. The Supreme Court decides legitimacy; a coalition text does not.
  - **Deal-breaker:** the new no-wider-disqualification line. Our test ("whoever opposes eliminating Hamas has no place in the Knesset") stays.
- **6a.** Acceptable. It removes the Arab-only shield that broke "100% למשרתים - 0% למשתמטים". Two changes:
  - (c) add: "By the end of the term the government will bring legislation applying the national-civil service duty to every citizen at 18." / «עד תום הקדנציה תביא הממשלה חקיקה שתחיל חובת שירות לאומי-אזרחי על כל אזרח בגיל 18».
  - (d) replace with: "If the law is not enacted within a year, personal economic sanctions on evaders take effect by government decision." / «הסנקציות הכלכליות האישיות יופעלו בהחלטת ממשלה».
  - **Deal-breaker:** any quota, a "3%" figure, or any Haredi party in the coalition.
- **7.** Keep "all innocent civilians". Delete "Israelis and Palestinians alike, including in Gaza".
- **9.** Pick [A]. Must add: "Israel will act to eliminate Hamas's military and governing capabilities." / «ישראל תפעל לחיסול היכולות הצבאיות והשלטוניות של חמאס». We will not sign "end of the war" without that line.
- **10.** Pick [A] with «קומץ אלים». Administrative detention is our own position ("חד וחלק כן"). Never "טרור מתנחלים".
- **11.** 14 days is acceptable. Ours was "first decision".
- **12.** Pick [A], with "יהודה ושומרון" first in the Hebrew.
- **13.** No annexation this term is acceptable. Pick [B]: construction in existing communities continues by government decision. Reject [C] (an E1 freeze).
- **14.** Evacuating outposts on private land and violent hubs is fine, as rule of law. Must add regularization on state land by government decision.
- **15.** Pick [A] ("the Palestinians"). **Deal-breaker:** [B] "the Palestinian people" plus a diplomatic process. We reserve our Jordan/confederation plan for Areas A and B.
- **16.** Pick [A], a coordinator. Keep the Shin Bet against criminal organizations: this is our "טרור פלילי… יד ברזל" line. **Deal-breaker:** [B], a Ra'am deputy minister in policing.
- **17.** Accept, framed as employment and integration with service-track targets. Decision 550 was approved while Liberman was Finance Minister.
- **18.** Pick [A], but only with: "enforcement against new illegal construction continues in full."
- **19.** Pick [A].
- **20.** Pick [A]. YB keeps a free vote against Joint List or Balad nominees.
- **Chapter E:** reject.
- **Annex 1:** reject both [A] and [B]. Our Annex 2 line stays. We refuse Balad's demand to delete it.
