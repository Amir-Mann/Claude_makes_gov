# Round 1: Yesh Atid (Lapid), 5 of Together's 12 MKs

Simulation only. Our sources are `research/yesh-atid.md` (§§3, 4 and 7).

## 1. Structure

**Our ranking, honestly:**
1. **S2, policy and not a deal.** It is our election-night line: "להקים ממשלה ציונית… אולי לא תהיה קואליציה רחבה ביום הראשון, נרחיב אותה בהמשך". It is also the only version Bennett can survive, and if Bennett leaves we have no government.
2. **S1.** Lapid built 2021 with Ra'am and can defend a parliamentary agreement. It is a hard sell for Bennett.
3. **S4-unity:** Likud *without* Netanyahu. It is acceptable only on the conditions in §7. Our liberal base would see it as a defeat: no civil marriage, and Likud ministers from the 7 October government.
4. **S3, Ra'am as a coalition member.** Lapid told AP in May 2026 that he would not form another coalition with Ra'am. We would not block S3 if Bennett, Eisenkot and Liberman accepted it, but we will not lead it.
5. **S4-elections: the worst for us.** We fell from 24 seats to 5 inside someone else's list. New elections could finish the brand.

**Investiture vote under S2 or S1:** all 5 MKs vote FOR. We will not be the faction that is absent.

## 7. If Bennett walks to S4: do we split from Together?

**Arithmetic first.** Five Yesh Atid MKs leaving does not create a change government: 41 + 5 + 7 is 53, not 61. A split buys identity, not power.

- **S4-unity, with Netanyahu genuinely out (not PM, no rotation, no "pardon-and-stay"), the 7 October commission in the guidelines, and a 6a-style draft law:**
  - **We do not split.** We go in with Bennett and demand our 5/12 share.
  - Lapid said in 2022 that he would sit with Likud without Netanyahu. That is a search summary only (see dossier).
  - It is a bitter outcome, but staying out leaves us as 5 MKs in an opposition led by the Joint List and Golan.
- **S4-unity in which Netanyahu keeps any role, there is no state commission, or the Haredi exemptions remain:**
  - **We split.**
  - On paper 5 of 12 is more than a third, which is enough to split. Whether the merged-party structure allows it is legally unverified.
  - We would sit in opposition as Yesh Atid and lead it.
- **S4-elections:**
  - We do not re-run automatically with Bennett.
  - We first test a joint run with Yashar (Lapid offered Eisenkot the top slot in April).
  - If that fails, we stay with Bennett. A solo run at about 5 seats is too close to the threshold.
