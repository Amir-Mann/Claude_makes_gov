# Ra'am (United Arab List): round 1 response to draft v4

*Simulation. Based on research/raam.md. Does not speak for the real party.*

## 1. Structure

Our ranking is **S3 > S1 (amended) > S2 plus a letter > S4.**

**S3 is our campaign promise.** On 26 Oct we said: «دخول الائتلاف طريقنا لتحقيق المطالب والتأثير» ("entering the coalition is our path").

**S1 is our 2021 model, and we can defend it,** but only with:
- a seat in coalition management, and
- a real office (see clause 16).

**S2 as written: no.** It asks for 7 "yes" votes, and without them the government falls (48 against 53). In return we get nothing signed, while the Joint List, which only abstains, gets a bilateral letter under Annex 1[A]. That is backwards.

After 2021–22 we cannot accept a promise on paper alone. In Aug 2023 Smotrich said the government was not bound by Shaked's promise to Abbas, and the Decision 550 money was frozen. "Policy, not a deal" is exactly how that happens again.

The minimum for S2 is a **letter from the PM-designate to Ra'am**, mirroring Annex 1[A]. It must list:
- Chapter D with its numbers and dates;
- the crime post (clause 16);
- the al-Aqsa trigger.

**S4 is the worst outcome for us:**
- A unity government with Likud without Netanyahu excludes us.
- New elections would come after Likud calls our voters thieves, and 7 seats is our peak.

**How our MKs vote on investiture:**

| Structure | Vote |
|---|---|
| S3 or S1 | **For, all 7** |
| S2 with a letter | **For** |
| S2 with no letter | **Not for.** We stay absent in the first round of talks and negotiate. We do not vote *against* a change government. |

## 3. Questions put to Ra'am

**Q1. Yes on investiture under S2, with nothing signed?**
No, not as written. A letter turns S2 into "S1-lite", and with a letter we vote yes.

**Q2. A coordinator recommended by the Arab Local Authority Heads (16[A]) instead of our own office?**
No. It would be:
- outside the government;
- without authority over the police;
- with credit flowing to whoever runs the Local Authority Heads' committee, often our Joint List rival.

We put our 7 seats into this precisely to own the crime file.

**Our bridge:** **Yoav Segalovitz** (No. 2, a Jewish Zionist former police major-general) becomes **Deputy Minister for the fight against crime in Arab society**, in the ministry responsible for policing.
- Segalovitz personally accepts Yashar's four rules, so the office-holder meets Eisenkot's test.
- Ra'am keeps its free vote.
- The Democrats, if they get Public Security, are the natural host ministry.

If Yisrael Beiteinu vetoes a deputy minister, the fallback is: Segalovitz as **government coordinator with deputy-minister powers**, a budget, and a seat on the ministerial committee. A coordinator named by the Local Authority Heads is not acceptable.

**Q3. Would Abbas publicly accept the four rules, for S3?**
He would accept three of them, in his own words:
1. "Israel is defined in its Basic Laws as a Jewish and democratic state; we act within the law."
2. "The Declaration of Independence and its promise of equality."
3. "Service for all, civilian and voluntary for Arab youth" (May 2026: «خدمة للجميع»).

On the fourth rule:
- He will say: "Hamas must not rule Gaza; all armed factions must lay down their weapons."
- He will **not** say "destroy Hamas". He gave his reason on Kan: «פיקוח נפש».

If Eisenkot insists on the literal wording, we drop to S1 with Segalovitz in the post.

**Under S2 without a letter: REJECT.**
