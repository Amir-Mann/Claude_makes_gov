*Simulation. This does not speak for any real party. The basis is `research/bennett.md`.*

## 1. Structure: the hard question answered

**Can Bennett defend S2? No.**
- The pledge was never "I won't *sign* with Arab parties". It was: "מאז 7 באוקטובר אין מנדט לממשלה **שנתמכת** במפלגות ערביות" (Since 7 October there is no mandate for a government *supported* by Arab parties; 2 Feb 2026). Ynet's reporters added on 21 Oct that he would not lend a hand to a government built on Arab *abstention*.
- S2 is that government without the paperwork. It has 48 seats. It wins investiture only because Ra'am votes yes and Balad stays out.
- "We signed nothing" would make it worse. It is the 2021 broken promise again, now with deniability as the excuse. Likud is already calling the election "stolen" by the Arab parties. S2 would prove the charge in the voters' eyes. "Bennett broke his word twice" ends his career on the right.
- S2 is also dead on arithmetic, independently of us. Yisrael Beiteinu refuses it, and 48 − 7 = 41. **We will not be the first to break while Liberman holds firm.**

**Ranking:**
1. S4a: a unity government with Likud without Netanyahu.
2. S4b: new elections.
3. Opposition.
4. S2.
5. S1.
6. S3.

Our honest cost: if no government forms, Netanyahu stays on as caretaker PM until new elections. That is the "7 October government" we promised to send home. We are choosing that cost knowingly over a government we pledged not to form. It is not a free option.

**One narrow opening, not S2.** A change government that reaches **61 without counting Arab votes** is acceptable. That means 13 Likud MKs defecting or abstaining by agreement. In that case Ra'am may vote for it on its own initiative, as a surplus. This is the same line as Yisrael Beiteinu's.

**Is there a realistic path to S4 while Likud keeps Netanyahu? Only a narrow one, and only over time. It runs through the mandate clock:**
1. **Do not recommend Netanyahu, and do not recommend an Eisenkot S2 either.** Recommend "a Zionist unity government without Netanyahu". Wait for the official results on 4 Nov. The double envelopes (soldiers' votes) usually trim the Arab lists.
2. **Let Netanyahu's mandate fail.** He has 53 and needs 8 more. His camp is courting Bennett and Liberman (Ynet live, 28 Oct). We refuse publicly and in advance, so his mandate visibly expires on his own lack of seats.
3. **Make the offer to Likud, not to Netanyahu.** The offer:
   - parity and rotation inside a 74-seat Zionist government;
   - Likud holding Finance and Defense;
   - the Service Law;
   - the 7 Oct state commission.
   Bennett's own line, "he should go home. He should be pardoned" (4 Sep), is the bridge. A Herzog pardon tied to Netanyahu retiring is the only lever that moves Likud's MKs. That path depends on the President, not on us.
4. **The 21-day Knesset stage** (any MK with 61 signatures can be tasked), followed by the first Likud budget crisis or a leadership challenge. If Likud still holds by then, the default is new elections.

Our estimate: S4a happens within this round's timeframe at under 25%. New elections are the most likely outcome. We say that openly.

**Investiture vote:**
- Under S4a: all 12 vote **for**.
- Under S2: Bennett's 7 **do not join**, and vote **against** an investiture that rests on Balad's absence. Yesh Atid's 5 may split off and vote for. That risk is real, and we accept it.
