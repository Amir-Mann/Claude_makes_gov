# Yashar: round 1 review of draft v4

*Simulated negotiating team. This does not speak for the real party.*

## Structure

We start from the arithmetic. Every structure in v4 needs Lieberman's 7 and Bennett's 12 to vote yes.
- Both refuse any government that rests on Arab votes, including abstention.
- Without them we have 41 + Ra'am 7 = 48, against 53. That is not a government. It is a press release.

So we are not going to sign S1 or S3 with the Arab parties while our two partners walk out of the room.

**Ranking:**

1. **S4a, reached through a "Knesset-first" track.**
   - From 10 Nov, the anti-Netanyahu majority that already exists in the Knesset passes four bills with ad hoc votes:
     - replace the Speaker;
     - a state commission of inquiry law;
     - the Service Law;
     - a defendant law, barring an indicted MK from being tasked with forming a government.
   - Each faction votes for these bills on its own. Nothing is signed with anyone.
   - Lieberman has already said he accepts "ad hoc, unsigned Ra'am votes for our bills" (`research/yisrael-beiteinu.md` §7). Bennett accepts Ra'am votes "on specific civic bills".
   - The defendant law opens the way to a **broad Zionist government with Likud without Netanyahu**, with 66–74 seats. In Yashar's 28.10 deliberations this is option 3: "ממשלה רחבה עם הליכוד אבל בלי נתניהו".
2. **S2.** It is acceptable to us, but only if Bennett and Lieberman come round after S4a fails. We will not be the first to say it out loud.
3. **S1, cut down.** A parliamentary agreement with no Ra'am deputy minister. Our 26.9 pledge covered *membership*, not outside support.
4. **New elections.** Eisenkot called them "a mistake", and we lost ground in the count. They are still better than S3.
5. **S3.** Ra'am cannot sit in the government unless it accepts all four rules. Ra'am's own dossier lists "destroy Hamas" as its deal-breaker, so S3 is dead.

**Investiture votes:** all 21 Yashar MKs vote **for** under S4a or S2. Under S3 without the four rules, Yashar does not join.

**Herzog consultations:** Yashar recommends Eisenkot.
- We accept any faction's recommendation. A recommendation is not a coalition: Lapid in 2021, Netanyahu in 2019.
- We use the mandate to put the S4a question to the Likud: "Netanyahu or the government".

**Annex 1 bridge.** In place of letters to the Joint List, the Prime Minister-designate publishes the government's guidelines (Chapter D and clauses 5, 13 and 14) in a public letter to the **National Committee of Arab Local Authority Heads and to all Arab citizens**.
- It is a commitment to citizens, not to factions.
- Each faction then decides on its own how to vote.
- This gives Hadash a written commitment, as in 1992, without a signed exchange with Balad.

**Deal-breakers:**
- a Ra'am deputy minister without the four rules;
- any signed text with Balad;
- "occupation" or a Palestinian state;
- an Arab-only service exemption written as a protection;
- Netanyahu, or rotation with him.

## Gains vs concessions (our worldview)

**Gains:**
- Netanyahu out;
- the commission in 14 days;
- Service for All without quotas;
- "Zionist, statist";
- Hamas named and dismantled;
- powers returned from Smotrich to the IDF;
- administrative detention for Jewish terror;
- no Palestinian state.

**Concessions:**
- Chapter D as policy, which is mostly our own platform anyway;
- no annexation, which is our own position;
- tolerating other factions' votes.

**Verdict: SIGN WITH RESERVATION** on the v4 text as the basic guidelines for **either** S2 **or** a broad government.

The reservation: "ישר! אינה חותמת על הסכם, מכתב או נספח עם סיעה שלא קיבלה את ארבעת העקרונות; הצבעתן של סיעות אחרות היא החלטתן בלבד." ("Yashar signs no agreement, letter or annex with a faction that has not accepted the four principles. How other factions vote is their own decision.")
