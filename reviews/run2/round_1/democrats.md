# The Democrats (Golan): round 1 review of draft v4

Simulated negotiating team; this does not speak for the real party. Facts are from `research/democrats.md`.

## Structure

**Ranking: S3 > S1 > S2 >> S4.**
- **S3 (Ra'am in the coalition)** is what Golan said in public: [Q] "ניתן לייצר שותפות עם מנסור עבאס ולהכניס אותו לקואליציה" (Walla, 6 Oct). It also makes the 28 Oct line "בשותפות עם אזרחי ישראל הערבים" (in partnership with Israel's Arab citizens) true.
- **S1** (the 2021 model) is acceptable.
- **S2** is the most fragile option, and the most humiliating one for the partners whose votes it needs. As drafted it builds Netanyahu's "Jewish votes vs. Arab votes" division into clause 2. We accept S2 only if "Zionist" describes the government parties and is never used to rank votes (clause 2 wording below).
- **S4 is worse for us than any of the three.** A unity government with Likud means opposition for the Democrats: we ruled out sitting with any member of the outgoing government. New elections risk our 8 seats, since Eisenkot's "big party" campaign is draining us.
- **Our leverage:** the Zionist change bloc is 48 with us and 40 without us. No change government exists without the Democrats.

**Investiture vote:** all 8 Democrats MKs vote **FOR** under S3, S1 or S2, provided the must-haves below are met.

## Gains vs concessions (our worldview)

**Gains**
- Netanyahu out; no Haredi exemption.
- A state commission within 14 days.
- "Jewish terror" named, with tools: administrative detention and the Judea and Samaria police district.
- Outposts evacuated, no annexation, powers returned to the IDF.
- Chapter D: NIS 1bn a year for crime, about NIS 30bn for the Decision 550 successor, a Kaminitz freeze.
- Arab representatives recognized as legitimate.
- The crime ministry for Golan.

**Concessions**
- No "occupation" and no two states.
- Settlement construction continues under approved plans.
- The judiciary goes through a committee rather than immediate repeal.
- Ra'am may stay outside the cabinet.

**Verdict: SIGN WITH RESERVATION**, conditional on 3, 10, 13 and the portfolio being met. Reservation for Annex 2:
> "The Democrats see Jewish-Arab partnership as a foundation of Israel's future and will work during the term for the full inclusion of Arab parties in government. They read clause 13 as: no new communities and no expansion beyond approved plans. Their position on the judicial laws of the 25th Knesset is repeal."

## TV statement

Preference **T1 > T2 > T3**. We accept **T2**, provided the government leaders' joint text includes:
- "טרור יהודי" (Jewish terror);
- "a government for all citizens, Jews and Arabs alike, equal citizens";
- no line saying "we do not rely on Arab votes".

**Golan must say** in his own remarks: "הקול של אזרח שמצביע בטירה שווה בדיוק כמו הקול של אזרח שמצביע בגבעת שמואל" (the vote of a citizen in Tira is worth exactly as much as one in Givat Shmuel), and "נילחם בטרור הפלסטיני ובטרור היהודי ביד ברזל" (we will fight Palestinian terror and Jewish terror with an iron fist).

**He will not say:** "a Zionist majority" as a *condition* of legitimacy, or any line implying that Arab votes count less.

## Trades

- **With Bennett:** we drop the E1 freeze ([C]) and accept 13 [A] and 12 [A]. In return, his veto on Golan holding Public Security is lifted and clause 10 keeps the equivalence bridge.
- **With Liberman:** we fully back the v4 6a draft and economic sanctions on draft evaders. In return, his public boycott line is removed from the signed text.
- **With Ra'am:** we back S3 and coalition membership, and a deputy minister with budget authority in our ministry. In return, Ra'am backs Golan for Public Security.
- **With Hadash and Ta'al:** we back bilateral letters, equal monitoring seats and the PA dialogue line in clause 15. In return, they abstain on investiture and on any budget that funds Chapter D.
