# Ta'al (Ahmad Tibi): run 2, round 1 review of draft v4

*Simulation exercise. This text speaks for no real party.*

Our compass is Tibi's own 2026 line:
- «Anyone who wants a safety net from us must first guarantee a safety net for you, for our society» (JPost, 22 Oct).
- «We will not allow Netanyahu to be caretaker prime minister.»

Tibi has also said "القائمة العربية المشتركة هي التي ستحسم" ("the Joint List will be decisive"). We know that. So do they.

## Structure

| Rank | Option | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **S1, with Annex 1 [A]** (bilateral letters, 1992 model) | This is our 30-year doctrine: a blocking bloc and a safety net in exchange for written commitments, with no collective responsibility. |
| 2 | **S3** (Ra'am in the coalition) | More stable, and our role is the same. The cost is that Ra'am owns the deliverables and the credit. Acceptable only with Annex 1 [A] and equal monitoring seats. |
| 3 | **S2** | A "Zionist government" that counts on our abstention but signs nothing to us. This is a blank check in reverse. We do not sign blank checks. |
| 4 | **S4** | Likud without Netanyahu is still a right-wing government, and we become irrelevant. New elections leave Netanyahu as caretaker, which breaks our pledge. This is the worst for us. |

**Investiture vote under S1 with Annex 1 [A]:**
- Ta'al's MKs **abstain**, as a unified Joint List decision.
- We vote **for** replacing Ohana and **against** every no-confidence and dissolution motion.

**Under S2 with no letter:**
- We are **absent** on investiture and give **no** safety net on budgets or no-confidence motions.
- The government then lives vote by vote. That is not a threat. It is what S2 means.

**Clause 6a, replacing (b) and (c):**
"(b) No right, allowance, or benefit in land, housing, employment, or higher education will be conditioned on service.
(c) Nothing in this law imposes any service obligation on citizens not liable under the Security Service Law, or creates targets for them."
Arabic: «لا يفرض هذا القانون أي واجب خدمة على المواطنين غير الملزمين بموجب قانون خدمة الأمن، ولا يضع لهم أهدافاً».

**Clause 15, wording:**
"The government will work to renew a political process with the Palestinian leadership (PLO/PA), and will take no step that precludes a future settlement between Israel and the Palestinian people."
Arabic: «تعمل الحكومة على تجديد مسار سياسي مع القيادة الفلسطينية».
Without a "political horizon" Tibi breaks a campaign promise.

**Clause 18, wording:**
"Within 6 months the administrative enforcement chapter of the Kaminitz Law will be **repealed** as regards existing inhabited homes, including in Tayibe, Tira, Qalansawe and the Triangle, and the Negev; enforcement is frozen immediately."

**Clause 20, wording:**
"The government parties will vote for the presidium and committee nominees of each faction according to established practice, including a deputy Speaker from the Joint List and a Joint List member of the Finance Committee."
The deputy Speaker post was promised to Ta'al in the list deal. A "free vote" that kills it is a no.

**New items we want in the letter:**
- A law that a person under indictment will not be tasked with forming a government.
- A freeze on renewing the Citizenship (Family Unification) Law's restrictions; failing that, Ta'al keeps its free vote on it.
- Restoration of the cuts to Arab local authorities in the first budget.
