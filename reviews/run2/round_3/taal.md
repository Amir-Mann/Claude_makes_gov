## 4. TV statement (Joint List text, Hadash §4)

Tibi stands with Jabareen and adds a few lines in his own voice.

1. **Keep "بدولتين على حدود 1967" ("two states on the 1967 borders").** If Balad refuses, use «دولة فلسطينية مستقلة عاصمتها القدس الشرقية» ("an independent Palestinian state with East Jerusalem as its capital"), *not* the self-determination fallback.
   - Reason: two states are Ta'al's founding position. A text that drops them would let Ra'am call us vaguer than Abbas.
2. **Add, after "يوم تُخرق…":** «وسنحاسب الحكومة كل ثلاثة أشهر أمام شعبنا» ("and we will hold the government to account before our people every three months").
   - Reason: it ties the speech to the quarterly reports in Part B, so the safety net is visibly conditional.
3. **Tibi's own line:** «حقبة نتنياهو انتهت – وأنتم أنهيتموها» ("The Netanyahu era is over, and you ended it").
   - Reason: credit goes to the voters, not to the deal.

## 5. Honest estimate

No. Bennett and Lieberman chose new elections in round 2. Without their 14 seats the change bloc has 41 and v6 cannot pass. So the most likely result is elections around March 2027, with Netanyahu as caretaker.

Ta'al will campaign on one line: "we offered the safety net; they preferred Netanyahu."
