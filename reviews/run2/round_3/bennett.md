## 2. Brackets

**True deal-breakers:**
- **Clause 7, second bracket.** I reject it. Counter-wording: "החלטות הממשלה על דו"ח הוועדה יתקבלו בהסכמה רחבה" (the government's decisions on the committee's report will be made by broad agreement). Freezing powers by fiat, or repealing them by a narrow majority, is what we accused the outgoing government of doing.
- **Part B 6, "equal members".** I reject it. Counter-wording: "נציגי סיעות שהגיבו יקבלו את הדוחות" (representatives of factions that responded will receive the reports). Equal membership turns the letter back into a deal with the factions.
- **Part B 7, "no step that precludes a future settlement".** Delete it. Keep coordination with the Palestinian Authority. The bracketed words "between Israel and the Palestinians" are acceptable.
- **Clause 14** (not bracketed, but still open for me). It needs: "מאחזים על קרקע מדינה יוסדרו לפי חוק" (outposts on state land will be regularized according to law).

**Posturing (I would let these go):**
- **Clause 4, the disqualification line.** I prefer to delete it, but could accept "initiate" only.
- **Clause 13c.** Accept, provided "approved plans" includes plans already approved, among them E1.
- **The Yisrael Beiteinu line.** Indifferent.
- **The copy to the Follow-Up Committee.** I prefer to delete it.
- **Segalovitz.** Accept as written.
