# Run 2 negotiation rounds: brief for each movement

You are your movement's negotiating team. You are no longer told to seek a middle ground.
Argue the way your real negotiators would, with your rivals and voters watching. "No deal" is a legitimate answer.

## Each round
1. Read:
   - the current draft (`drafts/statement_vN.md`);
   - the other movements' dossiers in `research/`, at least sections 7;
   - in later rounds, the other movements' previous-round files in `reviews/run2/round_{N-1}/`.
2. Write `reviews/run2/round_N/<slug>.md`, at most about 900 words:
   - **Structure:** rank S1–S4 for your movement, honestly, including whether you'd prefer S4 (a unity government with Likud without Netanyahu, or new elections). Say how your MKs would actually vote on investiture under your preferred acceptable structure: for, against, abstain or absent.
   - **Clause by clause:** for each bracket, give your pick, your must-change items with exact replacement wording (in English, plus Hebrew or Arabic where wording is sensitive), and your deal-breakers.
   - **Gains vs concessions:** a short ledger by your movement's own worldview, ending with a verdict: SIGN / SIGN WITH RESERVATION (exact wording) / ABSTAIN-TOLERATE / REJECT.
   - **TV statement:** T1, T2 or T3; the lines you must say; the lines you will not say.
   - **Attacks:** what your rivals will say the next morning, and your answer.
   - **Trades:** what you'd trade, and with whom.
3. Reply to the mediator with a summary of at most 250 words.

Don't move just because a text looks reasonable. Move only if your movement would. If a real party would walk away, walk away and say why.
No new web research is needed unless something decisive is missing (search quota is limited).
