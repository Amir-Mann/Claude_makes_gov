# Round 1 review: Yesh Atid (within Together), on statement_v1

## 1. Verdict: **ACCEPT WITH CHANGES**

This text is very close to Yair Lapid's own vocabulary: Jewish and democratic per the Declaration of Independence, "טרור יהודי", a state commission, and the 2021 Arab-citizen package. It is missing the issue Yesh Atid was founded on, **equal burden (שוויון בנטל)**, and it is weak on Hamas and on the courts.

## 2. Gains vs concessions ledger (Yesh Atid's view)

| Gains | Concessions |
|---|---|
| The "October 7 government" is replaced. Netanyahu, Ben Gvir and Smotrich are out. | The government relies on Arab votes. The list leader pledged "לא נסתמך עליהן" ("we will not rely on them"), and Lapid told the AP (May 2026) "no coalition with Ra'am". Structurally this is outside support with **no Arab ministers**, so it is defensible. (dossier §3) |

**Net:** clearly positive, **but only if equal burden appears**. Without it, a Yesh Atid voter would see a statement that delivers for every partner except on our flagship promise.

## 3. Must-change items

**M1. Add a new clause after clause 6: equal burden.** The draft has no Haredi draft or service commitment, which is Yesh Atid's core cause since 2013 (dossier §1b, §2).
> "6a. The government will legislate, within its first year, a service law based on **equal burden** (HE: שוויון בנטל; AR: تقاسم العبء بالتساوي): an end to blanket exemptions from service, with no exemption quotas, and state benefits linked to military or civilian service. Civilian service in Arab society will develop as set out in clause 21. Parties to the parliamentary agreement retain freedom of vote on this law (clause 23)."

**M2. Clause 8: name Hamas as a terrorist organization.** As written, clause 8 condemns the act but not the organisation.
> "…the massacre carried out by **Hamas, a terrorist organization** (HE: ארגון הטרור חמאס; AR: حماس، منظمة إرهابية بموجب القانون الإسرائيلي)…"
The Arabic can follow the analysis's own bridge, "designated a terrorist organization under Israeli law". This is descriptive, the same technique as clause 4.

**M3. Clause 3: protect the courts and the constitutional process.** As written, the last sentence of clause 3 could block both. Add:
> "This does not prevent the government from protecting the independence of the judiciary, the Attorney General and the gatekeepers. Any change to the judicial system, and any constitutional process based on the Declaration of Independence, will be made only **with broad consent** (HE: בהסכמה רחבה; AR: بتوافق واسع)."

**Verdict flips to ACCEPT** if M1 is adopted and either M2 or M3 is adopted. **M1 alone is a deal-breaker if refused.**

## 4. Nice-to-haves
1. Clause 11: change "within 14 days" to "**at the government's first meeting**". This matches our promise of a commission "on the first day" (dossier §2).
2. Clause 17: add published targets for **employment, Hebrew-language education and higher education** to the Resolution 550 successor. This reflects our 2012 founding goal of Arab employment.
3. Clause 6: add "and will advance anchoring **civil equality** (שוויון אזרחי) in a Basic Law". This is our 2018 and 2023 position. We will drop it if Bennett objects.

## 5. Trade space
- **We would give up:**
  - The Nation-State Law equality amendment (nice-to-have 3).
  - Any two-state wording.
  - The 14-day versus first-day timing.
  - Coalition discipline for the Arab parties on the service law.
- **We would accept:**
  - The Kaminitz freeze and Negev recognition, which are our own 2021 precedent.
  - Clause 25's breach mechanism.
  - Hadash's need for wording on Palestinian Authority coordination in clause 15, as long as it contains no "כיבוש" ("occupation") and no statehood commitment.

## 6. Promise check
- **Kept:**
  - Netanyahu replaced.
  - A Zionist-led government (no Arab ministers, which keeps the AP undertaking in letter).
  - The Oct 7 commission.
  - "Jewish and democratic" per the Declaration.
  - Jewish terror confronted.
  - Rule of law in the territories.
  - The 2021 Arab-citizen legacy renewed.
- **Strained:** the list's pledge not to "rely" on Arab parties. Lapid personally never repeated that line, and he said on election night "נרחיב אותה בהמשך" ("we will expand it later") (dossier §3).
- **Broken unless M1 is adopted:** "שוויון בנטל וגיוס לכולם" ("equal burden and conscription for all").

**To our voters:** We replaced the October 7 government with a Zionist government with no Arab ministers, and its outside partners signed onto a Jewish and democratic state and a condemnation of Hamas. We will deliver a state commission, an equal-burden law and a real fight against Jewish terror, which is what we promised.
> "החלפנו את ממשלת 7 באוקטובר בממשלה ציונית בלי שרים מהמפלגות הערביות — והן חתמו על מדינה יהודית ודמוקרטית ועל גינוי חמאס. ועדת חקירה ממלכתית, חוק שוויון בנטל ומאבק אמיתי בטרור היהודי — מה שהבטחנו, זה מה שנביא."
