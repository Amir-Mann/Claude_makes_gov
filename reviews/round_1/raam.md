# Ra'am review of draft v1 (round 1)

## 1. Verdict

**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.**

The civil package is better than what we had in 2021, and it is written down and monitored. But the draft treats Ra'am as an outside supporter, lumped together with Hadash and Ta'al. It leaves our Negev base exposed to demolitions. And it says nothing about al-Aqsa, the issue that froze our coalition membership in April 2022.

## 2. Gains vs concessions (Ra'am's worldview)

**Gains**

- **Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir and Smotrich are out.** This was our first campaign aim (dossier §2).
- **Crime plan (cl. 16):** NIS 1bn a year, protected from cuts, with a lead minister. This is exactly "توفير الأمان للأبناء والبيوت" ("security for our children and homes").
- **Successor to Decision 550 (cl. 17):** at least about NIS 30bn in real terms, and Smotrich's cuts to local authorities restored.
- **Kaminitz (cl. 18):** enforcement frozen and an amendment within 6 months. That is better than 2021's "120 days, then forgotten".
- **Negev (cl. 19):** recognition decisions for a first group of villages within 18 months, with electricity and water.
- **Legitimacy (cl. 5–6):** we are "a legitimate part of Israeli democracy" and the government acts "for them and with them". We have demanded this since 2021.
- **Representation (cl. 20):** civil-service targets, a deputy Speaker and a committee chair.
- **Voluntary civic service (cl. 21):** no security link. This is Abbas's own model from July–August 2026.
- **West Bank (cl. 10, 13, 14):** Jewish terror is fought, no annexation, outposts evacuated. This delivers our 19 Oct pledge to stop settlement expansion and settler attacks, without our having to spend capital on it.
- **Freedom and leverage (cl. 23, 25):** a free vote on defence, settlements and identity, and an exit clause if Chapter D is breached.

**Concessions**

- **"Jewish and democratic" (cl. 4):** this is a description of the present, not an endorsement. Abbas already said in 2021: "מדינת ישראל נולדה כמדינה יהודית, וככה היא תישאר" ("Israel was born as a Jewish state, and so it will remain"). Clause 4 adds the Declaration of Independence's equality promise. This costs us little.
- **Naming Hamas for the 7 Oct massacre (cl. 8):** consistent with "we condemned the crimes of October 7" (Kan, 28 Jul 2026). It contains no personal "destroy Hamas" pledge, so it is acceptable. Clause 9 is government policy, and clause 23 keeps our free vote.
- **No cabinet role:** this is the real cost. Our aim was to be "الكتلة المكملة… شركاء في الائتلاف" ("the complementary bloc… partners in the coalition"), returning "with the violence and crime portfolio". Cl. 2 and cl. 23 reduce us to a 1992-style outside supporter. That is less than 2021.
- **Silence on "the Palestinian people", the Palestinian state and occupation:** tolerable for one term, because cl. 3 reserves our positions.

**Net:** positive on substance. Politically it is a downgrade from 2021 unless we get a role and Negev protection.

## 3. Must-change items

**1. Cl. 2 and 23: a role for Ra'am in fighting crime.**

Replace the last sentence of cl. 23 for Ra'am with:

> "Ra'am will be a member of the coalition (HE: סיעה חברה בקואליציה; AR: كتلة شريكة في الائتلاف) without a cabinet minister. Its representative will serve as **Deputy Minister responsible for the fight against crime and violence in Arab society**, and will lead the plan in clause 16."

Our candidate is Yoav Segalovitz, a Jewish former police major-general. Bennett and Lieberman can still say "no Arab ministers in the cabinet." Hadash and Ta'al can keep the parliamentary-agreement model.

**2. Cl. 18 and 19: protection for the Negev villages during recognition.**

Add to cl. 19:

> "During the recognition process there will be no demolitions of residential homes and no forced relocation (HE: ללא הריסות בתי מגורים וללא העתקה כפויה; AR: دون هدم بيوت سكنية ودون تهجير قسري) in the unrecognized villages, and plans for new Jewish localities in the Negev will be reviewed in coordination with the Bedouin local authorities."

As drafted, the freeze in cl. 18 covers only localities with a plan in preparation. That excludes exactly the unrecognized villages.

**3. New cl. 15A: al-Aqsa.**

> "The government will preserve the historic status quo at the holy places in Jerusalem, including **al-Masjid al-Aqsa / al-Haram al-Sharif** (AR: المسجد الأقصى / الحرم القدسي الشريف; HE: הר הבית / אל-אקצא)."

This is the existing official policy of every Israeli government, so it costs Bennett and Lieberman nothing. For us it is the minimum after April 2022.

**If items 2 and 3 are fixed, and item 1 or an equivalent role is granted, Ra'am signs: ACCEPT.**

## 4. Nice-to-have

- **Cl. 15:** add "towards peace and reconciliation between the Jewish people and the Palestinian people" (AR: بين الشعب اليهودي والشعب الفلسطيني).
- **Cl. 17:** add a guaranteed transfer schedule each fiscal year, with no mid-year diversions. In 2023 about NIS 497m was diverted.
- **Cl. 19:** extend the 2022 Electricity Law to all homes in localities with a plan in preparation.

## 5. Trade space

- We accept the bridge wording **"Hamas, designated a terrorist organization under Israeli law"** in cl. 8–9. It describes existing law, not a loyalty test. We will not accept a personal pledge to "destroy Hamas" signed by Ra'am.
- We drop any reference to the Nation-State Law or to a Palestinian state for this term.
- We accept a Haredi conscription law for Yisrael Beiteinu, with a free vote for Ra'am.
- If item 1 is refused, we take as a fallback: a Ra'am-nominated head of the government's anti-crime directorate, plus the Knesset Interior Committee chair. Clause 20 is then filled with Ra'am names.

## 6. Promise check

**Kept** (dossier §2, §3, §6):
- Replace the government without Netanyahu.
- Crime plan, budgets, Kaminitz, Negev recognition.
- No endorsement of the Nation-State Law and no loyalty test.
- Voluntary service only.

**At risk:**
- "Entering the coalition is our path": broken without item 1.
- Protection of Negev homes: broken without item 2.
- Al-Aqsa sensitivity: at risk without item 3.
