# Review of draft v1, as Hadash (חד"ש / الجبهة)

## 1. Verdict: **ACCEPT WITH CHANGES**

## 2. Gains vs concessions ledger (Hadash's view)

**Gains**
- **Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir and Smotrich are out.** This was our first promise ("החלפת ממשלת נתניהו – הצעד הראשון ההכרחי", Ynet, about 25.10).
- **Demands 1 and 2 are met in numbers:** a crime plan of NIS 1bn a year (cl. 16), a Decision 550 successor of about NIS 30bn (cl. 17), the Kaminitz Law frozen, master plans (cl. 18) and recognition of Negev villages (cl. 19).
- **Annexation is ruled out.** "טרור יהודי / الإرهاب اليهودي" is named and fought, outposts are evacuated with no retroactive legalization, and powers return from Smotrich to the IDF (cl. 10, 13, 14).
- **Gaza:** humanitarian aid and reconstruction (cl. 9).
- **Equality and legitimacy:** cl. 5 and 6, plus the Declaration's "complete equality" (cl. 4).
- **The 1992 model:** we stay outside, take no ministers, and keep full freedom of vote (cl. 23). Breach of Chapter D releases us (cl. 25).

**Concessions**
- We sign a text containing "מדינה יהודית ודמוקרטית". It is descriptive ("defined in its Basic Laws"), shielded by cl. 3, and the Nation-State Law is not endorsed. This is tolerable.
- We name "the massacre carried out by Hamas … acts of terror". Hadash condemned harm to civilians from day one (dossier §1b, 4b). This is tolerable **only** if the condemnation is visibly universal, including Gaza.
- We vote **yes** on investiture.

**Missing**
- **Demand 4, our most important** (Jabareen, 103FM, 13.10): a political horizon and negotiations with the Palestinian leadership. The text has no "העם הפלסטיני / الشعب الفلسطيني" and no recognized legal status for the territories.
- **Demand 3:** youth housing, education and employment.

**Net:** the civil gains are large and measurable. The national-political column is nearly empty, and on Gaza and Hamas we give more than we receive. As it stands, this is **marginally positive**. With the changes below it is **clearly positive**.

## 3. Must-change items

**M1, cl. 15 (political horizon).** Replace with:
> "The government will renew contact and civil and security coordination with the Palestinian leadership (the PLO/Palestinian Authority) (HE: ההנהגה הפלסטינית; AR: القيادة الفلسطينية) and will keep open a political horizon (HE: אופק מדיני; AR: أفق سياسي) for a negotiated agreement that ensures security, dignity and freedom for both peoples, the Jewish people and the Palestinian people (HE: העם הפלסטיני; AR: الشعب الفلسطيني). It will take no step that closes off such an agreement."

No "state" and no "1967". Clause 3 protects Bennett. Without the words "Palestinian people" and "political horizon", we cannot claim demand 4 at all.

**M2, cl. 7 and 9 (symmetry on Gaza).**
- Cl. 7: append "…including in Israel, in Gaza and in the West Bank (AR: في إسرائيل وغزة والضفة الغربية)."
- Cl. 9: open with "The government will act for a lasting end to the war in Gaza and the return of all hostages and the bodies of the fallen; …"

Keep "Hamas does not rule Gaza". We can live with that if the war ends.

**M3, cl. 12 (legal status).** Replace "military control" with the Israeli Supreme Court's own term:
> "…territories held since 1967 under belligerent occupation (HE: תפיסה לוחמתית; AR: احتلال حربي), as defined in the rulings of Israel's Supreme Court."

In the Arabic text, the order of the names should be "الضفة الغربية (يهودا والسامرة في القانون الإسرائيلي)".

**Flip:** if M1 and M2 are adopted, our verdict becomes **ACCEPT**. M3 is strongly wanted. If it is rejected, we can accept "military control" provided M1 contains "the Palestinian people".

## 4. Nice-to-have
1. **Cl. 21:** replace "expand voluntary civil service" with "a dedicated plan for Arab youth in housing, higher education and employment (our demand 3)". Any civil service should be run solely by Arab local authorities.
2. **Cl. 18:** freeze **all** residential demolitions in Arab localities and unrecognized villages pending plans, not only where a plan is "in preparation".
3. **Cl. 6:** add "and will restore the status of the Arabic language in public services". Separately, the government should not enforce the Nation-State Law against equality, without the law being named.

## 5. Trade space
- We accept "Jewish and democratic" as worded, and naming Hamas's massacre, and we would even accept "Hamas, designated a terrorist organization under Israeli law" (the analysis's bridge) **if** M1 and M2 are adopted.
- We accept no "Palestinian state" and no "1967" in the text.
- We accept a yes vote on investiture, abstention on budgets, and no ministers.
- We would trade the deputy-Speaker and committee-chair slot to Ta'al or Ra'am.

## 6. Promise check
- **Kept:**
  - Outside "safety net", no blank check (dossier §3).
  - Demands 1 and 2 (crime, demolitions, Kaminitz, development).
  - No endorsement of the Nation-State Law.
  - Anti-occupation clauses: no annexation, outposts, settler terror.
  - No coercion on service.
- **At risk:**
  - Demand 4 (political horizon) is broken in v1 and fixed by M1.
  - The Oct 7 wording without Gaza symmetry contradicts our "condemn harm to all civilians" line; this is fixed by M2.
  - "Military control" instead of الاحتلال (occupation) is fixed by M3.
