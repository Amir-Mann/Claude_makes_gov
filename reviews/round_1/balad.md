# Round 1 review — BALAD (التجمع الوطني الديمقراطي / בל"ד), reviewing as a NON-signatory

## 1. Verdict: ACCEPT WITH CHANGES

This verdict is about tolerance, not signature. Balad does not sign. Balad's position on each vote:

- **Investiture: abstain** (امتناع / הימנעות). We will not vote against, because ousting the "government of bloodshed" is our mandate (dossier §3).
- **Budgets: abstain**, but only if the three changes below are made. If they are not, Balad votes **against** any budget that funds settlement or war.
- **Why our budget vote matters:** the analysis's own arithmetic is 55 for, 53 + 3 against. Balad's 3 votes decide the budget, not the investiture.
- **Hadash and Ta'al:** Balad will **tolerate** their signature and will not break the Joint List faction.
  - It will publicly state its disagreement with clause 4 ("Jewish and democratic") and with the one-sidedness of clause 8.
  - It will not attack its partners as traitors.

**Signatory status:** Balad would not sign as drafted. Balad cannot put its name to clause 4, or to "Judea and Samaria" appearing in Arabic (clause 12).

Balad would sign a separate one-page **"understanding of non-obstruction"** (تفاهم عدم إعاقة). It would cover only:
- Chapter D;
- clause 5 (legitimacy of Arab citizens' representatives);
- clauses 10, 13 and 14 (Jewish terror, no annexation, outposts);
- our abstention commitment.

It would carry an explicit statement that Balad does not endorse clause 4.

## 2. Ledger from Balad's point of view

**Gains**
- Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir and Smotrich are out.
- A funded crime plan: NIS 1bn a year, monitored.
- A successor to Decision 550 worth at least NIS 30bn.
- A freeze on Kaminitz Law enforcement.
- Recognition of Negev villages.
- No annexation.
- "Jewish terror" named in both languages (الإرهاب اليهودي).
- Outposts to be evacuated, with no retroactive legalisation.
- Settler-held powers returned to the army.
- Arab representatives declared "legitimate" (clause 5).
- Service is only voluntary and civil.

**Concessions** (borne indirectly, through our list partners and our abstention)
- "Jewish and democratic" appears, though only as a description of the Basic Laws.
- Hamas is named while Gaza's civilians are not.
- No "occupation" (الاحتلال), no "Palestinian people", no political horizon.
- The Nation-State Law stays.
- The budget will still fund the war machine and existing settlements.

**Net:** positive for tolerance (abstention), but not enough for a signature. Clause 3 ("does not prejudice") protects "state of all its citizens". For abstention, the material gains outweigh concessions we are not signing.

## 3. Must-change items (max 3)

1. **Clause 7: symmetry on civilians.** Add after the existing text:
   "The signatories mourn all innocent civilians killed since 7 October, Israelis and Palestinians alike, including in Gaza"
   (AR: «المدنيين الأبرياء، الإسرائيليين والفلسطينيين على حد سواء، بما في ذلك في غزة»; HE: «ישראלים ופלסטינים כאחד, לרבות ברצועת עזה»).
   This is Abu Shehadeh's own line to the elections committee ("من اليهود والعرب على حد سواء"). Without it, clause 8 reads as a loyalty test.
2. **Clause 9: end the war first.** Replace the opening with:
   "The government will act to consolidate the end of the war in Gaza and prevent its resumption, to ensure that Hamas does not rule Gaza and cannot threaten the citizens of Israel, …"
   (AR: «تثبيت إنهاء الحرب ومنع استئنافها»).
   This matches the Joint List platform ("prevent their resumption").
3. **Clause 12 (Arabic version) and clause 13: no settlement money.**
   - (a) In the Arabic text, use «الضفة الغربية – أراضٍ تحت سيطرة عسكرية منذ 1967». Drop «يهودا والسامرة» from the Arabic, while the Hebrew may keep both names.
   - (b) Add to clause 13: "The state budgets during the term will include no allocations to unauthorised outposts and no special coalition funds for settlement" (AR: «لا ميزانيات للبؤر الاستيطانية ولا أموال ائتلافية خاصة للاستيطان»).

If all three are made, Balad abstains on investiture **and** budgets and tolerates Hadash and Ta'al's signature. The verdict is then effectively ACCEPT for tolerance.

## 4. Nice-to-have (max 3)

- Clause 6: add "the government will not advance legislation expanding grounds for disqualifying lists or candidates (Section 7A)."
- Clause 15: add "civil and economic coordination with the Palestinian Authority," and "the Palestinian people" (الشعب الفلسطيني) once.
- Clause 20: make Arabic's status explicit: "Arabic will be used in all government services; the Nation-State Law's downgrade will not be enforced to restrict it."

## 5. Trade space

- Balad will not demand the word "occupation" or any end-state language. It accepts "military control" plus clause 3.
- It accepts that it holds no cabinet seat and no committee chair. The deputy Speaker post can go to Ta'al.
- It accepts clause 8 naming Hamas's massacre, if clause 7 is made symmetric.
- It drops Section 7A from must-haves if clauses 7, 9 and 12/13 are fixed.

## 6. Promise check

**Kept**
- Not inside a Zionist coalition.
- No vote for war or settlement budgets: abstention only, and only with no outpost money.
- Toppling Netanyahu.
- A funded crime plan.
- Stopping demolitions.
- Not affirming a "Jewish state" ourselves: clause 4 is not our signature (dossier §3, §4a).

**Strained**
- Abstaining on a budget breaks Balad's record of opposing every budget since 1996 (dossier §1b).
- Tolerating partners who sign "Jewish and democratic".

**To voters (2 sentences):**
«أسقطنا حكومة الدم ولم نوقّع على يهودية الدولة ولم نصوّت لميزانية حرب أو استيطان؛ امتنعنا فقط مقابل خطة ممولة ضد الجريمة ووقف الهدم والاعتراف بقرى النقب.»
«هذا ليس تنازلاً عن مشروع دولة جميع مواطنيها، بل استخدام لقوتنا كي يعيش شعبنا بأمان وكرامة.»

English: "We toppled the government of bloodshed without signing the state's Jewishness or voting for a war or settlement budget; we abstained only in return for a funded anti-crime plan, an end to demolitions and recognition of the Negev villages. This does not renounce the state-of-all-its-citizens project; it uses our strength so our people can live in safety and dignity."
