# Round 1 review: Together (Bennett 2026 faction), on draft v1

Reviewer: the BENNETT agent, in role. Dossier: `research/bennett.md`.

## 1. Verdict: ACCEPT WITH CHANGES

The text gets most of the words right. Our real cost is the structure: relying on Arab votes. I can carry that cost only if:
- the text visibly delivers our security and sovereignty-of-law agenda;
- it does not quietly freeze legal settlement.

## 2. Gains and concessions ledger (our worldview)

| Gains | Concessions |

| "הטבח שביצע חמאס", "acts of terror" (cl. 8). The Oct 7 state commission in 14 days (cl. 11): our day-one promise. | **Cl. 3 "irreversible", cl. 14 "no retroactive legalization" and cl. 15 "close off future options"** could be read as a freeze on legal settlement and on regularizing lawful-status land. This contradicts our 28 Sep platform. |

**Net:** positive but thin. The words are fine; the structure is costly. Fixing items 1–3 makes the gains clearly outweigh the concessions.

**The real alternative.**
- A unity government with a Likud that has dropped Netanyahu (66 seats) is better for our voters. Today it is not on offer.
- New elections risk Netanyahu returning.
- So a fixed version of this text is the best one actually available.
- We would also want cl. 2 to leave the door open for any Zionist party (including Likud after Netanyahu) to join later and reduce our dependence on the Arab parties. See nice-to-have 1.

## 3. Must-change items (max 3)

**1. Cl. 9: Hamas.** Replace the first sentence with:

> "Hamas, a terrorist organization under Israeli law (HE: ארגון הטרור חמאס; AR: حركة حماس، المصنّفة منظمة إرهابية وفق القانون الإسرائيلي), will be disarmed and will not rule Gaza or threaten the citizens of Israel. The government will act to that end, in coordination with the international framework for Gaza."

The wording is descriptive ("under Israeli law"), so Ra'am can sign it as a fact.

**2. Chapter C: protect legal settlement.**
- Cl. 14: replace the last sentence with:

  > "No outpost on private land will be legalized. Any other regularization will be only by a government decision, under the law and subject to judicial review."

- Cl. 15: delete "It will not take steps that close off future options."
- Add a new cl. 15a:

  > "Construction in existing communities in accordance with planning law and government decisions will continue (HE: בנייה חוקית ביישובים קיימים תימשך). Illegal construction will be enforced against equally, according to law."

**3. Cl. 16 and cl. 18: enforcement alongside the gains.**
- Cl. 16: add after "criminal organizations":

  > "with the involvement of all security bodies, including the Shin Bet, under law (HE: בשילוב כל גופי הביטחון, לרבות השב״כ, על פי חוק)".

- Cl. 18: replace the Kaminitz sentence with:

  > "Kaminitz Law enforcement against existing homes in localities whose plan is in preparation will be suspended pending an amendment within 6 months. Enforcement against new illegal construction and against criminal organizations will continue."

If all three are fixed, our verdict **flips to ACCEPT**, provided the coalition agreement among the Zionist parties includes the Servants Law (see nice-to-have 2).

## 4. Nice-to-have items

1. Cl. 2: add "The government is based on a Zionist core (HE: ממשלה ציונית), and Zionist parties that accept these principles may join it during the term."
2. Add a clause: "Equal burden: a service law with no blanket exemption quotas, and benefits tied to service (חוק המשרתים)."
3. Cl. 25: replace automatic release with "a breach will be referred to the monitoring committee for 60 days of remedy before release."

## 5. Trade space

- **Would give:** the Decision 550 successor at full size; Negev village recognition (we did 3 in 2021); an Arab deputy Speaker and committee chair; the "טרור יהודי" wording (acceptable because it is framed as an extremist minority); the "military control" description; no sovereignty for 4 years; the return of powers from political appointees to the IDF and the Civil Administration.
- **Would accept from others:** Ra'am's signature on "Jewish and democratic" and on "Hamas, a terror organization under Israeli law". That signature is the cover that makes "Abbas was a great partner" defensible after Oct 7.
- **Would not accept:** Balad in any form; any "Palestinian state", "occupation" or "political horizon" wording; any freeze on legal construction.

## 6. Promise check

- **Kept:**
  - No Palestinian state ("מתנגדים למדינה פלסטינית", 28 Sep).
  - No Netanyahu ("ברור שלא", 3 Jul).
  - The Oct 7 commission on day one.
  - Outpost evacuation (23 Jun).
  - "Jewish state"; no "occupation" (a lifelong line).
  - Area C sovereignty kept as a vision, because cl. 3 preserves positions.
- **Broken:** "לא נסתמך על המפלגות הערביות" (26 Apr) and "no government resting on Arab abstention" (21 Oct). Bennett would again have to say "I broke a promise", as he admitted about 2021 (4 Sep).
- **At risk if not fixed:** "Hamas must be disarmed" (platform); "legal settlement continues" (28 Sep); Negev "law and order".
