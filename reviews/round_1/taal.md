# Ta'al: review of draft v1 (round 1)

## 1. Verdict

**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.**

## 2. Gains vs concessions ledger

The ledger is written from Ta'al's worldview (dossier `research/taal.md`).

**Gains (visible and countable)**
- Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir and Smotrich are out. This is stage 1 of Tibi's plan, achieved (dossier §2).
- A crime plan of at least NIS 1bn a year with an annual homicide report (cl. 16). This is our #1 demand.
- A Decision 550 successor of about NIS 30bn, with grants to Arab local authorities restored (cl. 17). This meets our red line on cuts to local authorities (§3).
- Kaminitz enforcement frozen, master plans, and some demolitions frozen (cl. 18).
- Negev recognition with a deadline (cl. 19).
- An Arab deputy Speaker, a committee chair, and civil-service targets (cl. 20). This continues Tibi's signature issue since 2008.
- "Jewish terror", spelled טרור יהודי / الإرهاب اليهودي, and named against Palestinians (cl. 10). Outposts evacuated, no retroactive legalisation, no annexation, and the powers given to Smotrich's political appointees returned to the military (cl. 12–14).
- "Equal citizens… legitimate part of Israeli democracy" (cl. 5). For a list that was disqualified in September, this is a real answer.
- No coalition discipline and no ministers (cl. 23). This matches our "safety net from outside" (شبكة أمان) doctrine exactly.
- Monitoring, plus clause 25 (a breach releases us). This makes it the opposite of a "blank check".

**Concessions**
- A signed text that contains "Jewish and democratic state" (cl. 4). It is a description and is softened by cl. 3 and by the Declaration's equality clause, but Tibi has called the Nation-State Law apartheid since 2018 (§1b).
- Naming the Hamas massacre as "acts of terror" (cl. 8). This goes beyond our post-2023 line "against targeting civilians". It is defensible because the text is symmetric with cl. 10 and condemns an act, not the Palestinian national movement.
- "יהודה ושומרון" appears alongside "the West Bank" (cl. 12). Tolerable as dual naming.
- No "occupation" (الاحتلال), no Palestinian people, no political horizon.
- A yes vote on investiture. This is consistent with our stage-1 aim.

**Net:** clearly positive. Tibi said "the next government will not be a peace government" and that we are choosing "between the bad and the worse" (JPost 22 Oct 2026). This text delivers far more than "less bad". Three wording fixes are needed so the Joint List does not appear to endorse the Nation-State Law or to erase the Palestinian people.

## 3. Must-change items (at most 3)

1. **Clause 4: non-endorsement of the Nation-State Law.** Problem: "defined in its Basic Laws" can be read as signing Basic Law: Nationhood, which is a public red line ("support does not mean accepting the Nation-State Law", §3). Add:
   > "This clause describes the existing law. It does not constitute endorsement by any signatory of any particular Basic Law, including Basic Law: Israel – the Nation-State of the Jewish People (חוק־יסוד: הלאום / قانون القومية). Each signatory retains its position and its freedom to act lawfully to amend it."
2. **Clause 15: name the Palestinian people.** Problem: the text never says "Palestinian people" (الشعب الفلسطيني / העם הפלסטיני). It must appear, and it does not prejudge statehood. Replace the last sentence with:
   > "It will not take steps that close off the possibility of a future political settlement between Israel and the Palestinian people (העם הפלסטיני / الشعب الفلسطيني), and will maintain civil and security coordination with the Palestinian Authority."
3. **Clause 18: Kaminitz and demolitions.** Problem: "freeze pending an amendment" with no direction, and a freeze limited to localities with plans "in preparation", fall short of our public demand to repeal Kaminitz and halt demolitions, including the Triangle eviction orders (§2). Replace with:
   > "Enforcement of the Kaminitz Law (Amendment 116 to the Planning and Building Law) will be frozen immediately. Within 6 months the government will legislate an amendment that cancels the administrative fines and administrative demolition orders it introduced (قانون كامينيتس / חוק קמיניץ). Pending approved master plans, there will be a moratorium on the demolition of inhabited homes in Arab localities, including the Negev and the Triangle (Tayibe, Tira, Qalansawe)."

**If all three are fixed, the verdict flips to ACCEPT.** Items 1 and 2 are the minimum. Without them Ta'al cannot sign the document, although it could still vote for the investiture.

## 4. Nice-to-have items (at most 3)

- Clause 20: a Joint List seat on the **Finance Committee** (ועדת הכספים / لجنة المالية), and support for replacing Speaker Ohana in the first week of the new Knesset.
- Clause 20: a programme to place Arab teachers and doctors (about 8,000 and 4,000), with residency slots for the doctors.
- Clause 6: add "including the status quo on al-Haram al-Sharif / Temple Mount (الحرم الشريف / הר הבית)", and "the Citizenship Law will be reviewed".

## 5. Trade space

- We accept descriptive "Jewish and democratic" and an explicit Hamas clause. If Yashar or Yisrael Beiteinu need it, we would also accept "Hamas, designated a terrorist organization under Israeli law", **provided** must-change 1 and 2 are adopted.
- We accept no ministers and abstention-only on budgets.
- We drop "occupation" in favour of "military control".
- We would give up the al-Aqsa and Citizenship Law items before giving up anything in Chapter D.

## 6. Promise check

**Kept:**
- Topple Netanyahu without letting him stay on as caretaker.
- Outside support only.
- A comprehensive crime plan, demolitions, master plans, Negev recognition, and local-authority budgets.
- No obligation to vote for settlements (cl. 23).
- Deputy Speaker.

**At risk without the fixes:**
- "Not accepting the Nation-State Law" (must-change 1).
- Kaminitz repeal (must-change 3).
- The Palestinian people's presence in the text (must-change 2).

**Not delivered:**
- An Ohana-removal commitment.
- The Finance Committee seat.
- A political horizon. This was accepted in advance: "not a peace government".

We did not enter their government and did not give up a single principle: our national positions are written down as untouched."
