# Round 1 review — Yisrael Beiteinu (in role)

## 1. Verdict: **REJECT as drafted** (flips to **ACCEPT WITH CHANGES** if the 3 must-changes are made)

The text has no draft law, a soft line on Hamas, and the Joint List as a signed parliamentary partner. Together these make it a Meretz-style document with our signature on it. Our voters already lost seats to Winter over the "government backed by Arabs" warnings, and they will not forgive that.

## 2. Gains vs concessions (by our worldview)

| Gains | Concessions |
|---|---|
| Netanyahu out; Haredi parties out | Breaks "לא נתמוך בשום ממשלת מיעוט, גם לא לתקופת מעבר" (15 Oct). The cabinet is 48. |
| State commission of inquiry within 14 days (our "first decision") | Breaks "Without Ra'am, without the Joint List" (15 Oct). **Hadash and Ta'al are signatories** with a budget lever (cl. 22, 25). |
| "מדינה יהודית ודמוקרטית" + the Declaration of Independence (our 2022 line) | A **universal draft/service law is absent**. Cl. 21 even makes Arab service "voluntary… no security link". This is the opposite of our precondition. |
| Hamas named as the perpetrator of the 7 Oct massacre; "Hamas does not rule Gaza" | No "dismantling/elimination of Hamas". That was our own test for Knesset legitimacy (23 Sep). |
| Jewish rioters handled with administrative orders (we said "חד וחלק כן") | Cl. 10 uses "טרור יהודי" with no explicit "קומץ" (handful). That can read as a slur on 630k residents. |
| "יהודה ושומרון" appears; no Palestinian state, no "כיבוש" | A Kaminitz freeze and Negev recognition timetables (cl. 18–19) with no enforcement counterweight. |
| Crime plan of NIS 1bn a year (we call it טרור פלילי) | Clause 25 lets three Arab lists threaten to topple the government every year. |

**Net judgment:** negative as drafted. Our three core deliverables (draft, Hamas, no Joint List dependency) are missing, while we pay our largest promise cost. With the fixes below it becomes positive, because Netanyahu, the Haredim and the commission are worth it.

## 3. Must-change items

**MC1 — Add a service clause (new cl. 6a). Replace cl. 21.**
> "During the term the government will enact a **Service Law for All** (HE: חוק שירות לכולם; AR: قانون الخدمة للجميع). Every citizen at 18 serves in military or civil-national service (HE: שירות אזרחי-לאומי; AR: الخدمة المدنية-الوطنية). There will be **no quotas and no blanket exemptions**, and state benefits will be conditioned on service. In Arab, Druze and Circassian society, civil-national service will be carried out in the citizens' own communities, with no security duties, through a national service authority. It will expand by published annual targets toward full participation by the end of the term, with equal benefits."

**MC2 — Hamas (cl. 8–9).** Replace the opening of cl. 9 with:
> "**Hamas is a terrorist organization** (HE: ארגון טרור; AR: منظمة إرهابية). The government will act to **dismantle Hamas's military and governing capabilities** (HE: פירוק יכולותיו הצבאיות והשלטוניות של חמאס; AR: تفكيك قدرات حماس العسكرية والسلطوية) so that it does not rule Gaza and cannot threaten the citizens of Israel…"

Every signatory that wants our votes must sign this sentence. That is the test we set on 23 Sep.

**MC3 — Chapter E: Ra'am only.** Make the parliamentary agreement **between the government and Ra'am alone**. Hadash and Ta'al are not signatories. Their votes are their own affair. In Lieberman's words to i24: "Это их проблема" (that's their problem).

Delete cl. 25. Replace it with:
> "Disputes on implementation will be brought to the monitoring committee. **Chapter D is a government commitment to all Arab citizens, not a price for votes.**"

## 4. Nice-to-have

1. Cl. 10: replace "Jewish terror" with "**טרור של קומץ פורעים יהודים** (terror by a handful of Jewish rioters), who harm Palestinians, **the settlement enterprise** and the State of Israel".
2. Cl. 18: change "frozen" to "Kaminitz enforcement will be **prioritized** against new construction and construction tied to crime organizations, pending amendment". Cl. 19: add "together with enforcement against new illegal construction".
3. Add a line saying the coalition agreement of the Zionist parties will include **civil marriage, public transport on Shabbat and cancelling coalition funds**.

## 5. Trade space

- We accept Chapter D almost in full, including the 550 successor (my own treasury funded 550 in 2021). We also accept Arab committee chairs and a deputy Speaker.
- We accept Ra'am's yes votes on investiture and budgets, plus cl. 13–14 (no annexation for 4 years; illegal outposts evacuated by law). Settlement blocs and Area C are untouched.
- We would drop nice-to-haves 1–2 in exchange for MC1.
- **The real alternative:** our first preference is a Likud revolt, meaning a Zionist majority with a Likud that drops Netanyahu (66 seats, no Arab dependence). We will press for that for about 2–3 weeks.
  - New elections are risky for us at 7 seats after the leak to Winter.
  - So this framework, *with the fixes*, is a legitimate Plan B. It should not be signed before the Likud option is tested.

## 6. Promise check

- **Kept:** no Netanyahu; no Shas or UTJ; Oct 7 commission; Jewish and democratic state; no Palestinian state and no "כיבוש"; Hamas named; heavy hand on rioters.
- **Kept only with MC1:** "חוק גיוס שיחול על כולם".
- **Kept only with MC2:** "חיסול החמאס" as the government's goal.
- **Broken:** "ממשלה ציונית של רוב" (a Zionist majority government) and "בלי רע"מ" (without Ra'am). MC3 at least keeps "בלי הרשימה המשותפת" (without the Joint List).
