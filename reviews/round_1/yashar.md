# Round 1 review: Yashar! (ישר! עם איזנקוט) on draft v1

## 1. Verdict
**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.** The structure is right: a Zionist cabinet under Eisenkot, plus a parliamentary agreement in which Arab parties sign but hold no cabinet seats. But two of our four public coalition rules are not yet met. These are rule 3, service for all, and rule 4, Hamas as a terror organisation to be dismantled (dossier §3).

## 2. Ledger (by Yashar's own worldview)
| Gains | Concessions |
|---|---|
| Eisenkot PM; Netanyahu out; no rotation (dossier §3). | We depend on Arab votes for investiture and budgets. We never ruled out outside support, but voters heard "Ra'am won't be part of the government" (26.9). |
| State commission within 14 days (cl. 11), our flagship. | Cl. 21 makes Arab service only voluntary, which breaks rule 3. |
| "Jewish and democratic" plus the Declaration of Independence (cl. 4), our rules 1 and 2, now signed by the Arab parties. | Hamas is named only as perpetrator of the massacre, with no "terror organisation / dismantle" (cl. 8–9). That falls short of rule 4. |
| Administrative orders against Jewish terror (cl. 10); military powers taken back from Smotrich (cl. 13); illegal outposts and farms evacuated (cl. 14). All of these are our own campaign pledges. | "Territories under military control" (cl. 12) is tolerable only because it sits beside "יהודה ושומרון" and is factual. |
| No Palestinian state and no "occupation" or Nakba wording; clause 3 reserves our positions. | Cl. 15, "will not close off future options", is vague and can be read as a settlement freeze or a two-state hint. |
| Arab crime plan (cl. 16): our step 5, now with Arab partners on board. | The Chapter D price, roughly NIS 1bn a year plus a ≈30bn plan. This is consistent with our differential-budgeting pledge. |

**Net:** clearly positive *if* the three fixes below are made. Without them, the text hands Netanyahu the line "Eisenkot dropped his own conditions for Arab votes".

## 3. Must-change items
**(1) Cl. 8–9: Hamas (rule 4).** Replace cl. 9's first sentence with:
"Hamas is a terrorist organization (HE: ארגון טרור; AR: منظمة إرهابية), designated as such under Israeli law. The government will act to dismantle its military and governing capabilities (HE: פירוק יכולותיו הצבאיות והשלטוניות של חמאס; AR: تفكيك القدرات العسكرية والسلطوية لحماس) so that it does not rule Gaza and cannot threaten the citizens of Israel, in coordination with the international framework for Gaza."
*Bridge:* the dismantling commitment binds the government. Under cl. 23 the Arab parties keep freedom of vote on defence, and the first sentence is descriptive, like cl. 4.

**(2) Cl. 21: Service for all (rule 3).** Replace with:
"The government will enact a **National Service for All law** (HE: חוק שירות ממלכתי לכל; AR: قانون الخدمة المدنية/الوطنية للجميع) establishing that every citizen serves the state, in the IDF or in civilian service. For Arab citizens, service during this term will be civilian, in their own communities, run with the Arab local authorities, with no security link, and promoted through incentives and equal benefits ('whoever serves is rewarded', HE: מי שמשרת, מתוגמל), not sanctions."
The Arab parties keep freedom of vote on the law (cl. 23).

**(3) Cl. 15: Security freedom of action.** Replace "It will not take steps that close off future options" with:
"Israel retains full security responsibility and freedom of operational action against terrorism throughout the area (HE: חופש פעולה ביטחוני מלא; AR: حرية العمل الأمني). In accordance with clause 3, no final-status decision will be taken during the term."

**If all three are fixed, our verdict flips to ACCEPT.**

## 4. Nice-to-have items
1. Cl. 11: add "the decade preceding it and the conduct of the war" (our platform's wording).
2. Cl. 5: add "on the basis of shared citizenship, mutual responsibility and shared life (HE: ממלכתיות וחיים משותפים)".
3. Cl. 2: call the cabinet "a Zionist government" (HE: ממשלה ציונית) explicitly.

## 5. Trade space
- We accept "Jewish terror" (טרור יהודי) in cl. 10.
- We accept the full Chapter D package, including the Kaminitz freeze and Negev recognition.
- We accept the "military control" description in cl. 12.
- We accept no sanctions on Arab citizens this term, as long as the universal law exists.
- In return we need the Hamas wording and the service law. We could drop nice-to-haves 1–3 entirely.
- If the Arab parties cannot sign "dismantle", we accept moving the dismantling sentence into a government-only annex. The descriptive "terrorist organization" must stay in the joint text.

## 6. Promise check
- **Kept:**
  - Will not serve under Netanyahu.
  - Commission within 14 days.
  - Jewish and democratic plus the Declaration.
  - Ra'am and the Joint List get no cabinet seats (26.9 pledge).
  - No Palestinian state.
  - Administrative orders against Jewish terror.
  - Smotrich's powers reversed.
  - Illegal construction removed.
  - War on crime in Arab society (dossier §2–3).
- **Broken as drafted:**
  - Rule 3 (service for all).
  - Rule 4 (Hamas). Fixed by items 1 and 2.
- **Strained:** relying on Arab votes. This is defensible because we never ruled out outside support, and the Arab parties sign our rules.
