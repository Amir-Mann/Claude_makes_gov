## 3. Must-change items

**1. Clause 2: drop the "Zionist government" framing and say partnership.**
The problem: v1 reproduces the "Zionist majority" logic we campaigned against. Our voters, and Arab voters, will read it as a downgrade of partnership.
Replacement wording:
> "The government will be formed by Yashar, Together, Yisrael Beiteinu and The Democrats, headed by Gadi Eisenkot, **in partnership with** Ra'am, Hadash and Ta'al (HE: בשותפות עם; AR: بالشراكة مع), who sign a parliamentary agreement with it… The signatories may, by mutual agreement, widen the government during the term."

**2. Clause 10: make Jewish terror fully symmetric and name administrative detention.**
Replacement wording:
> "The government will fight Jewish terror with **the same determination and the same tools used against Palestinian terror** (HE: באותה נחישות ובאותם כלים המופעלים נגד הטרור הפלסטיני; AR: بنفس الحزم ونفس الأدوات المستخدمة ضد الإرهاب الفلسطيني), including **administrative detention under law and judicial review** (HE: מעצרים מנהליים; AR: الاعتقال الإداري), a dedicated Shin Bet–police unit, dismantling of terror hubs, and prosecution."

The "extremist minority" sentence can stay, for Bennett.

**3. New clause 11a: an equal draft law.**
Replacement wording:
> "Within its first year, the government will legislate an **equal service law** for the Haredi population, with targets and sanctions (HE: חוק גיוס שוויוני)."

Clause 23's free vote for the parties to the parliamentary agreement covers the Arab parties. Clause 21 (voluntary civil service in Arab society) stays as it is.

## 4. Nice-to-haves
1. **Clause 15:** "security coordination with the Palestinian Authority and support for its reform under international supervision" (Golan's plan). This also gives Hadash a sliver of political horizon.
2. **Clause 6:** "The government will advance anchoring the principle of equality in a Basic Law" (HE: עיגון עקרון השוויון בחוק יסוד).
3. **Clause 16:** "with a lead minister for public security". We will claim the Public Security portfolio in the coalition agreement.

## 5. Trade space
- We accept Yashar's and Lieberman's stronger Hamas wording ("Hamas, a terrorist organization under Israeli law…"). We would push for it ourselves.
- We give up "occupation", two states and repeal of the Nation-State Law for this term, because clause 3 protects our positions.
- We accept Arab parties outside the cabinet for now, if must-change 1 adds the partnership wording and the option to widen the government.
- We could accept a softer Kaminitz timetable (e.g. 9 months instead of 6) if the freeze on demolitions stays.
