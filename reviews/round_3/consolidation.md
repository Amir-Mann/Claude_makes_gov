## Final-text fixes (cost-free, no re-review needed)
1. **Clause 6a.** "On this civil-national track" becomes "on the civil-national track for Arab citizens set out in this clause … This protection does not apply to any other population." Requested by Yesh Atid and Yisrael Beiteinu, and it is the Arab parties' own intent.
2. **Clause 21.** Ra'am's Service Law vote is conditional on the bill containing clause 6a in full. Requested by Ra'am. Yisrael Beiteinu loses nothing, because 6a is its own agreed text.
3. **Annex 1.** It now notes clause 13 only "as regards the prohibition of annexation, the return of powers, and the budget prohibitions". Requested by Hadash, Ta'al and Balad. The government is not a party to Annex 1, so this costs the Zionist parties nothing.
4. The remaining reservations are recorded in Annex 2.
