# Ra'am final review of draft v3 (round 3)

## Verdict: **SIGN WITH RESERVATION**

## The two new trades

**1. Deputy Minister subordinate to the minister, with PM approval, outside the cabinet (cl. 22): acceptable.**

- The substance we asked for is intact: a Ra'am nominee leads the NIS 1bn-a-year crime plan (cl. 16).
- Our voters judge us by fewer murders, not by a seat in the security cabinet.
- Losing budget authority is a real cost. The annual homicide report and the monitoring committee (cl. 24–25) are our leverage instead.

**2. Binding vote FOR the whole Service Law, Haredi track included (cl. 21): the hardest point in the text.**

- *Against signing:* we are a religious party, so compelling yeshiva students sits badly with parts of our base; it closes doors to Shas and UTJ; and "the duty applies to all" covers Arab citizens in principle.
- *For signing:*
  - Ra'am voted against the blanket exemption law (dossier §3, §4e).
  - Equal burden is not our red line. The Haredi parties sit in Netanyahu's bloc, and in 2021 we governed without them.
  - The Arab track is exactly Abbas's model, and our round-2 protection is adopted verbatim: civilian, in our communities, no security link, incentives not sanctions, and no existing right or allowance lost (cl. 6a).
  - We did not trade principle. We traded a free vote for a written guarantee that our communities lose nothing.

## The reservation (attached to our signature, not a change to the text)

Ra'am's vote under cl. 21 is for the law **"as set out in clause 6a."**

If the bill brought to the Knesset omits or dilutes any of the following, Ra'am is released from the cl. 21 Service Law vote and may abstain:

- the Arab civil-national track;
- no security link;
- "not sanctions";
- the protection of existing rights.

Arabic: «التزام الموحدة بالتصويت مشروط بتضمين نص البند 6أ كاملاً في القانون».

## Net ledger

**Gains:** everything in v2 (crime plan led by us, a successor to Decision 550, Kaminitz fines and orders cancelled for existing homes, no demolitions or forced relocation in the Negev villages, al-Aqsa status quo, Nation-State Law disclaimer, "the Palestinian people"), plus "אל-אקצא" in the Hebrew text.

**Concessions:**
- "Jewish and democratic" as a description of existing law.
- Hamas named and to be disarmed, by the government and the international framework.
- A subordinate deputy minister.
- A yes vote on Haredi service.

All of these are descriptive, or are bounded by a written protection for our own public.

**To voters (AR):**
> "لم نصوّت على تجنيد أبنائنا – بل ثبّتنا أن خدمتهم مدنية في بلداتهم، بلا عقوبات وبلا مسّ بأي حق. وفي المقابل نقود محاربة الجريمة ونوقف الهدم."

("We did not vote to conscript our sons. We secured that their service is civilian, in their own towns, with no sanctions and no harm to any right. In return, we lead the fight against crime and stop the demolitions.")

**Net:** the visible gains clearly outweigh the concessions. This is a better deal than 2021, and we sign.
