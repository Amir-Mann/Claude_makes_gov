# Round 3 (final) review — BALAD (التجمع الوطني الديمقراطي / בל"ד)

## Final verdict: JOIN ANNEX 1 + ABSTAIN (on investiture and budgets)

**Our round-2 item: met.**
- Annex 1 now lists specific clauses: 7, 10, 12–14, 15a, 16–20, and the clause 15 reference to "the Palestinian people" (الشعب الفلسطيني).
- It disclaims "any clause not listed above, including clauses 4 and 6a, nor of any Basic Law" (AR: «لا يشكّل هذا الإعلان تأييداً لأي بند غير مذكور أعلاه، ولا لأي قانون أساس»).
- Explicitly not noted: Jewish-state clause 4, the Service Law in 6a, Hamas in clause 9, and freedom of action in clause 15.
- The note on "the Palestinian people" is a gain for us.

## Do the v3 changes hurt Balad?
- **Clause 13, "construction in existing communities will continue" (יימשכו): a real cost, but not decisive.**
  - Annex 1 "notes" clauses 12–14 as a block, so rivals could claim Balad "noted" settlement construction.
  - The surrounding text limits the damage: no annexation; powers returned to the army; **no outpost or coalition money**; no outpost legalised during the term; evacuation with quarterly reports.
  - Our remedy is unilateral. When Balad signs Annex 1, it issues its own statement: «إشارتنا إلى البند 13 تخص منع الضم وإعادة الصلاحيات ووقف تمويل البؤر، ولا تعني قبول أي بناء استيطاني؛ الاستيطان كله غير شرعي» ("Our reference to clause 13 concerns preventing annexation, returning powers and stopping outpost funding; it does not mean accepting any settlement construction; all settlement is illegitimate").
  - No change to the text is needed.
- **Clause 14, regularisations decided before 27 Oct may proceed:** these are limited, under judicial review, and none is on private Palestinian land. Tolerable.
- **Clause 6a, "no existing right lost" on the Arab civil track:** a gain, and Annex 1 disclaims 6a. Balad keeps a free vote against the Service Law.
- **Clause 24, Annex 1 factions as observers on the monitoring committee:** a gain. Balad's MKs see and publish the Chapter D reports.

## Votes
- **Investiture: abstain.** Our abstention keeps the 55–53 count.
- **Budgets: abstain,** conditional on clause 13 (no outpost or coalition funds) and Chapter D funding appearing in every budget law. If either is breached, we vote against, and Annex 1 lapses under its own terms.
- **No-confidence and dissolution bills:** Balad will not support them while Chapter D is implemented.

## Net judgment
Without signing a Jewish state, a war budget or a settlement budget, Balad helps topple the "government of bloodshed" and gains concrete results:
- Kaminitz enforcement on existing homes frozen;
- no demolitions in the Negev villages;
- NIS 1bn a year against crime;
- a successor to Decision 550;
- Arabic in government services;
- the al-Aqsa status quo;
- "the Palestinian people" named;
- Gaza's dead mourned, and the end of the war consolidated.

Gains clearly exceed concessions, so we join Annex 1 and abstain.

**To voters:** «أسقطنا حكومة الدم دون أن نوقّع على يهودية الدولة أو نصوّت لميزانية حرب أو استيطان، وامتناعنا أداة لحماية شعبنا لا تنازل عن مشروع دولة جميع مواطنيها.»
