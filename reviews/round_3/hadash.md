# Final review of draft v3, as Hadash (חד"ש / الجبهة)

## Verdict: **SIGN WITH RESERVATION** (Annex 1)

## Was our item met? Yes.

- **Clause 6a.** It now reads "אף אזרח לא ייפגע בזכות, בקצבה או בהטבה קיימת בשל אי־שירות / لا يُحرم أي مواطن من حق أو مخصص أو امتياز قائم بسبب عدم الخدمة".
  - The protection covers the civil-national track, and that track is the one for Arab citizens.
  - Our "no coercion" red line therefore holds (dossier §2).
  - The general rule "whoever serves is rewarded" bites mainly on the Haredi track. That is not our fight.
- **Annex 1.** It now:
  - disclaims every clause it does not list, "including clauses 4 and 6a", and every Basic Law;
  - names "the Palestinian people" (cl. 15) explicitly.
  - We sign no loyalty language.

## New gains in v3
- **A Joint List deputy Speaker** (cl. 20). Ra'am no longer monopolizes Arab representation.
- **Observer status on the monitoring committee**, with its reports (cl. 24). This is our tool for judging whether Chapter D is implemented.
- **"No unauthorized outpost will be legalized during the term"** (cl. 14), with quarterly reports on evacuation. This is stronger than v2.

## Changes that hurt
1. **Cl. 13:** "planning and construction will continue (יימשכו)" in existing settlements. This openly continues settlement building. Annex 1 lists clauses 12–14, so we must make clear what we are *not* noting.
2. **Cl. 14:** regularizations decided before 27.10.2026 may proceed (not on private land). This may let parts of Smotrich's legalization decisions through.
3. **Clauses 10 and 16 are listed in Annex 1 unchanged.** Clause 10 still includes administrative detention, and clause 16 still includes the Shin Bet role. Our round-2 nice-to-have was not adopted.

None of these is decisive. We sign nothing in the government text, and our safety net remains conditional on Chapter D.

## Reservation (our own statement accompanying Annex 1; no change to the government text)

> "Hadash notes clauses 12–14 for their prohibition of annexation, of outpost funding and legalization, and for the evacuation of outposts. It regards all settlements in the territories occupied in 1967 as illegal and opposes any construction in them. Noting clauses 10 and 16 does not mean accepting administrative detention or Shin Bet involvement in civilian policing, which Hadash opposes in principle."

- HE key terms: "כל ההתנחלויות בשטחים הכבושים מ־1967 אינן חוקיות"; "מתנגדת עקרונית למעצרים מנהליים".
- AR key terms: "جميع المستوطنات في الأراضي المحتلة عام 1967 غير شرعية"; "نعارض مبدئيًا الاعتقال الإداري".

## Net judgment
Demands 1–3 are met in money and timelines, and demand 4 minimally: "the Palestinian people", no annexation, outposts frozen and evacuated. We get representation and monitoring. We concede only tolerance, not signatures. **Visible gains clearly outweigh concessions. Hadash signs Annex 1.**
