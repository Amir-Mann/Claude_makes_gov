# Ta'al: final review of draft v3 (round 3)

## Verdict

**SIGN WITH RESERVATION** (Annex 1). No must-change.

## Our round-2 item

Met in full:
- **Clause 20:** a deputy Speaker from each of the two Arab lists, with the exact HE/AR wording.
- **Clause 24:** the Annex 1 factions are observers on the monitoring committee and receive its reports.

The Joint List now has visible standing of its own. Ta'al's slot from the August list deal can be honoured.

## Changes since v2: do any hurt us?

- **Clause 14 (no unauthorised outpost legalised during the term; quarterly evacuation reports):** a gain.
- **Clause 18 ("new" construction means built after swearing-in):** a gain. Every existing home in the Triangle and the Negev is protected.
- **Clause 6a (no existing right, allowance or benefit lost for not serving on the civil-national track):** a gain. It answers our round-2 nice-to-have, and Annex 1 explicitly disclaims 6a.
- **Clause 15a (al-Aqsa named in Hebrew too):** a gain.
- **Annex 1 (clause-specific list; everything else disclaimed, including clauses 4 and 6a; "the Palestinian people" in clause 15 noted):** a gain. It is cleaner than v2.
- **Clause 13 ("planning and construction will continue (יימשכו) in existing communities"):** the one loss. Our dossier is clear: no vote for settlements and no support for settlement budgets. Annex 1 "notes in particular" clauses 12–14 as a block, which could be read as welcoming continued settlement building. This needs a reservation, not a refusal: we have no duty to vote, and the clause adds no new budget.
- **The other changes** (Ra'am voting for the Service Law, the subordinate deputy minister, "the government is not a party"): neutral for us.

## Reservation (Ta'al's own sentence, appended to its Annex 1 signature)

> "Ta'al's reference to clauses 12–14 concerns the prohibition of annexation, the evacuation of outposts and the ban on legalising them. It does not express support for settlement construction, which we regard as illegal under international law (الاستيطان غير شرعي وفق القانون الدولي)."

We also keep, without reservation in the text, our principled objection to administrative detention as a tool against anyone. We do not sign clause 10's list of tools; we only note the clause.

## Net judgment

Decisively positive: Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir and Smotrich are out; the crime plan, the 550 successor, the Kaminitz and demolitions freeze, Negev recognition and a no-legalisation clause for outposts are all won; and the Joint List gets a deputy Speaker and a monitoring seat. We give only a pledge not to bring the government down, and we sign no line of Zionist doctrine.
