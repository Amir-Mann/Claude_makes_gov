# Round 3 (final) review — Yisrael Beiteinu (in role)

## Final verdict: **SIGN WITH RESERVATION**

**Our remaining item is met.** Clause 21 now reads: "Ra'am will vote in favor of the Service Law for All as set out in clause 6a." Our flagship law is no longer hostage to a free vote.

## Changes made for others: do they hurt us?

**6a, "no existing right lost" (civil-national track).** We can live with it *for Arab citizens this term*, since we never promised sanctions on Arab citizens in this Knesset; the principle "the duty applies to all" is in law. The danger is a **Haredi loophole**: "civil-national service" is also a general option in the first bullet. Haredi draft-dodgers could claim they are on "this track", or petition the High Court that keeping Arab benefits while cutting theirs is discriminatory.

**This is our reservation**, recorded as an interpretive note in the coalition agreement among the Zionist parties (not a reopening of the text):
> "The protection in clause 6a applies only to the community-based civilian track for Arab, Druze-optional and Circassian-optional citizens defined in that clause. It does not apply to yeshiva students or to any other population. For them, 'state benefits are tied to service' (מי שלא משרת – לא מתוגמל) applies in full."
> (Hebrew phrase: "whoever doesn't serve isn't rewarded")

**Deputy Speaker for the Joint List (cl. 20).** Acceptable. The post is ceremonial; Joint List MKs have held it before, and it confers no government role.

**Joint List observers on the monitoring committee (cl. 24).** Acceptable. They have no vote, and the government "is not a party to" Annex 1. The Joint List remains outside the government and outside the agreement, so "בלי הרשימה המשותפת" (without the Joint List) is kept.

**Annex 1 disclaims clauses 4 and 6a and does not list clause 9 (Hamas).** That confirms our line rather than hurting it: the Joint List did not pass our Hamas test, so it is not a partner. Ra'am signed the whole framework, including "Hamas, a terrorist organization… will be disarmed". The government's dependence on the Joint List and Balad abstaining remains our weak point; we will say plainly that we do not count on them.

## Net
Gains (Netanyahu and Haredim out, a Service Law Ra'am must vote for, Oct 7 commission, no coalition funds, Hamas to be disarmed, no "כיבוש"/Palestinian state) outweigh the broken "בלי רע"מ" / "no minority government" pledges and a subordinate Ra'am deputy minister, especially against new elections at 7 seats.

**One-line net:** We sign. The gains outweigh the broken "without Ra'am" pledge, provided the 6a protection is confined to the Arab civil track.
