## The clause 6a protection
- The protection says: "On **this civil-national track**, no citizen will lose any existing right for not serving."
- The first bullet of clause 6a offers *every* citizen "the IDF or civil-national service". So the protection could be read as covering the general civil track.
- On that reading, yeshiva students could register for the civil track on paper and keep all their benefits. That would hollow out the core of the law and our promise "מי שלא משרת — לא יקבל" ("whoever doesn't serve gets nothing").
- For Arab citizens, the protection is fair and consistent with Lapid's support for incentive-based civil service.

**Reservation, as an interpretive note recorded at signing:**
> Yesh Atid signs on the understanding that the protection in clause 6a applies only to **the civil-national track for Arab citizens** (HE: המסלול האזרחי־לאומי לאזרחים הערבים; AR: المسار المدني للمواطنين العرب). It does not apply to yeshiva students or any other population. Phased implementation begins within the first year on every track.

**Preferred fix:** change "On this civil-national track" to "**On the civil-national track for Arab citizens**". This matches round 2's stated intent and costs no party anything. If it is made, our verdict becomes **SIGN**. If not, we still sign with the reservation; we will not refuse.
