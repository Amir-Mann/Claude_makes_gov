# Round 3 (final) review: The Democrats, draft v3

## Verdict: **SIGN WITH RESERVATION**

**Clause 14 (our remaining item): met.**
- Our wording was adopted verbatim: "No unauthorized outpost will be legalized during the term" (לא יוכשר במהלך הקדנציה אף מאחז בלתי מורשה).
- Only steps decided before 27 Oct 2026 may proceed, under judicial review and not on private land.
- Evacuation progress will be published quarterly.

**Changes made for other parties**
- **Clause 13, "construction… will continue" (יימשכו).** This is the real cost, and ex-Meretz voters will feel it.
  - It is bounded: existing communities only, by law and government decisions, with no annexation, no money for outposts and no coalition funds.
  - We had already conceded existing settlements as a category (as distinct from outposts) (dossier §6).
  - Tolerable, with the reservation below.
- **Clause 22, Ra'am's Deputy Minister subordinate to the minister.** No harm, and likely a gain.
  - We will demand the policing portfolio ("נדרוש את התיק לביטחון פנים", 10 Aug 2026).
  - Ra'am's deputy would then run the crime plan under a Democrats minister: Jewish-Arab partnership in practice.
- **Clauses 6a/21.** Ra'am votes *for* the Service Law, which advances "גיוס לכולם" (draft for all). Protecting existing rights keeps the partnership credible.
- **A deputy Speaker per Arab list, and Joint List observers on the monitoring committee.** Both move closer to Golan's 28 Oct call for "partnership with Israel's Arab citizens".

**Reservation (recorded alongside our signature)**
1. We read "existing communities" (clause 13) as communities lawfully established within their approved jurisdiction: no new communities, and no expansion through retroactive legalization. In the cabinet we will oppose decisions that entrench de-facto annexation. Our positions on separation and two states are reserved under clause 3.
2. In the coalition agreement we will seek the ministry responsible for policing, so that clauses 16 and 22 sit under our responsibility. This is not a precondition for our signature.

**Net judgment.** Clearly positive. We get:
- Netanyahu and the Haredi parties out;
- an Arab party in a government post;
- Jewish terror fought with the same tools as Palestinian terror;
- outposts evacuated and none legalized;
- no annexation;
- an Oct 7 commission;
- an equal service law;
- NIS 1bn a year against crime and a Decision 550 successor.

The price is silence on "occupation" and two states, and continued lawful construction in settlements.

> "הוצאנו את נתניהו והחרדים, הבאנו מפלגה ערבית לממשלה, נלחמים בטרור היהודי ומפנים מאחזים — ואף מאחז לא יוכשר."

(English: We removed Netanyahu and the Haredim, brought an Arab party into the government, are fighting Jewish terror and evacuating outposts, and not a single outpost will be legalized.)
