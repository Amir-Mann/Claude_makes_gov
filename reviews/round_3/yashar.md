# Round 3 (final) review: Yashar! (ישר! עם איזנקוט) on draft v3

## Final verdict: **SIGN**

## Our remaining item: met, and exceeded
- We asked that Ra'am support the civil-national track (cl. 21).
- v3 goes further: Ra'am "will vote in favor of the Service Law for All as set out in clause 6a, including its civil-national track".
- All four of our coalition rules are now accepted in writing by the party that supports us from outside:
  - Jewish and democratic (cl. 4);
  - the Declaration of Independence (cl. 4);
  - service for all (cl. 6a + 21);
  - Hamas a terror organisation, disarmed, not ruling Gaza (cl. 9).
- Eisenkot's 26 Sept statement ("no party currently accepts the rules") is now answered on the record. Ra'am accepted them.
- "מי שמשרת – מתוגמל" was added (cl. 6a). "The government is not a party to Annex 1" was added.

## Do changes made for others hurt us?
- **"No existing right lost for non-service" (cl. 6a).** It applies to the Arab civil-national track only. Benefits stay tied to service in general, so the Haredi track keeps its teeth. It matches our incentives-first approach for Arab citizens. **No harm.**
- **Deputy Minister subordinate, approved by the PM, outside the cabinet and security cabinet (cl. 22).** This strengthens our line that Ra'am is not in the government. **Helps.**
- **"No unauthorized outpost legalized during the term" plus quarterly reporting (cl. 14), with planning in existing communities that "יימשכו על פי חוק".** This is our "settlement must be legal" position exactly. Some of our right flank (Kahana, Cohen) may grumble, but steps decided before 27 Oct proceed. **Acceptable.**
- **A deputy Speaker for the Joint List; Annex 1 observers on the monitoring committee.** Likud will use both as attack lines. Both are parliamentary or advisory roles with no executive power. **Minor cost, accepted.**
- **Annex 1 disclaims cl. 4 and 6a.** The Joint List is outside the government, and its declaration is unilateral; it is not a partner. **Tolerable.**
- **"Temple Mount / al-Aqsa" in the Hebrew text.** This is descriptive of the status quo. **No harm.**

## Net judgment
Yashar gets the premiership, the commission at the first meeting, all four rules signed, administrative orders against Jewish terror, and the end of Smotrich's extra powers. Its concessions are presentational: a Ra'am deputy minister outside the cabinet and Joint List parliamentary roles. Gains clearly outweigh costs.
