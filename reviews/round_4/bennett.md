# Round 4: TV statement and signature check (Bennett / Together)

## 1. Read on TV? YES IF one change is made

Change the Negev line. As written, "recognize the villages in the Negev" sounds like recognizing *all* of them. That contradicts our Negev law-and-order plan, and Likud would clip it.

The framework itself (cl. 19) promises a recognition *process*, in agreement with residents.

- **HE:** "להפסיק להרוס בתים קיימים של משפחות, ולקדם הסדרה והכרה בכפרים בנגב שמחכים כבר עשרות שנים."
- **EN:** "To stop demolishing families' existing homes, and to advance the regularization and recognition of villages in the Negev that have waited for decades."
- **AR:** "...وأن نمضي في تنظيم القرى في النقب والاعتراف بها..."

Everything else is readable for Bennett in Hebrew:
- He accepted the symmetric mourning in round 2.
- "Illegal outposts" and "no annexation in these four years" match his 23 Jun pledge and our cl. 3 and cl. 13 reading.
- "Disarm Hamas", "Jewish and democratic" and the Service Law go in his own remarks immediately afterwards.

Bennett signs the joint public statement plus the full framework, as a government party.
