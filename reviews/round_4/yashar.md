# Round 4: TV statement and signature check (Yashar! / Gadi Eisenkot)

## 1. Read on TV?
**YES IF one change is made.** Eisenkot reads in Hebrew.

The commitment "To make sure the war does not return / לוודא שהמלחמה לא תחזור" reads as a personal guarantee. A former Chief of Staff cannot give that guarantee while Hamas is not yet disarmed. It also conflicts with the security freedom of action in clause 15, and it gives Likud the clip "Eisenkot promised never to fight Hamas again". The framework itself says "act to… prevent its resumption" (clause 9).

Replace with:
- **HE:** «לפעול כדי שהמלחמה לא תחזור.»
- **EN:** "To work to ensure the war does not return."
- **AR (for the others):** «أن نعمل لضمان ألّا تعود الحرب.»

Every other line is acceptable to Yashar. "Jewish and democratic", Hamas as a terror organisation that is disarmed, and the Service Law will go in Eisenkot's own remarks straight after the joint reading.

## 2. Sign?
**(b): the joint public statement plus the full framework.** Eisenkot heads the government and is the party responsible for every chapter.

**Same page:** **Yes, if the signatures are grouped under role headings:**
- "Government" (Yashar, Together, Yisrael Beiteinu, Democrats);
- "Parliamentary agreement" (Ra'am);
- "Annex 1 declaration" (Hadash, Ta'al, Balad).

Grouping makes it visible that the Joint List and Balad are not partners. That matters for Eisenkot's "partners accept our rules" line and for his Zionist-government framing. He will not sign an undifferentiated list.

## 3. Net judgment
The statement can be read with one verb softened ("make sure" becomes "work to"), and the role-grouped signature page protects the "Zionist government" framing.
