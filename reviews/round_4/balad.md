# Round 4 — Balad (التجمع / בל"ד): TV statement and signature check

**Reader and signatory:** Bakr Awawdeh (بكر عواودة), head of Balad's Knesset delegation. He is Balad's top-ranked candidate on the Joint List (No. 4); his election is inferred from the seat count, not yet confirmed. Party chair Sami Abu Shehadeh is not an MK, because he was disqualified and withdrew. A stage next to Eisenkot, who backed his disqualification, would overshadow the night. Line to read: «أنا بكر عواودة، رئيس كتلة التجمع الوطني الديمقراطي». He reads in Arabic.

## 1. Read on TV? YES IF one change is made
The mourning line must name Gaza, as clause 7 already does. Without Gaza, a first-person condemnation of Hamas reads to our voters as a one-sided loyalty test. Gaza in clause 7 was Balad's round-1 condition for accepting the Hamas line.
- AR: «وأنعى كل حياة بريئة فُقدت منذ ذلك اليوم، إسرائيليين وفلسطينيين على حد سواء، بما في ذلك في غزة.»
- HE: «…ישראלים ופלסטינים כאחד, לרבות ברצועת עזה.»
- EN: "…Israelis and Palestinians alike, including in Gaza."

All nine parties already accepted this wording in clause 7. Everything else in the statement is acceptable in Arabic: "القتلى", "البؤر الاستيطانية غير القانونية", the Declaration cited for equality, and "some will not stand in its way".

## 2. Sign? Option (d): the joint statement plus Annex 1 only, with the Annex 2 reservation
The reservation: our note on clause 13 covers no annexation and no outpost money, not settlement construction. We do not endorse clauses 4, 6a or 9.

**Same page:** yes. Balad would sign next to Lieberman and Bennett. The statement's own premise is "standing beside people I argued with". We would prefer the signatures grouped by role (government / parliamentary agreement / Annex 1), not a separate page. If they refuse to share a page with us, that choice is theirs, not ours.

## 3. Net judgment
With Gaza named, Balad can say every line in Arabic on camera and stand beside its opponents.
