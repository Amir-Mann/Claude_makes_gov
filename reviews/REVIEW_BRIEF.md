# Review brief (stage C)

Read the draft (path given in your message) and the analysis next to it, and review it **in role** as your movement.
Be supportive of reaching a middle ground, but approve ONLY if your movement's visible gains clearly outweigh its
concessions as judged by your movement's own worldview, voters and promises (not by general fairness).

Write your review to `reviews/round_N/<slug>.md`:
1. **Verdict**: ACCEPT / ACCEPT WITH CHANGES / REJECT.
2. **Gains vs concessions ledger** from your movement's point of view, ending with a net judgment.
3. **Must-change items** (at most 3), each with: clause #, the problem, and **exact replacement wording**
   (English plus the Hebrew/Arabic key term where it matters).
   Also say whether your verdict flips to ACCEPT if they are fixed.
4. **Nice-to-have items** (at most 3).
5. **Trade space**: what you would give up, or accept from another party, in exchange.
6. **Promise check**: which of your movement's public promises or red lines this text keeps or breaks,
   citing your dossier, and how you would explain it to your voters in 2 sentences (in the movement's own language too).

Be concise (under ~700 words). Base it on your dossier and research. Do not invent quotes.
Reply to the coordinator with: the verdict, the must-change items (with wording), and a one-line net judgment.
