# Draft v3 — "Four Years of Repair": Framework of Principles, 2026–2030

> Working language: English; binding key terms in Hebrew (HE) and Arabic (AR).
> Government parties: Yashar · Together (Bennett, Yesh Atid) · Yisrael Beiteinu · The Democrats.
> Parliamentary agreement: Ra'am (Chapter E).
> Safety-net declaration: Hadash and Ta'al, with Balad able to join (Annex 1).
> Changes are explained in `reviews/round_1/consolidation.md` and `reviews/round_2/consolidation.md`.

---

## Preamble

1. This framework records principles agreed by parties with different worldviews **for one Knesset term (2026–2030)** only.
   Its purpose is to replace the outgoing government, restore the functioning of the state after 7 October, and repair what can be repaired in four years.
2. The government will be formed by Yashar, Together, Yisrael Beiteinu and The Democrats, headed by Gadi Eisenkot.
   It will rest on a parliamentary agreement with Ra'am (Chapter E).
   It will serve all citizens of Israel, Jews and Arabs alike.
   Matters not addressed here, such as religion and state, will be settled in the coalition agreement among the government parties.
3. The parties hold **different, sometimes opposite, positions on long-term questions**: the final status of the territories, the constitutional arrangement of the state, and the national aspirations of its citizens.
   This framework **does not decide these questions, does not prejudice them, and no party waives its positions on them** (HE: אינה מכריעה ואינה גורעת מעמדות הצדדים; AR: لا يحسم ولا يمسّ بمواقف الأطراف).
   During the term, no irreversible step will be taken in these matters.
   This does not prevent the government from protecting the independence of the judiciary, the Attorney General and the gatekeepers.
   Any change to the judicial system or constitutional process will be made only **with broad consent** (HE: בהסכמה רחבה; AR: بتوافق واسع).

## Chapter A — The state and its citizens

4. The State of Israel is defined in its Basic Laws as a **Jewish and democratic state** (HE: מדינה יהודית ודמוקרטית; AR: دولة يهودية وديمقراطية).
   The government will act within this framework and in the spirit of the **Declaration of Independence**, which promises "complete equality of social and political rights to all its inhabitants irrespective of religion, race or sex"
   (HE: שוויון זכויות חברתי ומדיני גמור לכל אזרחיה בלי הבדל דת, גזע ומין; AR: المساواة التامة في الحقوق الاجتماعية والسياسية لجميع مواطنيها دون تمييز في الدين أو العرق أو الجنس).
   *This clause describes the existing law.*
   No party is taken thereby to endorse, or to renounce its position on, any particular Basic Law, including Basic Law: Israel – the Nation-State of the Jewish People (HE: חוק־יסוד: הלאום; AR: قانون القومية).
   Each party keeps its freedom to act lawfully on it.
5. The Arab citizens of Israel (HE: האזרחים הערבים של ישראל; AR: المواطنون العرب في إسرائيل) are equal citizens.
   Their elected representatives are a legitimate part of Israeli democracy, and the government will act **for them and with them** as for all citizens.
6. During the term the government will not initiate legislation that harms the civil equality of any group of citizens, nor condition civil rights on political views.
   Government services will be fully available in Arabic.
6a. **Service for All.** Within its first year the government will enact a Service Law for All (HE: חוק שירות לכולם; AR: قانون الخدمة للجميع), based on equal burden (HE: שוויון בנטל):
   - every citizen serves the state, in the IDF or in civil-national service;
   - no exemption quotas and no blanket exemptions;
   - state benefits are tied to service: whoever serves is rewarded (HE: מי שמשרת – מתוגמל).
   The duty applies to all; implementation will be phased by track.
   For Arab citizens, during this term, service will be **civilian, in their own communities, run with the Arab local authorities, with no security link**.
   It will expand by published annual targets, through **incentives and equal benefits, not sanctions** (AR: بالحوافز والمساواة في الامتيازات، لا بالعقوبات).
   On this civil-national track, **no citizen will lose or have reduced any existing right, allowance or benefit for not serving**; service adds benefits and does not condition existing ones (HE: אף אזרח לא ייפגע בזכות, בקצבה או בהטבה קיימת בשל אי־שירות; AR: لا يُحرم أي مواطن من حق أو مخصص أو امتياز قائم بسبب عدم الخدمة).

## Chapter B — Terrorism and the security of all

7. The parties **condemn all terrorism and all deliberate harm to innocent civilians**, whoever the perpetrator and whoever the victim (HE: כל טרור וכל פגיעה מכוונת באזרחים חפים מפשע; AR: كل إرهاب وكل مسّ متعمّد بالمدنيين الأبرياء).
   They mourn all innocent civilians who lost their lives since 7 October, Israelis and Palestinians alike, including in Gaza (HE: ישראלים ופלסטינים כאחד, לרבות ברצועת עזה; AR: الإسرائيليين والفلسطينيين على حد سواء، بما في ذلك في غزة).
8. The parties condemn without reservation the **massacre carried out by Hamas on 7 October 2023**: the murder, rape and abduction of civilians (HE: הטבח שביצע חמאס; AR: المجزرة التي ارتكبتها حماس).
9. **Hamas, a terrorist organization under Israeli law** (HE: חמאס, ארגון טרור על פי החוק הישראלי; AR: حماس، المصنّفة منظمة إرهابية وفق القانون الإسرائيلي), will be **disarmed and will not rule Gaza** or threaten the citizens of Israel (HE: פירוק חמאס מנשקו; AR: نزع سلاح حماس).
   The government will act to that end in coordination with the international framework for Gaza.
   It will also act to consolidate the end of the war and prevent its resumption, to return any remaining hostages and the bodies of the fallen, and to enable humanitarian aid and reconstruction for the civilian population in accordance with international law.
10. **Jewish terror.** The government will fight Jewish terror (HE: טרור יהודי; AR: الإرهاب اليهودي) and nationalist violence against Palestinians and their property **with the same determination and the same tools used against Palestinian terror**.
    These include **administrative detention under law and judicial review** (HE: מעצרים מנהליים; AR: الاعتقال الإداري), a dedicated Shin Bet–police unit, dismantling of illegal violent hubs, and prosecution.
    This violence is perpetrated by a small extremist minority that harms Palestinians, the residents of the area and the State of Israel itself.
11. At its first meeting, the government will establish a **state commission of inquiry** into the events of 7 October, the decade preceding them and the conduct of the war.

## Chapter C — The territories under military control

12. Judea and Samaria / the West Bank (HE: יהודה ושומרון (הגדה המערבית); AR: الضفة الغربية (يهودا والسامرة في القانون الإسرائيلي)) are territories held under **military control** since 1967 (HE: שטחים בשליטה צבאית; AR: أراضٍ تحت سيطرة عسكرية).
13. During the term there will be **no annexation, application of sovereignty or change in the legal status** of the territories.
    The powers transferred from the military commander to political appointees will be returned to the IDF and the Civil Administration.
    In existing communities, planning and construction will continue only in accordance with the law and government decisions (HE: יימשכו על פי חוק והחלטות ממשלה).
    The state budgets will include **no allocations to unauthorized outposts and no coalition funds**.
14. Outposts and "farms" established without a government decision and without legal authorization, first and foremost on private Palestinian land (HE: מאחזים בלתי מורשים; AR: البؤر الاستيطانية غير القانونية), **will be evacuated** according to a timetable published within 90 days.
    **No unauthorized outpost will be legalized during the term** (HE: לא יוכשר במהלך הקדנציה אף מאחז בלתי מורשה; AR: لن تُشرعَن أي بؤرة استيطانية خلال الولاية).
    Only regularization steps already decided by a government decision before 27 October 2026 may proceed, under law and judicial review, and none on private Palestinian land.
    Progress on the evacuation timetable will be published quarterly.
15. Israel retains full security responsibility and **freedom of operational action against terrorism** throughout the area (HE: חופש פעולה ביטחוני מלא; AR: حرية العمل الأمني).
    The government will maintain security and civil coordination with the Palestinian Authority and work with regional and international partners to stabilize the area.
    In accordance with clause 3, no final-status decision will be taken during the term.
    The parties' positions on a future settlement between Israel and the Palestinian people (HE: העם הפלסטיני; AR: الشعب الفلسطيني) are fully reserved.
15a. The government will preserve the historic **status quo at the holy places** in Jerusalem, including the Temple Mount / al-Haram al-Sharif (HE: הר הבית / אל-אקצא; AR: المسجد الأقصى / الحرم القدسي الشريف).

## Chapter D — A government that acts for Arab citizens (four-year commitments)

> Chapter D is a commitment of the government to all Arab citizens, not a price for votes.

16. **National emergency plan against crime and violence** in Arab society: at least **NIS 1 billion per year**, multi-year and protected from cuts.
    It covers policing, prevention and enforcement against criminal organizations, with the involvement of all relevant state bodies, including the Shin Bet against criminal organizations, under law and judicial oversight.
    It will be run with the Arab local authorities, led by the Deputy Minister named in clause 22, with a published annual report on homicides and solved cases.
17. **A successor five-year economic plan** to Government Decision 550, **no smaller in real terms** (≈ NIS 30 billion), approved in the first budget, including restored grants to Arab local authorities.
    It will set published targets for:
    - employment;
    - Hebrew-language and higher education;
    - placement of Arab teachers;
    - medical residency slots;
    - a **youth plan** for housing, higher education and jobs.
18. **Planning and housing**: master plans for Arab localities on a published timetable, and expansion of jurisdiction.
    Enforcement of the Kaminitz Law against **existing inhabited homes** is frozen immediately.
    Within 6 months an amendment will cancel the administrative fines and administrative demolition orders against them, pending approved plans, including in the Triangle and the Negev.
    Enforcement against **new** illegal construction (built after the government is sworn in) and against criminal organizations will continue.
19. **The Negev**: a recognition and regularization process for unrecognized villages.
    Recognition decisions on a first group will be taken within 18 months, in agreement with residents, with connection to electricity and water.
    During the process there will be **no demolition of inhabited homes and no forced relocation** in these villages (HE: ללא הריסת בתי מגורים וללא העתקה כפויה; AR: دون هدم بيوت مأهولة ودون تهجير قسري).
20. **Representation**:
    - targets for Arab representation in the civil service and on the boards of public companies;
    - a deputy Speaker from each of the two Arab lists, Ra'am and the Joint List (HE: סגן יו"ר הכנסת מכל אחת מהרשימות הערביות; AR: نائب لرئيس الكنيست من كل من القائمتين العربيتين);
    - the chair of at least one Knesset committee.

## Chapter E — Parliamentary agreement with Ra'am

21. Ra'am will vote in favor of the government's investiture and its budgets, and will not support no-confidence motions or Knesset-dissolution bills.
    Ra'am will vote in favor of the Service Law for All as set out in clause 6a, including its civil-national track for Arab citizens and the protection of existing rights.
    Otherwise it keeps freedom of vote.
22. Ra'am's nominee, with the approval of the Prime Minister, will serve as **Deputy Minister for the fight against crime and violence in Arab society**.
    The post sits in the ministry responsible for policing, under the responsible minister and the government's policy, outside the cabinet and without a vote in the security cabinet (HE: סגן שר בכפיפות לשר הממונה, מחוץ לממשלה ולקבינט; AR: نائب وزير تابع للوزير المسؤول، خارج الحكومة والكابينت).
    Ra'am will also chair one Knesset committee.
23. The parties will support the election of a new Knesset Speaker in the first week of the Knesset.
24. A **monitoring committee** will meet monthly and publish a quarterly report on Chapter D.
    It is made up of the government, Ra'am and representatives of the National Committee of Arab Local Authority Heads.
    Representatives of the factions that made the declaration in Annex 1 take part as observers and receive its reports.
25. Disputes will be brought to the monitoring committee.
    If a material breach of Chapter D is not remedied within 60 days, Ra'am is released from clause 21.

## Annex 1 — Safety-net declaration (unilateral; Hadash and Ta'al; Balad may join)

> The undersigned factions of the Joint List welcome the replacement of the outgoing government and the commitments of Chapter D.
> They note in particular clauses 7, 10, 12–14, 15a and 16–20, and the reference to the Palestinian people in clause 15.
> They will not support motions of no confidence or Knesset dissolution against this government while Chapter D is being implemented.
> On every other vote they keep full freedom, including on defense and identity matters.
> Their declaration does not constitute endorsement of any clause not listed above, including clauses 4 and 6a, nor of any Basic Law (AR: لا يشكّل هذا الإعلان تأييداً لأي بند غير مذكور أعلاه، ولا لأي قانون أساس).
> The government is not a party to this declaration.
