### Title
Four years of reform [إصلاح]: the broad guidelines of the government and an open letter, 2026–2030

**The situation at the close of negotiations, 29 October 2026: not agreed.**

This is the text as it settled after three rounds. Seven of the nine parties can coexist with it:
- Yashar, Yesh Atid and the Democrats are ready to sign Part A.
- The United Arab List will vote in favour of the government.
- Hadash, Ta'al and Balad will let it pass.

As for the Bennett party and Yisrael Beiteinu, they reject any government whose winning of confidence depends on the votes of the Arabs [أصوات العرب] or on their abstention from voting. And without their 14 seats the change camp is left with 41 seats, and therefore this government cannot be formed.

And there still remains among the seven parties one outstanding point: the membership of the implementation-monitoring committee (Part B, clause 6).

The only part that all nine parties agreed to is Part C, "Knesset First", which is bills that each bloc votes on separately.

The Hebrew text is the original, and the English and Arabic texts are based on it.

### Part A. The broad guidelines of the government

Whereas the elections of the 26th Knesset did not produce a majority for a government headed by Benjamin Netanyahu;

And whereas the undersigned blocs have different, and sometimes contradictory, positions on questions of principle, among them the final status of the West Bank (Judea and Samaria), the constitutional order of the state, and the national aspirations of its citizens;

And whereas the signatories wish to form a government for one Knesset term, that will return governance to its sound course after the seventh of October and repair what can be repaired within four years;

Therefore the following was agreed:

1. A government shall be formed headed by MK Gadi Eisenkot, in which the blocs Yashar, Together, Yisrael Beiteinu and the Democrats take part. It is a Zionist and statist [دولانيّة] government, serving all citizens of Israel, Jews and Arabs alike. And every citizen's vote has the same weight.

   *The Bennett party and Yisrael Beiteinu reject Part A (see the status statement at the head of the document).*

2. These broad guidelines do not decide any question of principle, and no party waives its position on them. And the government will not take during its term any irreversible step on these questions. As for the matters not regulated here, among them matters of religion and state, they will be regulated in the coalition agreements.

**The State and its citizens**

3. The government parties affirm [تؤكد] that the State of Israel is a Jewish and democratic state, as defined in the Basic Laws. And the government will work in the spirit of the Declaration of Independence, which promises "complete equality of social and political rights to all its inhabitants [سكانها], without distinction of religion, race or sex". And this clause does not bind any party to a position on Basic Law: Israel – the Nation State of the Jewish People, and every party may act regarding it according to law.

4. The Arab citizens of Israel are citizens with equal rights, and their elected representatives are a legitimate part of Israeli democracy. And the government will not initiate legislation that widens the grounds for disqualifying lists and candidates from running in the Knesset elections.

5. The government will not initiate legislation that harms the civil equality of any group of citizens, and will not make civil rights conditional on political views. And government services shall be available in the Arabic language in full.

6. Service for all. The government shall enact during its first year a law based on equality in bearing the burden:
   - a. Every young man and young woman liable for it under the Security Service Law shall perform service, in the Israeli army or in national-civil service. No exemption quotas will be set and no blanket exemptions granted. Personal economic sanctions will be imposed on the evaders starting from the dates set by the law. And state funding for educational institutions will be conditional on teaching the core subjects.
   - b. The privileges connected to service will be granted to whoever serves, whoever they are. And no existing general right or allowance will be made conditional on service.
   - c. As for the citizens not liable for service under the Security Service Law, national-civil service for them in this term will be a voluntary civil service inside their communities, run in cooperation with the local authorities. And the law does not change the existing call-up arrangements.
   - d. If the law is not enacted within a year, the government will decide on budgetary measures against institutions whose students evade service, and will decide regarding the stopping [وقف] of the privileges connected to service.

7. The government will work to push forward [ستعمل على الدفع قدمًا] Basic Law: Legislation, by broad consensus, and a law limiting the term of the Prime Minister. And a public committee will review the laws on the judicial system that the 25th Knesset enacted, provided that it submits its report within a year. And until the government decides on the committee's report, the powers that those laws created will not be used. And the return to the arrangements that preceded those laws is not considered a change requiring broad consensus.

**Security and the fight against terrorism**

8. The government parties condemn all terrorism and every deliberate attack on innocent civilians, whoever the attacker and whoever the victim. And they express their grief for every innocent civilian killed since the seventh of October, Israelis and Palestinians alike, including in the Gaza Strip.

9. The government parties condemn without reservation the massacre that the Hamas movement committed on 7 October 2023: the killing, rape and abduction of civilians.

10. The Hamas movement is a terrorist organization. Its military capabilities and its capacity to govern will be dismantled, and it will not rule the Gaza Strip and will not threaten the citizens of Israel. And Israel retains security freedom of action, in coordination with the international framework for Gaza. And the government will work to consolidate the end of the war, to return the remaining abductees [المخطوفين] and the bodies of the dead [جثامين القتلى], and to enable humanitarian aid and reconstruction for the civilian population.

11. Jewish terrorism. The government fights Jewish terrorism and nationalist violence against Palestinians and their property with the same firmness with which the state fights all terrorism. And among its tools in this are administrative detention according to law and under judicial supervision, a joint Shabak–police unit, re-establishing the police district in the West Bank (the Judea and Samaria district), dismantling violence hubs established without a permit, and filing indictments. This violence is the work of a small violent minority [أقلية صغيرة عنيفة], and it harms the Palestinians, the residents of the area and the State of Israel itself.

12. The government establishes, within 14 days of its swearing-in, a state commission of inquiry into the events of the seventh of October, the decade that preceded them, and the conduct of the war.

**The West Bank (Judea and Samaria)**

13. In the West Bank (Judea and Samaria), subject to the authority of the military commander since 1967, the government will act during its term as follows:
   - a. No annexation and no imposition of sovereignty, and no change in the legal status.
   - b. The powers transferred from the military commander to political appointees will be returned to the Israeli army and the Civil Administration.
   - c. No new settlements [مستوطنات] will be established. And construction in the existing settlements [المستوطنات] will take place within their approved plans and by decision of the government.
   - d. The state budget will not include any allocations for illegal [غير القانونية] settlement outposts, nor coalition money for them.

14. The settlement outposts and "farms" established without a government decision and without legal permission will be evacuated, starting with those built on private Palestinian land. A timetable will be published within 90 days, and a report on the progress of implementation will be submitted every quarter. And no illegal settlement outpost will be legalized [تُشرعَن] during the term, except the legalization steps the government decided on before 27 October 2026. These steps continue according to law and under judicial supervision, provided that none of them is on private Palestinian land.

15. Israel retains full security responsibility and full security freedom of action in the whole area. And the government maintains security and civil coordination with the Palestinian Authority, and works with regional and international partners for stability. And in accordance with clause 2, no decision on the permanent settlement will be taken during the term, and the parties' positions on it remain fully reserved.

16. The government preserves the historic status quo in the holy places in Jerusalem, including in the Blessed al-Aqsa Mosque / al-Haram al-Sharif [المسجد الأقصى المبارك / الحرم القدسي الشريف].

17. The commitments contained in the open letter of the Prime Minister-designate (Part B) will be adopted as a government decision at the government's first meeting, and funded in the first state budget.

### Part B. Open letter from the Prime Minister-designate to the National Committee of Heads of Arab Local Authorities

*Copy to the Chairman of the High Follow-Up Committee for the Arab Masses. Published before the confidence vote, and attached to the broad guidelines of the government.*

Honourable heads of the Arab local authorities,

The government that I am working to form will work for all citizens of Israel. And I present to you here, publicly, what it will do for Arab society during the next four years. These are commitments toward the citizens and toward the local leaderships they elected, and are not a price for votes. And I ask you to hold us to account for them every three months.

1. Crime and violence.
   - A national plan of no less than one billion shekels annually, entered into the budget base for four years.
   - The plan includes police work, prevention and law enforcement on crime organizations. And this includes the Shabak, provided that it works according to law and under judicial supervision, and against these organizations alone.
   - The plan is run by a government coordinator for combating crime and violence in Arab society, appointed by government decision for the whole term. The minister responsible for the police delegates to him written powers over the plan's budget, and only the government has the power to dismiss him.
   - The government intends to appoint Yoav Segalovitz to this post. And before he takes it up he will resign from the Knesset and declare publicly, in his own name, his acceptance of the principles of the government's broad guidelines, among them clauses 3 and 10.
   - And the government publishes every year the number of murders, and the proportion of cases solved and in which indictments were filed.

2. The economic plan.
   - A five-year plan succeeding Government Decision 550, not less than it in real value (about 30 billion shekels), and approved in the first budget.
   - Restoring the grants that were cut from the Arab local authorities.
   - Announced targets in employment, Hebrew-language teaching and higher education, and in absorbing Arab teachers and medical specialization posts, and in housing and job opportunities for young people.

3. Planning and housing.
   - Outline plans for Arab towns according to an announced timetable, and expansion of jurisdiction areas.
   - From the day the government takes the oath, the application of the Kaminitz Law (Amendment 116 to the Planning and Building Law) to existing inhabited homes is frozen.
   - Within six months an amendment is enacted that suspends the administrative fines and demolition orders issued against these homes until the plans are approved, and opens a track to regularize their status. And this includes the Triangle and the Negev, among them Tayibe, Tira, Qalansawe and the Negev towns.
   - And enforcement of the law on new construction and on crime organizations continues.

4. The Negev.
   - A track of recognition and regularization for the unrecognized villages, in which recognition decisions for a first group of them are taken within 18 months, with their connection to the electricity and water networks.
   - Throughout this track no inhabited home will be demolished. And where relocation is necessary, it will only be within a planning scheme reached by agreement with the residents and their elected representatives.

5. Representation.
   - Targets for the representation of Arabs in the state service and on the boards of directors of government companies.
   - The Knesset presidium and its committees are formed according to the followed custom and the size of each bloc. And the government parties will not obstruct the election of a deputy Speaker of the Knesset from any bloc whatsoever.

6. Monitoring implementation.
   - The implementation-monitoring committee, headed by the coordinator, meets once a month and publishes a report every three months.
   - Its members are representatives of the government and of the National Committee of Heads of Arab Local Authorities.
   - Every bloc in the Knesset that responds publicly to this letter names one MK to the committee. **[Not agreed. First wording: "as a member equal in standing", demanded by the United [List], Hadash and Ta'al, and accepted by Yesh Atid and the Democrats. Second wording: "receives its reports and is entitled to direct questions to it", demanded by Yashar and Yisrael Beiteinu.]**

7. The holy places and the path ahead.
   - The government preserves the historic status quo in the holy places in Jerusalem, including the Blessed al-Aqsa Mosque / al-Haram al-Sharif.
   - And it maintains coordination with the Palestinian Authority, and will not take any step that prevents a future settlement between Israel and the Palestinians, and will respond to regional and international efforts for stability and settlement.

### Part D. Responses of the Arab blocs (unilateral, and the government is not a party to them)

**The United Arab List**
- The United [List] votes in favour of granting confidence to the government, and in favour of the budgets that fund the commitments of Part B toward Arab society.
- It withdraws its support if the status quo in the Blessed al-Aqsa Mosque is touched, or if Part B is breached and the breach is not remedied within 60 days.
- It retains freedom of vote on questions of religion and state, conscience and security, and does not adopt clauses 3 and 10 of Part A.
- And it considers not appointing the coordinator, or dismissing him without cause [دون سبب], a material breach of Part B.

**The Joint List (Hadash, Ta'al, Balad)**
- "We did not vote for this government." Hadash and Ta'al abstain on the confidence vote, and Balad's MKs absent themselves.
- As long as Part B is being implemented, the List remains a safety net for our people, and does not support no-confidence motions or bills to dissolve the Knesset.
- The safety net falls if Part B is breached, or the status quo in the Blessed al-Aqsa Mosque is breached, and the breach is not remedied within 60 days.
- Balad will not absent itself from the budget vote unless it is free of any money for annexation or for illegal settlement outposts [للبؤر الاستيطانية غير القانونية], and funds Part B; otherwise its MKs vote against it.
- Each component of the List holds to its positions:
  - Hadash to its positions on the occupation, the illegality of the settlements, and the right of the Palestinian people to self-determination and an independent state on the 1967 borders;
  - Ta'al to its position on the occupation, the illegitimacy [عدم شرعية] of the settlements, the right of the Palestinian people to an independent state on the 1967 borders with East Jerusalem as its capital, and rejection of administrative detention;
  - Balad to its position on the occupation and the settlements;
  - and the three components to their rejection of military and civil service in all its forms.

## B. tv_statement.md, Arabic blocks

### (a) Arabic translation of the government-party leaders' statement

Good evening.

We stand here together. We disagreed, and we will disagree tomorrow too.
But Israel tonight gets a new government.
A Zionist and statist government. The government of a Jewish and democratic state, in the spirit of the Declaration of Independence.
A government for all citizens of Israel. One hundred percent. Jews and Arabs alike. Every citizen's vote has the same weight.

Within fourteen days: a state [رسمية, lit. "official"] commission of inquiry. Into the seventh of October, and into the years that preceded it, and into the war.
We will not evade responsibility. Not even our own responsibility.

Hamas is a terrorist organization. Its military capabilities and its capacity to govern will be dismantled, and it will not rule Gaza.
And we will return home every abductee [مخطوف], and every person killed [قتيل] whose body has not yet been returned.

The era of evading conscription is over. Whoever is obliged to serve will serve, in the army or in civil service. And whoever serves will be rewarded.

The Arab citizens in Israel are equal citizens. We will fight the crime that kills them almost every day. This is not their problem. It is the state's problem.

And in Judea and Samaria (the West Bank) the law will prevail. We will fight Jewish terrorism, that violent handful that harms the innocent and harms us too. We will evacuate the illegal settlement outposts [البؤر الاستيطانية غير القانونية], and we will not proceed to annexation.

We do not ask you to believe us. Test us. Every three months, and in numbers.

Good luck to us all.
