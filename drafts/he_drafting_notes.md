# Hebrew drafting notes: final/statement_he.md (run 2)

Source: `final/statement_en.md`. Model wording comes from `research/STYLE_GUIDE.md`, and the locked terms from `reviews/run2/round_3/*.md` §3. The run-1 Hebrew was used only to see what to avoid.

## 1. Register choices

- **Part A follows the architecture of a real agreement.**
  - A preamble of "הואיל ו… / והואיל ו… / לפיכך הוסכם בין הצדדים כדלהלן:".
  - Clause 1 reads "תוקם ממשלה בראשות ח"כ…".
  - Headings are bare topics with no "פרק X —".
  - Sub-items are lettered "א.–ד.".
  - There is no bold inside clauses and no em dash. En dashes appear only in part headings (the attested "נספח א' – …" pattern), in the Nation-State Law's title and in statute years.
- **Clause length varies on purpose.** Clauses 9, 12 and 16 are one sentence each; clauses 11 and 14 run to a paragraph.
- **Formulas.**
  - Verbs: "הממשלה תפעל ל…", "תקדם", "תביא לחקיקת".
  - Connectives: "ובכלל זה", "לרבות", "בהתאם לסעיף 2", "על פי דין", "בכפוף לביקורת שיפוטית", "אין בסעיף זה כדי…", "אין בהם משום ויתור".
  - Conditional: "לא נחקק החוק בתוך שנה, תחליט…" (6ד).
- **Money.** Figures use "₪". Floors are "לא יפחת מ…", and budget protection is "שתוכנס לבסיס התקציב".
- **Legal names.**
  - חוק שירות ביטחון [נוסח משולב], התשמ"ו–1986: the full official title. I added "[נוסח משולב]" because that is the correct citation.
  - חוק ועדות חקירה, התשכ"ט–1968.
  - Other terms: חוק יסוד: החקיקה; תיקון 116 לחוק התכנון והבנייה ("חוק קמיניץ"); המפקד הצבאי; המנהל האזרחי; מחוז ש"י; שב"כ; מעצר מנהלי; מאחזים בלתי מורשים.
  - "הסדרה" is used for regularization and "פינוי" for evacuation.
- **Part B is a real letter.**
  - It opens with "לכבוד / יו"ר הוועד הארצי…", then "העתק: יו"ר ועדת המעקב העליונה" and "שלום רב,".
  - It is written in the first person in the opening paragraph only ("הממשלה בראשותי"). The items keep the crisp nominal style of Israeli government plans.
  - It signs off "בכבוד רב, / גדי איזנקוט, ח"כ / המועמד להרכבת הממשלה". The line breaks use `<br>` because the builder strips trailing spaces.
  - Warmth comes from short sentences ("אין זה מחיר תמורת קולות.") and "לדרוש מאיתנו דין וחשבון", not from added content.
- **Double names.** Only the two attested ones are used: "יהודה ושומרון (הגדה המערבית)" (heading) and "הר הבית (אל-אקצא)".

## 2. Locked wording: conflicts and how they were resolved

| Issue | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Arab citizens.** "אזרחי ישראל הערבים" (Bennett, Democrats, Yisrael Beiteinu) or "האזרחים הערבים של ישראל" (Yashar, Yesh Atid, Ra'am) | **"אזרחי ישראל הערבים"** (Part A, clause 4) | Same meaning. The "X של ישראל" form is an English genitive (STYLE_GUIDE H-9). The construct form is how a Hebrew legal text says it, and it also avoids the forbidden "המגזר" and "מיעוטים". **Yashar, Yesh Atid and Ra'am should be told.** |
| **Al-Aqsa.** Part B 7 and Part D read "al-Aqsa" in the English, but Yisrael Beiteinu requires "הר הבית" in Hebrew (not "אל-אקצא" alone) | Part B 7 and Ra'am's reply: **"הר הבית (אל-אקצא)"**. Joint List reply: **"אל-אקצא"** alone | Part B is the government's text, so YB's lock applies, and Ra'am's own Hebrew lock is "הר הבית (אל-אקצא)". Part D is the Joint List's unilateral statement, to which YB is not a party, so their own term stands. **Flag:** YB could still object to "אל-אקצא" alone appearing anywhere in the Hebrew document. |
| **Exemptions.** Yashar: "אין פטור גורף"; Yesh Atid: "אין פטורים גורפים" | "אין מכסות פטור ואין פטורים גורפים" | The English has the plural "no blanket exemptions". Same meaning. |
| **Commission deadline.** Yashar: "תוך 14 יום" | "בתוך 14 ימים מיום השבעתה" | Same meaning, written in legal register. |
| **Kaminitz.** Yesh Atid: "תיקון 116 … ("חוק קמיניץ")"; Yashar: "חוק קמיניץ (תיקון 116)" | Yesh Atid's form at first mention (Part B 3), then "חוק קמיניץ" (Part C) | This is citation order only. The wording keeps "תוקפא" (freeze), never "בוטל" (repealed), as Bennett requires. |
| **Attribution.** Ta'al requires that "the government parties condemn / affirm" | "סיעות הממשלה" in clauses 3, 8 and 9, not "הצדדים" | "הצדדים" is more idiomatic in an agreement, but it could be read as covering all nine parties. |

## 4. Points where I am not fully sure the meaning is identical

1. **"Arab votes"** is rendered as the Arab parties' votes (see §3).
2. **A7** keeps the English ambiguity on purpose: "בהסכמה רחבה" sits right after Basic Law: Legislation. A reader could still take it to cover the term-limit law as well, just as in the English.
3. **A6ד, "השעיית ההטבות הקשורות בשירות".** The English does not say whose benefits are suspended, and neither does the Hebrew.
4. **The Public Security / National Security ministry name** (see §3).
5. **B6 bracket.** The text is kept visibly unagreed, in bold brackets. The Ra'am, Hadash and Ta'al option is phrased "שיכהן בה כחבר במעמד שווה" so that it reads grammatically after "חבר כנסת אחד".
