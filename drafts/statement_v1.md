# Draft v1 — "Four Years of Repair": Joint Framework of Principles, 2026–2030

> Working language: English, with the binding key terms given in Hebrew (HE) and Arabic (AR).
> Signatories (8): Yashar · Together (Bennett, Yesh Atid) · Yisrael Beiteinu · The Democrats · Ra'am · Hadash · Ta'al.
> Balad is **not** a signatory (see analysis_v1.md §2). Its MKs remain free to abstain or support individual votes.

---

## Preamble — what this document is, and what it is not

1. This document records the principles agreed by parties with different worldviews for **one Knesset term (2026–2030)** only.
   Its purpose is to replace the outgoing government, restore the functioning of the state after 7 October, and repair what can be repaired within four years.
2. The **government** will be formed by the Zionist parties that sign this document (Yashar, Together, Yisrael Beiteinu, The Democrats), headed by Gadi Eisenkot.
   Ra'am, Hadash and Ta'al sign a **parliamentary agreement** with the government (HE: הסכם פרלמנטרי; AR: اتفاق برلماني), on the model of 1992, as set out in Chapter E.
3. The signatories hold **different, sometimes opposite, positions on long-term questions**: the final status of the territories, the constitutional arrangement of the state, and the national aspirations of its citizens.
   This document **does not decide these questions, does not prejudice them, and no signatory waives its positions on them** (HE: אינו מכריע ואינו גורע מעמדות הצדדים; AR: لا يحسم ولا يمسّ بمواقف الأطراف).
   During the term, the government will not take steps that are irreversible in these matters.

## Chapter A — The state and its citizens

4. **The existing constitutional framework.** The State of Israel is defined in its Basic Laws as a **Jewish and democratic state** (HE: מדינה יהודית ודמוקרטית; AR: دولة يهودية وديمقراطية).
   The government will act within this framework and in the spirit of the **Declaration of Independence**, which promises "complete equality of social and political rights to all its inhabitants irrespective of religion, race or sex"
   (HE: שוויון זכויות חברתי ומדיני גמור לכל אזרחיה בלי הבדל דת, גזע ומין; AR: المساواة التامة في الحقوق الاجتماعية والسياسية لجميع مواطنيها دون تمييز في الدين أو العرق أو الجنس).
5. **Arab citizens.** The Arab citizens of Israel (HE: האזרחים הערבים של ישראל; AR: المواطنون العرب في إسرائيل) are equal citizens.
   Their elected representatives are a **legitimate part of Israeli democracy**, and the government will act **for them and with them** as it acts for all citizens.
6. During the term, the government will not initiate legislation that harms the civil equality of any group of citizens, and will not condition civil rights on political views.

## Chapter B — Terrorism and the security of all

7. The signatories **condemn all terrorism and all deliberate harm to innocent civilians**, whoever the perpetrator and whoever the victim
   (HE: כל טרור וכל פגיעה מכוונת באזרחים חפים מפשע; AR: كل إرهاب وكل مسّ متعمّد بالمدنيين الأبرياء).
8. The signatories condemn without reservation the **massacre carried out by Hamas on 7 October 2023**: the murder, rape and abduction of civilians.
   These were acts of terror (HE: הטבח שביצע חמאס; AR: المجزرة التي ارتكبتها حماس).
9. The government will act to ensure that **Hamas does not rule Gaza and cannot threaten the citizens of Israel**, in coordination with the international framework for Gaza.
   It will also act to allow humanitarian aid and reconstruction for the civilian population, in accordance with international law.
10. **Jewish terror.** The government will fight with full force **Jewish terror** (HE: טרור יהודי; AR: الإرهاب اليهودي) and nationalist violence against Palestinians and their property.
    These are perpetrated by an extremist minority that harms Palestinians, the residents of the area and the State of Israel itself.
    It will use all legal tools: a dedicated investigative unit, prosecution, and administrative orders under law and judicial review.
11. The government will establish, within 14 days, a **state commission of inquiry** into the events of 7 October and the policy that preceded them.

## Chapter C — The territories under military control

12. **The existing situation.** Judea and Samaria / the West Bank (HE: יהודה ושומרון / הגדה המערבית; AR: الضفة الغربية / يهودا والسامرة) are territories held under **military control** since 1967 (HE: שטחים בשליטה צבאית; AR: أراضٍ تحت سيطرة عسكرية).
13. **Status quo.** During the term there will be **no annexation, application of sovereignty or change in the legal status** of the territories.
    The powers transferred from the military commander to political appointees will be **returned to the IDF and the Civil Administration**.
14. **Illegal outposts.** Outposts and "farms" established without a government decision and without legal authorization, first and foremost on private Palestinian land
    (HE: מאחזים בלתי מורשים / בלתי חוקיים; AR: البؤر الاستيطانية غير القانونية), **will be evacuated** according to a timetable published within 90 days.
    No outpost will be retroactively legalized during the term.
15. The government will maintain security coordination and work with regional and international partners to stabilize the area and reduce violence.
    It will not take steps that close off future options.

## Chapter D — A government that acts for Arab citizens (four-year commitments)

16. **National emergency plan against crime and violence** in Arab society: at least **NIS 1 billion per year**, multi-year and protected from cuts.
    It covers policing, prevention and enforcement against criminal organizations, run with the Arab local authorities, with a lead minister and a published annual report on homicides and solved cases.
17. **A successor five-year economic plan** to Government Decision 550, **no smaller in real terms than its predecessor** (≈ NIS 30 billion), approved in the first budget.
    It will include restoring grants to Arab local authorities that were cut.
18. **Planning and housing**: master plans for Arab localities on a published timetable, and expansion of local-authority jurisdiction.
    Enforcement of the Kaminitz Law will be **frozen pending an amendment** to be legislated within 6 months.
    Demolitions of residential homes in localities where a plan is in preparation will be frozen.
19. **The Negev**: a recognition and regularization process for unrecognized villages, with recognition decisions on a first group within 18 months, in agreement with residents, and connection to electricity and water.
20. **Representation**: targets for Arab representation in the civil service and on boards of public companies; an Arab deputy Speaker; the chair of at least one Knesset committee.
21. **Service**: the government will expand voluntary civil service in Arab society, run with local authorities and with no security link, with equal benefits.

## Chapter E — The parliamentary agreement

22. **Ra'am** will vote in favor of the government's investiture and its budgets.
    **Hadash and Ta'al** will vote in favor of the investiture and will at minimum abstain on the budget laws.
    None of the three will support a motion of no confidence or a Knesset-dissolution bill, for as long as Chapters A–D are being implemented.
23. Apart from these votes, the parties to the parliamentary agreement keep **full freedom of vote**, including on defense, settlement and identity matters.
    They are not bound by coalition discipline and hold no cabinet posts.
24. A **joint monitoring committee** (government and parties to the agreement) will meet monthly and publish a quarterly implementation report on Chapter D.
25. Breach of Chapter D releases the parties to the agreement from Chapter E.
