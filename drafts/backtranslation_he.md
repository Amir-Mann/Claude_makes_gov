### Title
Four years of repair [תיקון]: basic guidelines and an open letter, 2026–2030

**State of affairs at the end of the negotiations, 29 October 2026: no agreement was reached.**

This is the text as it stands after three rounds. Seven of the nine movements can live with it:
- Yashar, Yesh Atid and the Democrats will sign Part A.
- Ra'am will vote for the government.
- Hadash, Ta'al and Balad will allow it to be formed [יאפשרו לה לקום].

The Bennett party and Yisrael Beiteinu reject any government whose formation depends on the votes of the Arab parties [קולות המפלגות הערביות] or on their abstention. Without their 14 seats, the change bloc is left with 41, and therefore this government will not be able to be formed.

Among the seven, too, one question remains open: the composition of the monitoring committee [ועדת הבקרה] (Part B, clause 6).

The only part that all nine movements agreed on is Part C, the "Knesset First" bills, on which each faction votes separately.

### Part A – The basic guidelines of the government's policy

Whereas the elections to the 26th Knesset did not confer a majority on a government headed by Benjamin Netanyahu;

And whereas the undersigned factions hold different, and at times opposing, positions on questions of principle, among them the final status of Judea and Samaria [יהודה ושומרון], the constitutional order of the state and the national aspirations of its citizens;

And whereas the undersigned seek to establish a government for the term of one Knesset, to restore proper order of governance after 7 October and to repair in four years what can be repaired;

Therefore it has been agreed between the parties as follows:

1. A government shall be established headed by MK Gadi Eisenkot, and shall be composed of the factions Yashar, Together [ביחד], Yisrael Beiteinu and the Democrats. The government will be a Zionist, statist [ממלכתית] government and will serve all the citizens of Israel, Jews and Arabs alike. The vote of every citizen is equal in its weight.

2. These basic guidelines do not decide any question of principle, and there is nothing in them amounting to a waiver by any party of its position on these questions. During its term the government will not take an irreversible step on these questions. Matters not settled here, including matters of religion and state, will be settled in the coalition agreements.

**The State and its citizens**

3. The government factions declare that the State of Israel is a Jewish and democratic state, as defined in the Basic Laws. The government will act in the spirit of the Declaration of Independence, according to which the state "will ensure complete equality of social and political rights to all its citizens [לכל אזרחיה] without distinction of religion, race and sex". Nothing in this clause obliges any party to a position regarding Basic Law: Israel – the Nation State of the Jewish People, and every party may act regarding this law according to law.

4. The Arab citizens of Israel are citizens with equal rights, and their elected representatives are a legitimate part of Israeli democracy. The government will not initiate legislation that would widen the grounds for disqualifying lists and candidates from participating in Knesset elections.

5. The government will not initiate legislation that harms the civic equality of any group of citizens, and will not condition citizens' rights on political views. Government services will be provided in full also in Arabic.

6. Service for all. Within one year of its formation the government will bring about the enactment of a law based on equality in the burden, as detailed below:
   - a. Every young person liable for service under the Security Service Law [Consolidated Version], 5746–1986, will serve in the IDF or in national-civic service. There are no exemption quotas and no blanket exemptions. Personal economic sanctions will be imposed on draft-evaders [משתמטים], from the dates to be set in the law. State funding for educational institutions will be conditioned on core studies.
   - b. The benefits connected to service will be given to whoever serves, whoever they are. No existing universal right or allowance will be conditioned on service.
   - c. As for citizens who are not liable for service under the Security Service Law, national-civic service in this term will be voluntary, civilian and within their communities, and will be operated in cooperation with the local authorities. The law will not change the existing conscription arrangements.
   - d. If the law has not been enacted within a year, the government will decide on budgetary measures against institutions whose students evade service and on suspension [השעיית] of the benefits connected to service.

7. The government will advance [תקדם] Basic Law: Legislation, by broad consent, as well as a law limiting the term of office of the Prime Minister. The laws concerning the judicial system enacted in the 25th Knesset will be examined by a public committee, which will submit its conclusions within one year. Until the government decides on the committee's conclusions, the powers established in these laws will not be exercised. A return to the arrangements that preceded these laws is not a change requiring broad consent.

**Security and the fight against terror**

8. The government factions condemn all terror and every deliberate harm to innocent civilians, whoever the perpetrator and whoever the victim. They mourn every innocent civilian killed since 7 October, Israelis and Palestinians alike, including in the Gaza Strip.

9. The government factions condemn without reservation the massacre that Hamas committed on 7 October 2023, including murder, rape and abduction of civilians.

10. Hamas is a terrorist organization. Its military and governmental capabilities will be dismantled; it will not rule the Gaza Strip and will not threaten the citizens of Israel. Israel retains security freedom of action, in coordination with the international framework for Gaza. The government will act to consolidate the end of the war and to return the remaining hostages [החטופים] and the bodies of the fallen [החללים], and will also act to enable humanitarian aid and reconstruction for the civilian population.

11. Jewish terror. The government will fight Jewish terror and nationalist violence against Palestinians and their property with the same determination with which the state fights all terror, including by means of administrative detention according to law and under judicial review, a joint Shin Bet–police unit, the re-establishment of the Judea and Samaria District [מחוז ש"י] of the Israel Police, the dismantling of violence hubs established without a permit, and prosecution. This violence is the work of a violent handful [קומץ אלים], and it harms Palestinians, the residents of the area and the State of Israel itself.

12. Within 14 days of its swearing-in the government will decide, under the Commissions of Inquiry Law, 5729–1968, on the establishment of a state commission of inquiry into the events of 7 October, the decade that preceded them and the conduct of the war.

**Judea and Samaria (the West Bank)**

13. In Judea and Samaria, held since 1967 under the authority of the military commander, the government will act during its term as follows:
   - a. There will be no annexation and no application of sovereignty, and the legal status will not be changed.
   - b. Powers transferred from the military commander to political appointees will be returned to the IDF and the Civil Administration.
   - c. No new communities [יישובים] will be established. Construction in the existing communities [יישובים] will be done within the approved plans and according to a government decision.
   - d. The state budget will not include allocations for unauthorized outposts [מאחזים בלתי מורשים] nor coalition funds for them.

14. Outposts and "farms" established without a government decision and without lawful authorization will be evacuated, starting with those on private Palestinian land. A timetable for evacuation will be published within 90 days, and a report on progress will be published every quarter. During the term no unauthorized outpost will be regularized [יוסדר], except regularization steps that the government decided on before 27 October 2026. These steps will continue according to law and subject to judicial review, and none of them will apply to private Palestinian land.

15. Israel retains full security responsibility and full security freedom of action in the entire area. The government will maintain security and civil coordination with the Palestinian Authority and will work with regional and international partners to strengthen stability. In accordance with clause 2, no decision on a permanent settlement [הסדר קבע] will be taken during the term, and the positions of the parties regarding such a settlement are fully reserved to them.

16. The government will preserve the historic status quo at the holy places in Jerusalem, including on the Temple Mount (al-Aqsa) [בהר הבית (אל-אקצא)].

17. The commitments in the open letter of the candidate to form the government (Part B) will be approved as a government decision at the government's first meeting and will be funded in the first state budget.

### Part B – Open letter of the candidate to form the government to the National Committee of Heads of Arab Local Authorities

*The letter will be published before the vote on the formation of the government and will be attached as an annex to the basic guidelines.*

To: the Chairman of the National Committee of Heads of Arab Local Authorities

Copy: the Chairman of the High Follow-Up Committee

Dear Sir [שלום רב],

The government I am forming will act for all the citizens of Israel. In this letter I set out, publicly, what the government headed by me will do for Arab society in the next four years. These are commitments toward the citizens and toward the heads of authorities whom they elected. This is not a price in exchange for votes. I ask you to hold us to account for them every quarter.

1. Crime and violence.
   - A national plan in the amount of at least one billion NIS per year, to be entered into the budget base for four years.
   - The plan will include policing, prevention and enforcement against crime organizations, including activity by the Shin Bet, according to law, under judicial review and against these organizations only.
   - At the head of the plan will stand a government coordinator for the fight against crime and violence in Arab society, to be appointed by government decision for the entire term. The minister responsible for the police will delegate to the coordinator, in writing, powers over the plan's budget, and only the government will be able to remove him from office.
   - The government intends to appoint Yoav Segalovitz to the post. Before taking up the post he will resign from the Knesset and declare publicly that he accepts the principles of the basic guidelines.
   - Every year the government will publish the number of murders and the rate of cases solved and in which an indictment was filed.

2. Economic plan.
   - A new five-year plan following on Government Decision 550, whose scope will not be less than it in real terms (about 30 billion NIS), to be approved in the first budget.
   - The grants to Arab local authorities that were cut will be restored.
   - Targets to be published in employment, Hebrew teaching and higher education, placement of Arab teachers, medical residency slots, and housing and employment for young people.

3. Planning and housing.
   - Master plans for Arab localities according to a timetable to be published, and expansion of jurisdiction areas.
   - From the day the government is sworn in, enforcement under Amendment 116 to the Planning and Building Law ("Kaminitz Law") against existing and inhabited residential homes will be frozen.
   - Within six months the law will be amended so that administrative fines and demolition orders against these homes will be suspended until the plans are approved, and a track for their regularization will be opened. This will also apply in the Triangle and the Negev, including Tayibe, Tira, Qalansawe and the Negev localities.
   - Enforcement against new construction and against crime organizations will continue.

4. The Negev.
   - A recognition and regularization process for the unrecognized villages, including recognition decisions for a first group within 18 months, and connection to electricity and water.
   - As long as the process continues, no inhabited home will be demolished. Insofar as relocation is required, it will be done only within a regularization plan in agreement with the residents and their elected representatives.

5. Representation.
   - Targets for the representation of Arab citizens in the civil service and on the boards of government companies.
   - The Knesset presidium and its committees will be composed according to accepted practice and according to the size of each faction. The government factions will not prevent the election of a deputy Speaker from any faction whatsoever.

6. Monitoring.
   - A monitoring committee headed by the coordinator will meet every month and publish a report every quarter.
   - Its members will be representatives of the government and representatives of the National Committee of Heads of Arab Local Authorities.
   - Every faction in the Knesset that publicly answers this letter will appoint one MK to the committee **[not agreed. Alternative one: "who will serve on it as a member of equal standing", as demanded by Ra'am, Hadash and Ta'al, and with the agreement of Yesh Atid and the Democrats. Alternative two: "who will receive its reports and may direct questions to it", as demanded by Yashar and Yisrael Beiteinu.]**

7. The holy places and the way forward.
   - The government will preserve the historic status quo at the holy places in Jerusalem, including on the Temple Mount (al-Aqsa).
   - It will maintain coordination with the Palestinian Authority, will not take a step liable to prevent a future settlement between Israel and the Palestinians, and will respond to regional and international efforts to advance stability and a settlement.

Respectfully,
Gadi Eisenkot, MK
Candidate to form the government

### Part D – Responses of the Arab factions (unilateral statements; the government is not a party to them)

**Ra'am**
- Will vote for the formation of the government and for budgets that fund Part B.
- Will withdraw its support if the status quo at al-Aqsa is harmed, or if a breach of Part B is not corrected within 60 days.
- Retains freedom of vote on matters of religion and conscience, and does not adopt clauses 3 and 10 of Part A.

**The Joint List (Hadash, Ta'al, Balad)**
- "We did not vote for this government." Hadash and Ta'al will abstain in the vote on the formation of the government; Balad's MKs will be absent from it.
- As long as Part B is being implemented, the List will not support no-confidence motions or bills to dissolve the Knesset.
- The safety net will lapse if a breach of Part B, or harm to the status quo at al-Aqsa, is not corrected within 60 days.
- Balad will be absent from the budget votes only if it contains no funds for annexation or for outposts [למאחזים, no qualifier], and it funds Part B.
- Each of the parties composing the List retains its positions:
  - Hadash, on the occupation, the illegality of the settlements [ההתנחלויות] and the right of the Palestinian people to self-determination;
  - Ta'al and Balad, on the occupation and the settlements;
  - all three, in their opposition to military or civilian service in any form whatsoever.

## B. tv_statement.md, Hebrew blocks

### (a) Hebrew original

Good evening.

We stand here together. We argued, and we will argue tomorrow too.
But tonight Israel receives a new government.
A Zionist, statist government. A Jewish and democratic state, in the spirit of the Declaration of Independence.
A government of all the citizens of Israel. One hundred percent. Jews and Arabs alike. The voice [vote, קול] of every citizen has the same weight.

Within 14 days: a state commission of inquiry. On the seventh of October, on the years before it, and on the war.
Without running away from responsibility. Not even from our own responsibility.

Hamas is a terrorist organization. Its military and governmental capabilities will be dismantled, and it will not rule Gaza.
And we will bring home every hostage [חטוף] and every fallen [חלל] who has not yet been returned.

The era of draft-evasion is over. Whoever is liable for service will serve, in the army or in civilian service. Whoever serves will be rewarded.

The Arab citizens of Israel are equal citizens. We will fight the crime that kills them almost every day. It is not their problem. It is the state's problem.

And in Judea and Samaria there will be law. We will fight Jewish terror, the violent handful that harms the innocent and harms us too. We will evacuate illegal outposts [מאחזים בלתי חוקיים]. And we will not annex.

We are not asking you to believe us. Check us. Every three months, in numbers.

Good luck to us all.

### (b) Hebrew translation of Ra'am's statement

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful.

My dear brothers and sisters, we told you from the beginning: a position is important, but a position alone does not build a school.

Ra'am will vote for the new government, because our supreme interest was and remains to replace the government of incitement and racism.

We have not given up our identity or our principles. What we obtained are public commitments, with numbers and dates: an emergency plan against crime and violence at one billion shekels a year, freezing of the Kaminitz Law from the day of the swearing-in, recognition of the Negev villages and a five-year plan of 30 billion shekels for our communities. And we will follow the implementation day by day.

We condemned every harm to innocent civilians, and we condemned the crimes of the seventh of October. And we grieve for every innocent soul that was killed, Arab and Jewish, Palestinian and Israeli.

The blessed al-Aqsa Mosque is a red line, and the status quo there must be respected.

Our support is not an open cheque. If al-Aqsa is harmed or the commitments are breached, we will withdraw it.

We extend a hand to a real partnership between Arab and Jewish citizens, and to peace and reconciliation between the two peoples.

And may Allah make our path succeed.

### (c) Hebrew translation of the Joint List's statement

Sons of our people,

A few days ago you went out to vote at a rate never seen before, and you brought down the government of blood, the government of Netanyahu, Ben Gvir and Smotrich. This is your achievement, yours and no one else's. The Netanyahu era is over, and you are the ones who ended it.

We did not vote for this government, we did not sign on to the Jewishness of the state, and we did not give anyone an open cheque.

What we offer is a safety net for our people, not for the government, in exchange for written and public commitments: one billion shekels a year against crime, freezing of the Kaminitz Law, an end to house demolitions and recognition of the Negev villages, no annexation and with evacuation of the outposts, and preservation of the status quo at the al-Aqsa Mosque. On the day these commitments are breached, the safety net falls. And every three months we will demand an accounting from the government before our people.

We reject the harm to innocent civilians of both peoples since the beginning of the war, and demand a final end to the war against Gaza and its reconstruction.

There is no democracy with occupation. We will continue to struggle to end it, and for an independent Palestinian state on the borders of 4 June 1967 whose capital is East Jerusalem, a just solution to the refugee problem according to the resolutions of international legitimacy, and full civil and national equality.

No to the disqualification of our people's representatives. No to military or civilian service, in any form. We want an equal partnership.
