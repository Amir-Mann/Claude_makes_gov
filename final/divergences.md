# Where the texts diverge: the Arab parties vs Together (Bennett) and Yisrael Beiteinu

This page sets each side's own wording side by side, issue by issue. <mark>Highlighted</mark> words are the points of direct conflict.
- **Bennett and Lieberman:** their quotes come from their dossiers and their round files, and from the lines they would add to the government leaders' TV statement. Neither of them signs the framework.
- **The Arab parties:** their quotes come from their own TV statements (b) and (c) and from their Part D replies.
- **Yesh Atid:** although it runs inside Together, its positions are close to the framework text. It is noted only where it differs from Bennett.

| # | Issue | Together (Bennett) / Yisrael Beiteinu | Ra'am / Joint List (Hadash, Ta'al, Balad) | Where the final text lands |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who the government rests on | Bennett: "אין מנדט לממשלה <mark>שנתמכת במפלגות ערביות</mark>" (no mandate for a government supported by Arab parties). Lieberman: "<mark>לא ניתן להישען</mark> על ח"כים מרע"ם ומהרשימה המשותפת" (Ra'am and Joint List MKs cannot be relied on), and no minority government "<mark>even for a transitional period</mark>". | Ra'am: «القائمة الموحدة <mark>ستصوّت لصالح</mark> الحكومة الجديدة» (Ra'am will vote for the new government). Joint List: «<mark>لم نصوّت</mark> لهذه الحكومة… شبكة أمان لشعبنا لا للحكومة» (we did not vote for this government… a safety net for our people, not for the government). | **This is the impasse.** The government exists only with Ra'am's yes and the Joint List's abstention, so Bennett and Lieberman decline. |
| 2 | The character of the state | Both: "ממשלה <mark>ציונית</mark> ממלכתית", "מדינה <mark>יהודית</mark> ודמוקרטית" (a Zionist, statist government; a Jewish and democratic state). | Joint List: «<mark>لم نوقّع على يهودية الدولة</mark>» (we did not sign up to the state's Jewishness). Ra'am "<mark>does not adopt</mark> clause 3". | Part A, clause 3 is affirmed by the government parties only. The Arab factions are not signatories. |
| 3 | Hamas | Lieberman: "<mark>חיסול</mark> היכולות הצבאיות והשלטוניות של חמאס" (eliminating Hamas's military and governing capabilities). Bennett: "ישראל תחזיק <mark>בקו הצהוב</mark> עד שיפורק" (Israel will hold the yellow line until Hamas is disarmed). | Ra'am: «أدنّا جرائم السابع من أكتوبر» (we condemned the crimes of 7 October), without naming Hamas and without "destroy". Joint List: «نرفض المسّ بالمدنيين الأبرياء من الشعبين <mark>منذ بداية الحرب</mark>»; «<mark>وقف الحرب على غزة</mark> نهائيًا» (we reject harm to innocent civilians of both peoples since the war began; a final end to the war on Gaza). | Part A, clause 10: "dismantled", which is the government's wording. Ra'am records that it does not adopt clause 10. |
| 4 | The Gaza dead | Neither will say on camera "Israelis and Palestinians alike, <mark>including in Gaza</mark>". | Ra'am: «نتألّم لكل نفس بريئة… <mark>فلسطينية</mark> وإسرائيلية» (we grieve for every innocent soul, Palestinian and Israeli). Joint List: «<mark>الحرب على غزة</mark>» (the war on Gaza). | Part A, clause 8 mourns "including in the Gaza Strip". This is one reason Bennett and Lieberman would not read the text. |
| 5 | The territory | "<mark>יהודה ושומרון</mark>"; held "in the authority of the military commander". Neither uses <mark>"כיבוש"</mark> (occupation). | Joint List: «لا ديمقراطية <mark>مع الاحتلال</mark>» (there is no democracy with occupation). The Arabic version puts «<mark>الضفة الغربية</mark>» (the West Bank) first. | Part A uses "Judea and Samaria (the West Bank)" and "military commander". The word "occupation" appears only in the Arab replies. |
| 6 | A Palestinian state | Bennett: "<mark>לא תקום מדינה פלסטינית</mark>" (no Palestinian state will be established). Lieberman: "the Palestinians" only, and he rejects the "no step that precludes a settlement" line. | Joint List: «<mark>دولة فلسطينية مستقلة على حدود الرابع من حزيران 1967 عاصمتها القدس الشرقية</mark>، وحلّ عادل <mark>لقضية اللاجئين</mark>» (an independent Palestinian state on the 4 June 1967 borders with East Jerusalem as its capital, and a just solution to the refugee question). Hadash adds "the Palestinian people". | Part A: no final-status decision, positions reserved. Part B: "no step that precludes a future settlement between Israel and the Palestinians". |
| 7 | Settler violence | Lieberman: "<mark>קומץ אלים</mark>" (a violent handful), never "טרור מתנחלים" (settler terror). Bennett adds "נגד כל בנייה בלתי חוקית, <mark>יהודית ופלסטינית</mark>" (against all illegal construction, Jewish and Palestinian). | Balad: «<mark>إرهاب المستوطنين</mark>» (settler terror). Ta'al: «الإرهاب اليهودي» (Jewish terror). | Part A, clause 11 says "Jewish terror", committed by a "small, violent minority". Each side gets one of its terms. |
| 8 | Outposts and settlements | Bennett: whatever stands on legal land "<mark>should be regularized</mark>", including on state land. He also wants construction in existing communities, E1 included. | Hadash: all settlement is <mark>illegal</mark>. Ta'al: «عدم شرعية المستوطنات» (the settlements are illegitimate). The outposts are «غير القانونية» (illegal). | Part A, clause 14: "unauthorized" outposts evacuated, and none regularized beyond decisions already taken. Clause 13c: no new communities. Bennett treats both as deal-breakers. |
| 9 | Service | Lieberman: "<mark>100% למשרתים, 0% למשתמטים</mark>" (100% for those who serve, 0% for evaders), with automatic sanctions. Bennett: "whoever doesn't serve doesn't receive". | Joint List: «<mark>لا للخدمة العسكرية والمدنية بكل أشكالها</mark>» (no to military or civil service in any form). Ra'am: «خدمة مدنية <mark>تطوعية</mark>» (voluntary civil service) only. | Clause 6: a duty for those liable under the Security Service Law, and voluntary service for others. The Joint List votes against; Ra'am votes for only while the voluntary track stands. |
| 10 | Disqualification | Lieberman: <mark>delete</mark> clause 4's line on widening disqualification grounds. Both voted on 23 Sep to disqualify Balad's chair. | Balad: «<mark>لا لشطب ممثلي شعبنا</mark>» (no to disqualifying our people's representatives). Without the line, Balad votes against. | Clause 4 keeps a narrowed line ("will not initiate legislation"). |
| 11 | The holy site | "<mark>הר הבית</mark> (אל-אקצא)" (the Temple Mount, al-Aqsa). Bennett rejects an <mark>al-Aqsa breach trigger</mark>. | Ra'am: «المسجد الأقصى المبارك <mark>خط أحمر</mark>… إذا مُسّ الأقصى… <mark>نسحبه</mark>» (the blessed al-Aqsa Mosque is a red line… if al-Aqsa is harmed… we withdraw our support). Hadash: <mark>never «جبل الهيكل»</mark> (Temple Mount). | The government text says "status quo" with both names in Hebrew. The breach trigger appears only in the Arab parties' own replies. The Arabic version omits "Temple Mount" (flagged in `CHANGES_run2.md`). |
| 12 | Monitoring the commitments | Lieberman: Arab MKs as <mark>observers only</mark>, with reports and questions. | Ra'am, Hadash, Ta'al: «<mark>عضو متساوٍ</mark> في المكانة» (a member of equal standing). A deal-breaker for all three. | **Open:** Part B, clause 6 remains in brackets. |
| 13 | Crime | Lieberman: "<mark>טרור פלילי</mark>… <mark>יד ברזל</mark>" (criminal terror… an iron fist). Neither will accept a Ra'am office-holder. | Ra'am: its own coordinator, with «الأرقام والمواعيد» (numbers and dates). The Joint List: Ra'am must not "own" the file. | Agreed in substance (NIS 1bn a year, Shin Bet against criminal organizations only). The coordinator is Segalovitz, who resigns his seat first. |
| 14 | The Negev | Bennett's platform: "<mark>סנקציות כלכליות כבדות על סרבני פינוי</mark>" (heavy economic sanctions on those who refuse to be relocated). | Ra'am, Hadash and Ta'al: relocation only «<mark>بالاتفاق مع السكان وممثليهم المنتخبين</mark>» (by agreement with the residents and their elected representatives); never «ترحيل قسري» (forced expulsion). | Part B, clause 4 uses the Arab parties' wording. |
| 15 | The hostages | "<mark>כל החטופים והחללים</mark> יחזרו הביתה" (every hostage and every fallen soldier will come home), a core line for Lieberman, Yashar and Yesh Atid. | <mark>Neither Arab statement mentions the hostages.</mark> Ra'am's legal usage is «المحتجزين» (detainees). | The hostages appear only in Part A, clause 10 and in the government leaders' TV statement. |

**Reading this table.** On about half the issues (1, 6, 8, 10 and 12) the gap is about substance, not wording. That is why the run-2 negotiation ended without a government: no drafting choice reconciles "not supported by Arab parties" with a government that exists only because of Ra'am's votes.
