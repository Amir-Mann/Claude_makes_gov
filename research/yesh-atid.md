## 1. Identity and latest result

- **Leader:** Yair Lapid (b. 1963). He founded Yesh Atid in 2012, served as Finance Minister (2013–14), Foreign Minister and alternate PM (2021–22), PM (Jul–Dec 2022) and opposition leader (2023–26).
- **List:** He is **#2 on "Together" (ביחד)**, behind Naftali Bennett. The list was formed on 26 Apr 2026 (https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/hkljptspwl).

## 2. Ideological foundations (lifelong)

- **Founding goals (2012):**
  - civil and middle-class priorities;
  - "equality in the burden", meaning a draft for all;
  - protecting the High Court;
  - a constitution;
  - two states with the settlement blocs kept.

  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yesh_Atid
- **Equal burden** is the party's signature cause: the 2013–14 Peri law with criminal sanctions (run-1 lead; the 2013 quote is unverified).
- **Arab parties: pragmatic, not ideological.**
  - Jan 2013: Lapid refused to build a blocking bloc "with the Hanin Zoabis" [Q-EN, ynet 28 Jan 2013].
  - 2021: he was the architect of the government with Ra'am. The government recognised 3 villages on 3 Nov 2021, and Lapid personally brought Ra'am back in Apr 2022 (ToI links in §0).
  - 2022: "דברו איתנו שוב אחרי הבחירות" (25 Aug 2022), but Hadash–Ta'al excluded: "תומא סלימאן לא יכולה להיות בממשלה ולא תהיה בממשלה" (30 Oct 2022, Maariv).
  - The same day he said he would not sit with Netanyahu: "ואיתו אי אפשר לשבת" [Q].
- **Nation-State Law.** Yesh Atid opposed the law. Its MKs voted for the Mreeh equality amendment (29 Jul 2020), Lapid backed Liberman's amendment, "מסכים עם כל מילה" (15 May 2022), and Cohen's amendments in Nov 2023 had all 24 Yesh Atid MKs (links in §0).
- **Palestinians:**
  - "Two states for two peoples", conditional on "a peaceful" state (UN, 22 Sep 2022).
  - After 7 Oct he hardened: the recognitions were "אסון מדיני… פרס לטרור" (21 Sep 2025).
  - Gaza: Egyptian trusteeship, then a reformed PA (25 Feb 2025).

## 3. 2026 platform, campaign red lines and promises about partners

**Joint Together platform** (https://polimeter.co.il/parties/beyahad, quoting be-yahad.org.il):
- **Service law:**
  - [Q] "מי שמשרת — יקבל בחזרה מהמדינה. מי שלא משרת — לא יקבל";
  - NIS 8.8bn;
  - opposition to any plan with exemption percentages.
- **Education:** [Q] "או 100 אחוז ליבה, או 0 אחוז מימון". Full core curriculum within 8 years.
- **Governance:** [Q] "תיקח אחריות ותכונן חוקה על בסיס עקרונות מגילת העצמאות". An 8-year PM limit, and a state commission of inquiry on day one.
- **Economy:** end the rabbinate's kashrut monopoly; cut food prices by 30% over 3 years.
- **Religion and state:**
  - The joint platform on Shabbat transport is a compromise: lines to hospitals, IDF bases and the airport, plus metropolitan agreements (ynet, 31 Aug 2026).
  - Lapid's own pledges go further:
    - civil marriage and Shabbat transport "in secular majority localities" (Mar 2026, via ToI 20 Apr 2026);
    - equal rights for "every couple" within "the government's first 100 days" (JPost, 10 Oct 2026);
    - "בממשלה הבאה נעביר תחבורה ציבורית בשבת, נישואין אזרחיים וגיוס לכולם" [S, C14 on X, 23 Mar 2026].

**Promises about partners:**
- **Netanyahu:** never. Lapid on election night: "בנימין נתניהו וממשלתו קרסו היום, זו תבוסה גמורה" [Q].
- **Haredi parties:**
  - Tibon (#6): "We will not have a government with the Haredi [parties], and we will end the Haredi autonomies" [Q-EN, 6 Sep 2026].
  - JPost: the alliance opposes coalitions with Haredi parties whose service positions conflict with its own [P].
- **Arab parties:**
  - The list's binding line is Bennett's: "המפלגות הערביות לא ציוניות ולא נסתמך עליהן" (26 Apr 2026).
  - Lapid's own line, per AP: he "would not form another coalition with Ra'am" [P, AP 16 Oct 2026].
  - Together backed disqualifying Balad's Abu Shahada [P, polimeter].
  - Bennett nonetheless called Abbas a good partner (18 Oct 2026).
  - Arab-language press read the merger as a partnership whose numbers depend on Arab support: al-Quds, 28 Apr 2026 (https://www.alquds.com/ar/posts/237427) wrote that the polls showed the alliance could not reach a majority «دون دعم قائمة عربية واحدة على الأقل».
- **"Change bloc" pact** (26 Sep 2026, signed at Lapid's home by Eisenkot, Bennett, Golan and Liberman):
  - a "Zionist, statesmanlike government", "Jewish and democratic";
  - no attacks on each other;
  - working groups on equal burden, the commission, a PM term limit, a constitution and a 100-day plan;
  - **no mention of Arab parties**.

  Source: https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/s16nsks5ze
- **Eisenkot:**
  - **Before the merger,** Lapid offered him the top slot (Calcalist, 26 Apr 2026). Lapid reportedly offered to drop to #3 if Eisenkot joined (Wikipedia).
  - **Rivalry in the final week.** Lapid on 26 Oct: [Q] "להטעות את הציבור זו לא הדרך שלנו, זו הדרך של המחנה השני", and of the "biggest party" claim, "זה פשוט לא נכון" (https://www.srugim.co.il/101014423-…). Also [Q] "קמפיין המפלגה הגדולה עלול להפסיד לנו את הבחירות… אם ננסה לשתות אחד את השני, כל הגוש בסכנה" (https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/skda6fn2fl).
  - **Draft dispute.** Tibon attacked Eisenkot's openness to Shas as "30 percent enlistment". Eisenkot reportedly proposed allowing about 3% of Haredi men to be exempted for study [S, alhurra]. That conflicts with our "no exemption percentages".

## 5. Positions on the framework topics

- **Jewish state / Nation-State Law:**
  - We want "Jewish and democratic" anchored in the Declaration of Independence, with civil equality. Our 2023 amendment says "a home with equal rights for all its citizens".
  - Clause 4 of the framework (a description of the law plus a quote from the Declaration) is our formula.
- **Terrorism, Hamas, 7 Oct, Gaza, hostages:**
  - Hamas must be named and disarmed, and all hostages and bodies returned.
  - Gaza: Egyptian or international trusteeship, then a reformed PA (Feb 2025).
  - Palestinian statehood moves now are a "reward for terror" (2025).
  - Mourning Gazan civilians is not taboo for us, but we will not equate them with the victims of the massacre.
- **Arab citizens:**
  - Resolution 550, the crime plan, the 3 Negev villages and the Kaminitz freeze are **our 2021 legacy** (§0 #11).
  - We favour a strong crime plan with the Shin Bet (joint platform: organised crime is "terror"). Bennett's Negev plan is enforcement-heavy, with mandatory minimums and a state of emergency.
  - Representation: Yesh Atid has an Arab HQ (Abu al-Hija, Atidna). There is no Arab candidate in a realistic slot.
- **West Bank:**
  - "טרור יהודי", "בושה לישראל" (Jul 2026); "a moral stain" (Aug 2026).
  - We are against annexation. We support "separation", and two states in principle while keeping the blocs.
  - We do not use "occupation".
  - Evacuating unauthorised outposts fits our rule-of-law line [INF]. We found no specific 2026 statement on outposts.

- **Haredi draft:**
  - No exemption percentages.

## 7. Negotiating position for run 2

**Who we are at the table:** 5 of 12 MKs in a list Bennett heads. We cannot sign alone; Bennett signs for Together. Our leverage:
- Lapid's personal standing as former PM;
- his record as the architect of the 2021 government;
- the anti-Netanyahu wing of the electorate;
- **the ability to split from Together** [INF]. A third of a faction can split under Knesset rules; 5 of 12 qualifies on paper, but the merged-party structure may block it. This is unverified.

**Real must-haves:**
1. **Netanyahu is not PM.** No Yesh Atid scenario exists under him.
2. A **state commission of inquiry at the first government meeting** (clause 11, already in the framework).
3. **A Service Law with no exemption percentages,** benefits tied to service, and a fixed timetable. We demand that clause 6a add:
   - "core curriculum as a condition for state funding";
   - personal economic sanctions on draft evaders;
   - a first-year deadline with a fallback, so that if the law is not passed, benefits are cut by government decision.
4. **Religion and state in the coalition agreement, in writing:**
   - civil marriage and equal rights for every couple within 100 days;
   - Shabbat transport at local option;
   - ending the kashrut monopoly.

   The framework's "settled in the coalition agreement" (clause 2) is acceptable only if those are pre-agreed among the government parties. Ra'am keeps its free vote on them.
5. **Lapid gets a senior portfolio** (Foreign Ministry preferred, Finance as alternative) [INF]. Yesh Atid is proportionally represented within Together's share. Our ask: Together's portfolios are split by the 7:5 MK ratio, not by Bennett's sole choice.
6. Courts protected: the clause 3 wording ("only with broad consent") is enough.

**Real deal-breakers:**
- Netanyahu as PM, or rotation with him.
- Haredi exemption quotas, including Eisenkot's reported "3%".
- "Occupation" or "Nakba" in the Hebrew text.
- Joint List factions *inside* the government.
- Anything that lets Shas or UTJ in on their own draft terms.

**Our best alternative to this deal** [INF], from best to worst:
- **(a)** A Zionist unity government with Likud without Netanyahu (66 seats): we are in government with no Arab dependence. This is better for Bennett and Liberman; it is acceptable to us only without Netanyahu and with the draft law. It is unlikely while Netanyahu controls Likud.
- **(b)** This framework (55 + abstentions), a fragile minority-dependent government.
- **(c)** Opposition or new elections. Yesh Atid at 5 seats inside someone else's list is weak, and new elections risk erasing the brand. We do **not** want new elections.

So for us the framework beats every realistic alternative except (a). This is the opposite of Liberman and close to Golan. **Our real risk is not the Arab partners. It is Bennett walking away and taking Together with him.**

**Our posture toward the run-1 framework:**

Accept with demands.
- **Accept as written:** Clauses 1–5, 7–9, 10, 11, 12–15, 15a, and Chapter D.
  - Chapter D is our 2021 legacy, and we will claim it.
  - Clause 15's reference to the "Palestinian people" is acceptable to Lapid.
- **Demand changes:**
  - **(i) Clause 6a:** strengthen it as in must-have 3. Keep the Arab civil-service track as written.
  - **(ii) Clause 2:** add an annex binding on the government parties covering religion and state and the portfolio split.
  - **(iii) Chapter E:** keep it a **parliamentary agreement**, so it is not a "coalition with Ra'am", which Lapid told AP he would not form.
    - The Ra'am deputy minister (clause 22) is the most exposed point for us and for Bennett. It could be recast as "a director-general-level head of the national crime authority nominated in consultation with Ra'am", or kept only if Bennett accepts it.
    - We will not be the party that kills it.
  - **(iv) Annex 1:** the Balad safety net is tolerable as a *unilateral* declaration. However, Together backed disqualifying Abu Shahada, so Joint List "observers" on the monitoring committee (clause 24) should be removed or made non-formal.
  - **(v) Clause 20:** deputy Speakers, for both Ra'am and the Joint List. Lapid can live with them; Bennett keeps his free vote, as in Annex 2.

**TV statement:** Accept, with one demand.
- Lapid can read every line. "Jewish terror", "illegal outposts", "no annexation", "equal citizens as the Declaration promised" and the commission are his own vocabulary.
- **Demand:** the commitments should name "שוויון בנטל / שירות לכולם" ("equal burden / service for all"), or, if the Arab readers cannot say it, Lapid says it first in his own remarks.
- The line "Israelis and Palestinians alike, including in Gaza" is defensible for Lapid but will be clipped against him. He will reaffirm "Hamas is responsible for every death in Gaza" in his own remarks [INF].

**What can we trade?**
- Silence on a Palestinian state.
- Delaying the Nation-State amendment.
- "Judea and Samaria" alongside "military control".
- The Ra'am deputy minister, if Bennett insists on its removal.
- Lapid's portfolio preference: Foreign rather than Finance.

**What rivals and voters will say the next morning:**
- **Likud and Channel 14:** "Lapid brought the Muslim Brotherhood back — and this time Balad too."
- **Bennett's right flank:** "Lapid dragged Bennett into breaking his promise again."
- **Liberman:** "Lapid and Golan prefer Tibi to Likud."
- **Yesh Atid's own liberal base:** "You shrank from 24 to 5, gave Bennett the list, and still got no civil marriage."
- **Eisenkot's camp:** "Lapid is the obstacle to a broad Zionist government."

Our answer: the commission, the draft law and no Netanyahu, with Arab partners who condemned Hamas by name.
