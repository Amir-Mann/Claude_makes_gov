# Yesh Atid (יש עתיד), running within "Together" (ביחד / B'Yachad): stage-A dossier

Prepared 2026-10-28 by the Yesh Atid agent. The focus is Yesh Atid and Yair Lapid's **own** positions; Bennett's positions appear only where they bind the joint list.
Labels: **[Q]** is a verbatim quote. **[P]** is a paraphrase by the cited outlet. **[S]** means the claim reached me only through a search-engine summary and I could not open and verify the underlying article; treat it as lower confidence.

---

## 1. Identity

- **Leader:** Yair Lapid, who founded Yesh Atid in 2012, served as Prime Minister from July to December 2022 and has been opposition leader since 2023. He is **No. 2 on the joint list "Together" (ביחד)**, which Naftali Bennett heads.
- **List:** On 26 Apr 2026, Yesh Atid and "Bennett 2026" formed a "unified party led by Bennett" (חיבור של יש עתיד ומפלגת בנט 2026 למפלגה מאוחדת בראשות בנט) [Q, Bennett] (https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/bjev03jpwl). Yesh Atid received **4 of the top 10 slots and roughly half of the top 15** (https://www.timesofisrael.com/lapid-unveils-yesh-atid-lineup-for-byachad-electoral-slate/, 5 Sep 2026). Another report put it at 10 of the top 24 (Reshet Bet via JPost, 28 Apr 2026: https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-894432). Wikipedia reports that Bennett reportedly controls ministerial choices and candidate selection (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Together_(Israel)); this is unverified.
- **2026 seats:** Together won about 12 seats (preliminary). On a 4/10 to 1/2 share, that is likely **about 5–6 Yesh Atid MKs**. This is down from 24 seats in 2022. Polls before the merger had Yesh Atid at 4–7 seats, near the threshold (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bennett%E2%80%93Lapid_alliance).
- **Base:** Secular, middle-class, liberal Zionist voters in the centre (Gush Dan and the Sharon).
- **Ideology in three lines:** Liberal Zionist centrism, combining "equal burden" (Haredi draft, core curriculum) with liberal religion-and-state policy and defence of the courts and the rule of law. On security it is hawkish-pragmatic, and on Palestinians it favours "separation" or "two states for two peoples" in principle while keeping the settlement blocs. It defines itself against Netanyahu, Ben Gvir and Smotrich and against Haredi political power.

**Where recent positions diverge from long-held ones:**
1. **Arab parties.** In 2021–22 Lapid built and maintained the government with Ra'am, and he left the door open to the Joint List in 2022. In 2026 he gave **the AP in May an undertaking not to form another coalition with Ra'am** [P, AP via PBS: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/arab-led-parties-could-decide-the-israeli-election-if-anyone-would-partner-with-them]. He also sits on a list whose leader pledged "לא נסתמך עליהן" (we will not rely on them). This is the sharpest divergence.
2. **Palestinian state.** Lapid moved from "two states" (2012 platform, UN 2022) to calling the 2025 recognitions by the UK, Canada and Australia "a diplomatic disaster, a terrible step and a reward for terrorism" [Q via The National, 24 Oct 2026: https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/10/24/who-are-the-key-figures-in-israels-election-and-what-are-their-stances-on-palestinian-statehood/]. Bennett, who heads the joint list, said "לא נקים מדינה פלסטינית" (we will not establish a Palestinian state) [Israel Hayom, 17 Oct 2026, via https://polimeter.co.il/parties/beyahad]. Lapid has not, to my knowledge, repudiated two states. He has only stopped campaigning on it.
3. **Equality amendment to the Nation-State Law.** It remains declaratory policy, but in practice it was dropped in the 2026 joint platform: no platform page mentions it.

## 2. Platform and core promises (joint "Together" platform plus Yesh Atid's own emphases)

Platform site: https://be-yahad.org.il/plans/?bennett_plans_cat=198. Every chapter is titled "רק ביחד נתקן…" ("Only together will we fix…").
- **Oct 7 state commission of inquiry (ועדת חקירה ממלכתית) on day one.** Both Bennett and Lapid pledged at the 26 Apr 2026 launch to set it up "on the first day of the government" (https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/elections/2026-04-26/ty-article/0000019d-cac7-d95a-afbd-ebcfca080000).
- **Universal service, "חוק שירות לכולם" / "חוק המשרתים".** The principle is "מי שמשרת — יקבל בחזרה מהמדינה. מי שלא משרת — לא יקבל" ("whoever serves gets back from the state; whoever doesn't serve gets nothing"), at a cost of NIS 8.8bn. It allows **no exemption quotas**, and Haredi students get a full core curriculum within 8 years (https://polimeter.co.il/parties/beyahad). Lapid's election-night line was "שוויון בנטל וגיוס לכולם" ("equal burden and conscription for all") [Q] (https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/skmmqicngx).
- **Governance:** "הממשלה תיקח אחריות ותכונן חוקה" ("the government will take responsibility and draft a constitution") based on the **Declaration of Independence**. It also proposes an 8-year limit on the prime minister's term and **regulating judicial review "with broad agreement"** (the governance chapter on the platform site above).
- **Religion and state:** Civil marriage, public transport on Shabbat decided by local authorities, and ending the kashrut monopoly (https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/ex-pm-bennett-backs-public-transport-on-shabbat-civil-marriage-sparking-haredi-backlash/). Yesh Atid's own pledge is equal rights for every couple, legislated within 100 days (https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-910703).
- **Cost of living:** Cut food prices by 30% within 3 years.
- **Personal security:** Treat organised crime as a national-security threat, "זהו טרור ואיום אסטרטגי" ("this is terror and a strategic threat"), with Shin Bet and IDF coordination. There is a separate "Law and order in the Negev" plan with mandatory minimum sentences and a state-of-emergency framing (17 May 2026, Israel Hayom via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Together_(Israel)).

## 4. Positions on the statement topics

### (a) Jewish state / Nation-State Law
- Yesh Atid is firmly Zionist and backs "Jewish and democratic", anchored in the **Declaration of Independence**, which it wants as the basis of a constitution (platform governance chapter).
- On the Nation-State Law, Yesh Atid supports adding **civil equality**, through the Begin/Yesh Atid version and the 2023 amendments (see 1b).
- **Statement implications:** Signing "Jewish and democratic state, as per the Declaration of Independence, with full civil equality" is natural for Yesh Atid and is in fact its preferred formula. Mentioning "equality" in connection with the Nation-State Law is acceptable to Yesh Atid, but Bennett may balk at it.

### (b) Terrorism, Hamas, Oct 7, Gaza, hostages
- Yesh Atid would eagerly sign an explicit condemnation of Hamas.
- Lapid pushed for completing the hostage deal and for "all hostages home". His Feb 2025 plan proposed Egyptian guardianship of Gaza with a reformed PA (https://jpost.com/israel-news/article-843712).
- The state commission on Oct 7 is a **must-have**.
- Since the 2025 recognitions, Lapid has framed Palestinian statehood moves as "a reward for terrorism".

### (c) Arab citizens
- **Track record:** Resolution 550 (NIS 30bn), the NIS 2.5bn crime plan, recognition of 3 Negev villages and the Kaminitz freeze all came from the 2021 government in which Lapid was the architect (see 1b). Lapid will claim ownership of renewing these, and they are the easiest "gains" for him to defend. Resolution 550 was later cut in the 2024 budget, a cut Lapid opposed as opposition leader (https://boi.org.il/publications/pressreleases/6-3-24/).
- **Crime:** The joint platform treats organised crime as terror and a strategic threat, with Shin Bet involvement. The Negev plan uses law-and-order framing (mandatory minimums).
- **Representation:** Yesh Atid has an "Arab headquarters" led by **Mohammad Abu al-Hija of Tamra**, founder of "Atidna", described as an Arab-Zionist youth movement. He entered the Knesset in July 2026 (https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-903641). I found **no Arab candidate in a realistic spot on the 2026 list**.
- **Kaminitz / Negev:** Yesh Atid owns the 2021 freeze and recognition precedent. The joint Negev plan's emphasis on enforcement creates tension with that legacy.

### (d) West Bank
- **Settler violence:** Lapid has been the strongest voice in his bloc on this.
  - 30 Aug 2026: "It is time to put an end to Jewish terrorism in the West Bank." He called the government's failure "a national disgrace that is causing enormous damage to our standing in the world" and described the rioters as "a moral stain on Israel" [Q] (https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-20/Middle-East/Lapid-Time-to-End-Jewish-Terrorism-in-West-Bank-54947).
  - Late July 2026, in a CNN interview: settler violence is "טרור יהודי" and "בושה לישראל" ("Jewish terror", "a shame for Israel") [Q of the Hebrew rendering] (https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2026_q3/Article-db1e3ffd4cfaf91027.htm).
  - In that interview he reportedly also said the attackers are "not even settlers, just terrorists", and that he would send 300 police officers and two Border Police battalions [S; the English wording is unverified].
  - He stressed that he does **not** attribute the violence to settlers as a whole.
- **Outposts:** I found no specific 2026 Lapid statement. Evacuating **illegal unauthorised outposts** (מאחזים בלתי חוקיים) fits the rule-of-law framing and is consistent with his position. Settlements and the blocs themselves are not to be labelled illegal.
- **Annexation:** Lapid has historically opposed annexation ("you annex, you pay", a framing he applied to Gaza: https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/you-annex-you-pay-lapid-warns-taxpayers-will-be-saddled-with-cost-of-occupying-gaza/).
- **Palestinian state:** Lapid supports it in principle, with heavy conditions (see 1b), but he is silent on it in 2026, and the joint-list leader rejects it. Yesh Atid prefers the statement **say nothing about end states**, which matches the task framing.

### (e) Other coalition-relevant items
- **State commission on Oct 7:** day one, a must-have.
- **Haredi draft:** No exemption quotas, and sanctions on draft evasion. This is a core, career-long issue.
- **Judiciary:** Protect the High Court; any change to judicial review only "with broad agreement"; a constitution.
- **Religion and state:** Civil marriage or civil unions, equal rights for every couple within 100 days, and local Shabbat transport.
- **Economy:** Middle class, cost of living and reservists' benefits.

## 6. Negotiation profile

- **Must-haves:**
  1. Netanyahu out.
  2. A state commission of inquiry on Oct 7 from day one.
  3. A universal draft with no exemption quotas.
  4. No harm to the courts, and any judicial change only by broad consent.
  5. A coalition **framed as Zionist-led**: the government's core and premiership in Zionist parties, with Lapid as a senior minister (Foreign or Finance; this is my inference).
- **Nice-to-haves:** Civil marriage and equal rights for couples; Shabbat transport; a constitution process; restoring Resolution 550 and the crime budgets (Lapid's own legacy); an equality clause in Basic Law.
- **Possible concessions:**
  - **Outside support or abstention by an Arab party** instead of full membership, with the agreement framed as a policy package for all citizens. This is the 2021 precedent turned into a "safety net" model. Lapid's post-election line "נרחיב בהמשך" ("we will expand later") leaves room for it.
  - Silence on a Palestinian state.
  - Delaying the Nation-State Law amendment.
  - Accepting "Judea and Samaria" alongside "territories under military control".
- **Deal-breakers:** Any word implying Israel is not legitimately a Jewish state; "occupation" or "apartheid" language in the Hebrew text; "Nakba"; any ambiguity on Hamas; a promise to repeal the Nation-State Law outright (amending it is fine); anything that keeps Haredi exemptions; Balad-style anti-Zionist partners in the cabinet.
- **What Yesh Atid needs to show its voters:**
  - "We replaced the Oct 7 government."
  - The commission and the draft law.
  - Liberal religion-and-state wins.
  - Evidence that Arab partners are "Abbas-style" pragmatists who signed onto a Jewish-and-democratic state and condemned Hamas by name. The statement's points 1–2 are exactly that cover, which is why Yesh Atid **supports** the four-point framing.

## 7. Uncertainties

- I could not open the full text of the Bennett–Lapid agreement. Ministerial allocation, veto rights and whether Lapid personally gets a portfolio are unknown.
- The AP May 2026 "no coalition with Ra'am" undertaking is known only by paraphrase. I found no verbatim quote.
- The "אנחנו הרוב…" quote and the CNN "just terrorists" English wording rest on search summaries only.
- I found no 2026 Yesh Atid statement specifically on **outposts**, **Kaminitz**, or a **Resolution 550 successor**.
- Seat split inside Together's 12 seats: about 5–6 for Yesh Atid is an estimate.
- Several older items (2013 Zoabis, 2018 Nation-State Law, 2021 Ra'am deal) are well documented. The judicial-overhaul role is general knowledge and was not separately cited.
