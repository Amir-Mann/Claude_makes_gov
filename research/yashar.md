# YASHAR! (ישר! עם איזנקוט) — Movement dossier (stage A)

## 1. Identity

- **Leader:** Lt. Gen. (res.) Gadi Eisenkot — IDF Chief of Staff 2015–2019; National Unity MK 2022–2025; observer in the Oct-2023 war cabinet; left the emergency government with National Unity in June 2024; split from Gantz (announced June 2025) and founded Yashar on 16 Sept 2025. His son Gal was killed fighting in Gaza in Dec 2023. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yashar_(political_party)); [ToI 16.9.2025](https://www.timesofisrael.com/gadi-eisenkot-founds-new-party-two-months-after-resigning-from-knesset/); [Al Jazeera 19.1.2024](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/1/19/israeli-minister-says-only-a-deal-can-free-captives-as-rifts-split-cabinet))
- **List (submitted Sept 2026, list letters "דרך"):** 1 Gadi Eisenkot, 2 Yoram Cohen (ex-Shin Bet chief), 3 Orit Farkash-Hacohen (ex-Blue&White minister), 4 Adi Altschuler (Israel Prize, education-minister designate), 5 Matan Kahana (ex-Yamina/National Unity, religious-Zionist), 6 Hili Tropper (ex-culture minister; his "בית ציוני" party merged in), 7 Shaul Meridor, 8 Tair Ifergan, 9 Osnat Gani Gonen, 10 Shira Shapira; then Inbar Harush Gity, Elazar Stern (ex-Yesh Atid, ex-IDF manpower chief), **Kamil Abu Rokon (13, Druze, ex-COGAT head)**, Dvora Sharifian Bakar, Alex Rif, Inbar Yehezkeli, Roy Folkman (ex-Kulanu), … Elisaf Peretz (20, reservist/educator), Shlomi Yehiav (35, ex-student union chair). ([Kipa 7.9.2026](https://www.kipa.co.il/חדשות/1231179-0/); [ice](https://www.ice.co.il/local-news/news/article/1128972); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-906392)). Founding figures also incl. Tal Russo, Manuel Trajtenberg, Yoav Horowitz ([Ynetnews 16.9.2025](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/ryhasrloxg)).
- **2026 result:** ~21 seats (some reports 22; ~786k votes, ~16%) — largest opposition list ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yashar_(political_party))).
- **Base:** centre / soft-right, security-establishment, reservists and bereaved families, traditional Mizrahi voters (threat to Shas: [ToI 21.8.2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/eisenkot-threatens-to-siphon-traditional-voters-away-from-a-panicking-shas/)), voters who blame Netanyahu for Oct 7 and the draft failure ([JPost 27.10.2026](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-913037)).
- **Ideology in 3 lines:** *ממלכתיות* (statism/national responsibility) — Jewish & democratic state in the spirit of the Declaration of Independence; security hawk but rule-of-law general (anti-Netanyahu, anti-Ben-Gvir, anti-settler-terror); "service for all" (Haredim and Arabs) as the organizing social principle, no Palestinian state in the coming term.

## 2. Platform / core promises

"10 steps" ([yasharwitheisenkot.com](https://yasharwitheisenkot.com/), [agenda](https://yasharwitheisenkot.com/agenda_point/)):
1. **Healing the rift:** ועדת חקירה ממלכתית into Oct 7, the decade before it and the war's management; care for bereaved, hostage survivors, PTSD; rebuild western Negev and north. Pledged to set it up **within 14 days** of taking office ([TIME 25.10.2026](https://time.com/article/2026/10/25/gadi-eisenkot-exclusive-interview/)).
2. **Security:** new national security doctrine ("אסטרטגיה לאומית סדורה"), strong IDF, secure borders, expand circle of peace, "a solid Jewish majority for generations".
3. **חוק שירות ממלכתי לכל** — integrate Haredim and Arabs into service, first of all IDF; "מי שמשרת, מתוגמל". Sanctions on refusers incl. stopping school funding ([PNN 27.7.2026](https://pnn.ps/news/726000)).
4. Education: state education priority, core studies for all, Jewish tradition.
5. **Governance/crime:** emergency personal-security plan; national cabinet vs. organized crime; "national program to eradicate violence in Arab society". (Earlier National Unity plan he authored: crime cabinet, warrantless searches for serious crime, anti-protection-racket tools, doubling police manpower in a decade — [JDN](https://www.jdn.co.il/news/1843967/).)
6. Economy: cost of living, anti-concentration, incentives for those who serve, periphery.
7. **Democracy:** majority rule + minority rights, rule of law, "מערכת משפט עצמאית", free press, Basic Law: Legislation, **PM two-term limit**, cut ministries, end political appointments.
8. Reduce polarization: keep Israel Jewish and democratic, "יהדות מכילה", **differential budgeting to narrow gaps**.
9. Aliyah: 1M immigrants in ~10 years; keep the Law of Return incl. grandchild clause ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-905241)).
10. National planning authority toward 2048.

Campaign-trail additions:
- Gaza: [Q] "We have two options: the 20 points of President Trump, or to use force… I prefer the diplomatic option… But if not, I know how to dismantle Hamas with force." ([TIME 25.10.2026](https://time.com/article/2026/10/25/gadi-eisenkot-exclusive-interview/)); end Hamas control "within 18 months" ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-913037)).
- West Bank (Modi'in, 21.10.2026, via Ch.14): [Q] "We will restore administrative orders, abolish the additional minister in the Defense Ministry" (i.e., reinstate administrative detention for Jewish extremists; dismantle Smotrich's civil-administration powers); 60–70k Shin Bet-screened Palestinian workers from J&S; none from Gaza ([Jewish Edition 23.10.2026](https://thejewishedition.com/news/israel/2026/10/23/eisenkot-pledges-to-restore-administrative-detention-bring-70000-pa-arab-workers-into-israel/)).

## 3. Red lines & coalition promises

- **Netanyahu:** [Q] "I unequivocally pledge that I will not serve under Netanyahu, not even in a rotation arrangement… If that happens, record this: I'll resign an hour later." (103FM, 21.10.2026, [JNS](https://jns.org/news/israel-news/eisenkot-rules-out-serving-under-netanyahu-even-in-rotation-deal)). Likud *without* Netanyahu is legitimate (Stern, [Ynet 4.9.2026](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/yokra14887590): [Q] "נתניהו לא יהיה פה ראש ממשלה. נגמר."). Election night: Netanyahu should "accept the people's vote of no confidence" ([ToI 28.10.2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/eisenkot-and-netanyahu-each-vow-to-form-next-coalition-even-as-exit-polls-predict-stalemate/)).

  1. recognition of Israel as a **Jewish and democratic state** (July: "יהודית, דמוקרטית וליברלית" per [PNN](https://pnn.ps/news/726000));
  2. the values of **מגילת העצמאות**;
  3. **שירות לכל** (military or civilian national service);
  4. (added Sept) **recognition of Hamas as a terror organization to be dismantled** — [Q] "הוספתי לזה כלל נוסף של הכרה בחמאס כארגון טרור".
  Then: [Q] "אני לא רואה כרגע אף מפלגה, ערבית או חרדית, שמקבלת את הכללים האלו – כולל מנסור עבאס… ולכן הוא לא יהיה חלק מהממשלה הבאה" (Ch.12, 26.9.2026, [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/elections/2026-09-26/ty-article/.premium/000001a0-de92-d9ab-abfd-ffb3ac700000)).
  On the Zarzir meeting claim: [Q] "אומרים שהבטחתי שמנסור עבאס יהיה בממשלה העתידית שאני אקים - מדובר בשקר מוחלט… מי שלא יקבל את העקרונות שלי לא יהיה שותף" ([Ynet, ~16.9.2026](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/sy2400pkfg); Arabic: [Arab48 16.9.2026](https://www.arab48.com/إسرائيليات/أخبار/2026/09/16/آيزنكوت-بين-إنكار-الموحدة-وحسابات-الـ61-ونتنياهو-يستثمر-تهمة-العرب-) "كذب مطلق").

Eisenkot never ruled out a **minority government sustained by Arab abstention/outside support**; Yashar prepared such a 58–59-MK scenario ([ToI 23.8.2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/eisenkot-lays-groundwork-for-a-minority-government-if-necessary-to-oust-netanyahu/); [Arab48](https://www.arab48.com/إسرائيليات/أخبار/2026/09/16/آيزنكوت-بين-إنكار-الموحدة-وحسابات-الـ61-ونتنياهو-يستثمر-تهمة-العرب-) distinguishes abstention / outside support / membership). Post-election "not ruled out" ([Maariv 28.10](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/opinions/article-1382123); [Ynet](https://ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/s1bqmfcnzg)). Note: Abbas has *repeatedly said* he recognizes Israel as Jewish and democratic and backs civilian service ([PNN 27.7.2026](https://pnn.ps/news/726000)) — i.e., Ra'am arguably meets 1–3; the gap is mainly Hamas wording and politics.

## 4. Positions on statement topics

**(a) Jewish state / Nation-State Law.** "מדינה יהודית, דמוקרטית, בטוחה ועוצמתית" in the spirit of the Declaration — this is condition #1. No found public position on amending the Nation-State Law; Arab mayors raised it at Zarzir and the party said issues were discussed "in terms of amendment rather than repeal" ([Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/sy2400pkfg)). Gantz's camp (Eisenkot's former party) proposed a Basic Law: Equality ([Ynetnews](https://ynetnews.com/article/hjcpj9ri9)); Abu Rokon (Druze) on list. → Likely acceptable: anchoring equality via the Declaration's values / Basic-Law-level equality, without repealing the Nation-State Law.

**(b) Terrorism / Hamas / Oct 7 / Gaza / hostages.** Hamas = terror org to be dismantled (coalition condition). Hamas military wing [Q] "must be destroyed or dismantled… It must end with that" ([Al-Monitor/Reuters 23.10.2026](https://al-monitor.com/originals/2026/10/eisenkot-offers-hawkish-alternative-netanyahu-israeli-pm)). Prefers Trump 20-point plan, PA-linked technocrats + international force (analyst, [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-912193)). Hostages: deal first, then defeat Hamas ([TIME](https://time.com/article/2026/10/25/gadi-eisenkot-exclusive-interview/)). State commission within 14 days.

**(c) Arab citizens.** [Q] "איך מדינת ישראל מתייחסת ל-100% מהאזרחים שלה - לא ל-79% ולא ל-70%"; [Q] "הדבר שמחבר בין כלל אזרחי ישראל, 100% מאזרחי ישראל, זאת הממלכתיות והחובה לחיים משותפים"; but also [Q] "מי שיקבלו תקציבים ממדינת ישראל הם אלה שמחויבים למדינת ישראל" (Zarzir, Sept 2026, [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/sy2400pkfg)). Crime in Arab society is a top platform item (national program, crime cabinet, police expansion). Attacked Likud for trying to "remove 21% of Arab citizens from the camp" ([INN 16.7.2026](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/430276)). Differential budgeting (step 8). Arab integration via civilian national service, framed as earning-equality ("מי שמשרת, מתוגמל"). No found positions on Kaminitz law or Negev unrecognized villages (gap). Representation: Druze ex-general Abu Rokon at #13.

**(d) West Bank.** No Palestinian state, "certainly not in any first term" ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-913037)); [Q] "two states for two peoples is a mistake" (Ch.7 via [Al-Monitor](https://al-monitor.com/originals/2026/10/eisenkot-offers-hawkish-alternative-netanyahu-israeli-pm)); [Q] "I have never said two states for two peoples." Supports "separation" for Jewish majority; against annexation of millions; against dismantling the PA; "minus-state"/autonomy talk (Cohen: autonomy "the maximum") ([JNS](https://www.jns.org/news/israel-news/smotrich-seizes-on-eisenkot-allys-remarks-to-warn-of-evacuations-from-judea-and-samaria)). Settlements must be "legal" and serve national interest; demolish illegal construction; reverse Smotrich's Settlement Administration; restore administrative detention for Jewish extremists; "iron fist" on settler raids; likely clear most new "farms" and freeze/limit new building, block E1 (analyst projections, [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-912193)). Rejects large-scale evacuation of existing settlements. Uses **יהודה ושומרון**, never "occupation".

**(e) Other.** State commission (Oct 7 + prior decade + war) — non-negotiable flagship. Universal service law with sanctions. Judiciary: independent courts, anti-overhaul, Basic Law: Legislation, PM term limits. Religion & state: "inclusive Judaism", local autonomy. Economy: cost of living, those who serve first. Foreign: repair with Europe and US Democrats.

## 5. Terminology sensitivities

- **Uses / needs:** "מדינה יהודית ודמוקרטית"; "ערכי מגילת העצמאות"; "ממלכתיות/ממלכתי"; "ציוני" (government must be "ממשלה ציונית"/"ממשלת גיוס ציונית" — Yashar must be able to call it Zionist); "שירות לכל"; "חמאס ארגון טרור" (must be named, and "dismantled" — פירוק); "יהודה ושומרון"; "100% מאזרחי ישראל"; "חיים משותפים".
- **For settler violence:** comfortable with "טרור" for attacks by Jewish extremists; possibly prefers "טרור לאומני" / "פשיעה לאומנית" or "Jewish terrorism" (he has reportedly used both — see 1b). "מאחזים בלתי חוקיים" / "חוות" acceptable; enforcement framed as **rule of law and security**, not as moral concession to Palestinians.
- **Cannot sign:** "כיבוש"/occupation/الاحتلال as a term of the government; "Nakba"; "two states"/"Palestinian state"/self-determination commitment; any wording equating IDF and Hamas, or "resistance" for Hamas; anything implying repeal of the Law of Return or Jewish character. "Palestinian people" — not found used by him; likely tolerable only descriptively ("הפלסטינים", "האוכלוסייה הפלסטינית"). "Territories under military control" — acceptable only in factual-security phrasing ("שטחים בשליטה צבאית/ביטחונית של צה"ל" / "יהודה ושומרון"), not as legal-moral indictment.
- Arabic: in Arabic text he accepts "دولة يهودية وديمقراطية"; he would object to "الاحتلال" in a joint government text.

## 6. Negotiation profile

**MUST-HAVES**
1. Eisenkot as PM (Netanyahu out; no rotation under Netanyahu).
2. State commission of inquiry (within 14 days).
3. Universal national-service law (military/civilian) incl. Haredim and Arabs, with incentives/sanctions.
4. Statement affirms Israel as Jewish and democratic per the Declaration; Hamas named terror org to be dismantled; Israel's security freedom of action.
5. A government he can call "Zionist" — Zionist parties hold the cabinet; any Arab role framed as outside support/agreement on civil agenda, not "dependence".
6. No Palestinian state commitment this term.

**Nice-to-haves:** Ra'am-type partner explicitly accepting the 4 rules (lets him say his test was met); Lieberman and Bennett inside; 61+ rather than minority; PM term limits; Basic Law: Legislation; administrative detention for Jewish terror; end Smotrich's dual ministry.

**Possible concessions (low cost to Yashar's worldview):** big civil package for Arab citizens (crime war, differential budgets, planning/housing, Kaminitz freeze/amendment, recognition of some Negev villages, civil-service track designed with Arab leadership); equality anchored at Basic-Law level / Declaration of Independence; explicit condemnation of Jewish terrorism and evacuation of illegal outposts/farms (already his position); halt to E1 and to new unauthorized outposts; workers' permits; PA not dismantled; "100% of citizens" language.

**Deal-breakers:** Netanyahu PM; amnesty-free from service for Haredim (exemption law); text using "occupation", Nakba, right of return, or a Palestinian-state commitment; refusal by a partner to call Hamas terror / to back its dismantling; a government visibly dependent on parties rejecting the Jewish-democratic definition (Joint List *inside* the cabinet is very hard; Ra'am outside or accepting the rules is doable); wording restricting IDF action in Gaza/Lebanon.

**What Yashar must show voters:** "We replaced the October-7 government"; "the draft era of evasion is over"; commission established; "Zionist government" with security professionals at Defense/Shin Bet; Arab cooperation presented as ממלכתיות and law-and-order (war on crime) under the 4 rules — not as ideological concessions.
