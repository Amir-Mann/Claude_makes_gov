Prepared 28 Oct 2026 by the simulated Yashar negotiating team. This is a simulation and does not speak for the real party.
Conventions:
- **[Q]** marks a verbatim quote found at the cited URL. Hebrew and Arabic are kept as published.
- **[Q-en]** marks a quote verified only in an English rendering by the outlet.
- **[P]** marks a paraphrase or a reporter's characterization.

Method note: Stage A used about 25 web searches (Hebrew, English, Arabic) and about 50 page fetches. The shared search budget ran out before every item was checked, and the items still open are marked UNVERIFIED. Haaretz premium pages and some sites (Israel Hayom, Channel 14, Al-Quds al-Arabi, Courthouse News) were paywalled or returned 403.

## 2. Ideological foundations (lifelong)

- **ממלכתיות** and Zionism "ברוח מגילת העצמאות". Israel is a Jewish and democratic state with "רוב יהודי מוצק לדורות" ([platform](https://yasharwitheisenkot.com/agenda_point/)).

  - Dahiya doctrine, 2008: "This is not a recommendation. This is a plan. And it has been approved." (Reuters, via [IMEU](https://imeu.org/resources/important-figures/6-things-to-know-about-yashar-party-leader-gadi-eisenkot/497))
  - Hamas's military wing "must be destroyed or dismantled" ([Al-Monitor 23.10.2026](https://al-monitor.com/originals/2026/10/eisenkot-offers-hawkish-alternative-netanyahu-israeli-pm)).
- **Discipline and rule of law inward.**
  - In 2016 he rejected "emptying a full magazine at a girl holding scissors" ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/idf-chief-under-fire-for-saying-soldiers-should-try-not-to-kill-young-attackers/)).
  - In 2023, on settler rampages: [Q] "זה כבר טרור לאומי ובעיניי זה לא יהודי", backing administrative detention of "טרוריסטים יהודים" ([INN 25.6.2023](https://www.inn.co.il/news/605590)).

## 7. Negotiating position for run 2

**Must-haves**
1. Eisenkot as PM. No rotation with Netanyahu, ever.
2. A state commission set up within 14 days, with the full mandate.
3. A Service-for-All law with equal principles for Haredim and Arabs: benefits tied to service for everyone. The government text may not carve out sanctions-protection for one population only.
4. The text calls it a **Zionist** government ("ממשלת גיוס ציונית ממלכתית"). Hamas: "ארגון טרור… יפורק/יושמד".
5. **Anyone holding a government post, including a deputy minister, publicly accepts the four rules.** If Ra'am does (Abbas has already affirmed Jewish and democratic and civil service; the missing piece is Hamas), Eisenkot can say "my test was met". If not, Ra'am gets outside support only, with no deputy minister.
6. No Palestinian state, no annexation; Israeli security freedom of action.

**Deal-breakers**
- Netanyahu as PM or in rotation.
- A Haredi exemption.
- "Occupation" or statehood wording.
- A Ra'am deputy minister without Ra'am accepting the four rules.
- A government whose investiture depends on Balad's abstention as an advertised, signed arrangement.
- A joint TV reading with the Joint List.

**Best alternative to this deal (honest ranking)**
1. **A broad government with Likud without Netanyahu (66).** It is Yashar's preferred outcome if available. It fits "Zionist, statist" and "Likud as a party is a partner", and Lieberman and Bennett prefer it. But Likud shows no sign of dropping Netanyahu (Ynet rates it "very low"), and Trump and Herzog are pushing Netanyahu *with* Eisenkot, which Yashar has sworn off.
2. **A narrow Zionist government with a Haredi party or part of Religious Zionism** (Maariv option 2). Shas is weakened (7). This needs Shas to accept the draft. Unlikely now.
3. **A minority government** on "broad national consensus" issues, with Ra'am outside. This is his own August formula and narrower than run 1's Chapter D. It is blocked by Lieberman.
4. **Lead the opposition and wait.** Netanyahu also lacks 61. Herzog may first task whoever has more recommendations, likely Eisenkot if the Arab parties recommend him.
5. **New elections.** He called them "a mistake", and Yashar fell from 26 to 21 in the count. The alternative is weak, which pushes Yashar toward a deal but also makes Lieberman the pivot.

**Tradeable (cheap by Yashar's worldview)**
- A civil package for Arab society: crime money, the Decision 550 successor, planning, a Kaminitz freeze for existing homes, recognition processes in the Negev, the Arabic language.
- Outpost evacuation; no annexation; restoring powers to the IDF (already Yashar's pledge).
- The holy-places status quo; mourning innocent civilians.
- Deputy Speaker posts (a Knesset matter, not government).
- Committee chairs.

**What rivals and voters will say the next morning**
- Likud: "You said the largest party's leader is the automatic candidate, and that Ra'am will not be part of the government. A government resting on Abbas and Balad = a 'stolen election'."
- Bennett and Lieberman: "Eisenkot crossed the Arab-party line first."
- Kahana, Cohen and Stern inside Yashar: they will resist a Ra'am deputy minister and any "no sanctions for Arabs" asymmetry while Haredim face sanctions.
- Shas-leaning voters: "double standard on service".

**Posture toward the run-1 texts**
