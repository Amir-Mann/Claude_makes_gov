# Shared brief for movement agents (stage A)

Context (as of 2026-10-28): Knesset election held 27 Oct 2026; preliminary results (~85% counted):
Likud 26, Yashar 21, Joint List 12, Together (Bennett+Lapid) 12, Democrats 8, Yisrael Beiteinu 7,
Ra'am 7, UTJ 7, Otzma 7, Shas 7, RZP 6. Netanyahu bloc ~53; Zionist opposition ~48; Arab lists 19.
An anti-Netanyahu majority (61) mathematically requires Arab-party support (coalition or outside support).

Task framing (from the user): a short-term (4-year) coalition statement that
1. acknowledges Israel as a Jewish (and democratic) state as of now, without discussing end goals;
2. condemns all terrorism, Hamas explicitly;
3. recognizes Arab citizens as equal citizens deserving representation and a government that acts for them;
4. acknowledges territories under military control, Jewish (settler) terrorism that must be stopped, and
   illegal unauthorized outposts (מאחזים) that should be evacuated;
designed so Bennett/Lieberman break few promises and Arab parties can show large real gains.

## Your stage-A job
Research ONLINE (WebSearch/WebFetch, Hebrew + Arabic + English sources: party sites, platforms/מצע/البرنامج,
Knesset, Ynet, Haaretz, Maariv, N12, Kan, ToI, JPost, Arab48, Panet, Bokra, Kul al-Arab, Al-Ittihad, etc.).
Give special weight to the last ~4 months (July–Oct 2026, the campaign) and election-night / post-election statements,
AND equally to lifelong / pivotal ideological statements: founding charters and platforms, the leader's defining speeches,
books and votes over their whole career (e.g. key Knesset votes, past coalition agreements, historic positions). Add a
section '1b. Ideological foundations' covering these, and flag where recent statements diverge from long-held positions.

Write your dossier to the file path you were given, structured as:
1. Identity: leader, list, 2026 seats, base, ideology in 3 lines.
2. Platform / core promises (with source links; original-language quotes, then English).
3. Explicit red lines & campaign promises re coalition partners (esp. on sitting with / relying on Arab parties,
   or Zionist parties, Netanyahu, Haredim).
4. Positions on each statement topic: (a) Jewish state / Nation-State Law; (b) terrorism, Hamas, Oct 7, Gaza, hostages;
   (c) Arab citizens: crime/violence, budgets (e.g. Decision 550 successors), planning/housing, Kaminitz law,
   Negev unrecognized villages, representation; (d) West Bank: settlements, outposts, settler violence, annexation,
   Palestinian state, terminology; (e) other coalition-relevant items: Oct 7 state commission, Haredi draft,
   judiciary, economy, religion & state.
5. Terminology sensitivities: words this movement must use / cannot sign (e.g. כיבוש vs יהודה ושומרון,
   "Jewish state", "Palestinian people", الاحتلال, "terrorism" for Palestinian armed acts, "Nakba").
   Note original language and nuance.
6. Negotiation profile: MUST-HAVES, nice-to-haves, possible concessions, deal-breakers, what they'd need to show voters.
7. Uncertainties / things you could not verify.

Rules: cite sources (URL) for every factual claim; never invent quotes — mark paraphrase vs. quote; keep original-language
text verbatim; note date of each statement. Be honest about uncertainty.
Stay in role afterwards: you will later be asked to REVIEW draft statements as this movement — supportive of reaching a
middle ground, but approving only if the movement's visible gains clearly outweigh its concessions by its OWN worldview.
