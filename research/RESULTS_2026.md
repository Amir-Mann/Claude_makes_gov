# Knesset 26 election (27 Oct 2026): neutral fact base

Compiled 28 Oct 2026 (late evening, Israel time) for the coalition-negotiation simulation.

**Status: all figures are PRELIMINARY.** On the evening of 28 Oct about 89.8–89.9% of votes had been counted. About 430,000–480,000 double-envelope votes (soldiers, diplomats, prisoners and others) were still uncounted. Their count was due to start at 01:00 on 29 Oct and finish that evening. One seat is worth about 40,000 votes, so seats can still move. The CEC publishes final official results by **4 Nov 2026**.

Legend:
- **[Q]**: verbatim quote found at the cited URL. Hebrew and Arabic quotes are in the original language.
- **[P]**: paraphrase by the source.
- **[UNVERIFIED]**: searched for but not confirmed.
- **[bg]**: background or legal knowledge that I did not re-verify online this session.

### 1b. Discrepancies between sources and over the course of the count

- Arabic outlets, as summarised in search results: the Arab lists' total rose from 15 seats (about 50% counted) to 16 (about 52%) and then to 19.
- So the "early source" figures (Likud 27, Arab lists 15–17, Netanyahu bloc 53–54) match mid-count snapshots from 50–85% counted. They do not match the latest snapshot (Likud 26, Arab lists 19, bloc 53).

**Vote totals do not match across outlets.** For example, Yashar has 825,023 on Wikipedia and 786,109 on Arab48. The outlets probably captured the CEC feed at different times. I have not reconciled the absolute numbers. Seat numbers agree across all outlets at the 89.8% snapshot.

**Exit polls (27 Oct, 22:00) were very different from the count:**
- Kan, Channel 12 and Channel 13: opposition about 70 (Arab parties 17–18, Zionist opposition 52–53), coalition 50.
- i24: 65–55.
- Channel 14: 60–60.
- Source: he.wikipedia (https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/הבחירות_לכנסת_העשרים_ושש) and ToI.

**Framing differences:**
- Haaretz reports "opposition 67" because it counts the Arab lists with the opposition.

## 3. Bloc arithmetic (89.8% snapshot)

| Bloc | Seats |
|---|---|
| Netanyahu bloc: Likud 26, Shas 7, UTJ 7, Otzma 7, RZP–Zehut 6 | **53** (68 in the outgoing Knesset) |
| Zionist opposition: Yashar 21, Together 12, Democrats 8, Yisrael Beiteinu 7 | **48** |
| Ra'am | **7** |
| Joint List | **12** |

These are arithmetic only. The political feasibility of each path depends on the constraints listed after the table.

### Paths to 61

| Combination | Seats | Main obstacle |
|---|---|---|
| Zionist opposition + Ra'am + Joint List | **67** | Lieberman rules out relying on Ra'am or the Joint List. The Joint List offers only outside support. |
| Zionist opposition + Ra'am + Joint List, without YB | **60** | One seat short. |
| Zionist opposition + Ra'am | 55 | Short. Needs abstentions (see below). |
| Zionist opposition + Joint List | 60 | Short. |
| Netanyahu bloc + YB | 60 | Short. Lieberman refuses to sit with Netanyahu. |
| Netanyahu bloc + Ra'am | 60 | Short. Abbas has said there is no chance of working with Netanyahu. |
| Netanyahu bloc + YB + Ra'am | 67 | Lieberman; Abbas. |
| Netanyahu bloc + Together | 65 | Bennett and Lapid pledge to replace Netanyahu. |
| Netanyahu bloc + Yashar | 74 | Eisenkot rejects serving under Netanyahu, including in a rotation. |
| Netanyahu bloc + Democrats | 61 | Not plausible. |
| Likud + Yashar + Together | 59 | Short. |
| Likud + Yashar + Together + YB | 66 | "Unity" government. Lieberman reportedly favours an Eisenkot–Netanyahu unity government (Channel 12 via ToI, 28 Oct, 20:30). |
| Likud + Yashar + Shas + UTJ | 61 | Haredi draft clash with Yashar's "גיוס לכל" platform. |
| Zionist-only coalition without Haredim and without Arab lists | Requires Likud, e.g. Likud + Zionist opposition = 74 | Lieberman's stated model. He says "Likud without Netanyahu could be a significant partner". |

### Minority government investiture

Investiture needs only more votes for than against (Basic Law: The Government; restated by TRT/Kan). With 120 MKs, a government with F supporters needs at least 121 − 2F abstentions or absences, assuming everyone else votes against.

| Government (F = votes for) | F | Abstentions needed | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zionist opposition + Ra'am | 55 | **11** | The Joint List (12) abstains: 55 to 53. |
| Zionist opposition alone, Ra'am votes for from outside | 55 | 11 | Same. |
| Zionist opposition + Ra'am + Joint List voting for | 67 | 0 | Majority. |
| Yashar + Together + Dem + Ra'am, Joint List voting for, YB against | 60 | **1** | 60 to 60 fails. At least one YB MK must abstain or be absent. |
| Yashar + Together + Dem + Ra'am, YB and Joint List against | 48 | 25 | Not feasible. |
| Zionist opposition alone, both Arab lists abstain | 48 | 25 | 48 to 53 fails. Needs 6 or more Netanyahu-bloc MKs absent as well. |
| Netanyahu bloc alone | 53 | **15** | Would need, for example, the Joint List (12) plus 3 more abstaining. Not plausible. |
| Netanyahu bloc + YB | 60 | 1 | 60 to 59 if one MK abstains. |

**Bottom line:** no anti-Netanyahu government is possible unless Yisrael Beiteinu at least abstains and at least one Arab list either votes for or abstains. The only 61+ coalitions without Arab lists require Likud (or the Netanyahu bloc) together with Yashar or Together. This holds unless the double envelopes move seats.

Lieberman (15 Oct, X/Haaretz, and repeated in the ynetnews article of about 17 Oct) [Q]: "We will not support any minority government, not even a minority government for a transitional period." https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h1jtvqw3fl

## 6. Comparison with research/BRIEF.md (run 1's assumed figures)

BRIEF.md stated: "~85% counted: Likud 26, Yashar 21, Joint List 12, Together 12, Democrats 8, YB 7, Ra'am 7, UTJ 7, Otzma 7, Shas 7, RZP 6. Netanyahu bloc ~53; Zionist opposition ~48; Arab lists 19. An anti-Netanyahu majority (61) mathematically requires Arab-party support."

| BRIEF claim | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| The per-list seats as listed | **CONFIRMED** for the 89.8% snapshot | All 11 numbers match the CEC at 89.8%. |
| "~85% counted" | **SLIGHTLY WRONG / outdated** | The latest count is 89.8–89.9%. At about 84.5–85% the blocs were 54 / 49 / 17 (ToI 12:39), not the BRIEF's figures. The BRIEF combined the 85% label with the later numbers. |
| Netanyahu bloc 53, Zionist opposition 48, Arab lists 19 | **CONFIRMED** (preliminary) | |
| Implied finality | **OMISSION** | The BRIEF does not say that about 430–480k double envelopes (about 10–12 seats' worth) were uncounted, or that final results come on 4 Nov. Seats can still shift. |
| "Anti-Netanyahu majority requires Arab-party support" | **CORRECT but incomplete** | It is correct for an anti-Netanyahu government. But the BRIEF did not mention the non-Arab paths to 61 that keep Likud in: Netanyahu bloc + Yashar (74) or + Together (65), or a Likud–Yashar–Together–YB "unity" (66). It also missed that even 48 + Ra'am + Joint List minus YB is only 60. Yisrael Beiteinu's abstention or support is therefore pivotal, and Lieberman was reportedly pushing an Eisenkot–Netanyahu unity government on 28 Oct. |
| Outside-support threshold not quantified | **OMISSION** | An opposition + Ra'am minority government (55) needs 11 abstentions, i.e. the Joint List abstaining. |
| List compositions (not in the BRIEF; in the run-1 dossiers) | Mostly **CONFIRMED** | Together = Bennett 2026 + Yesh Atid headed by Bennett. Joint List = Hadash + Ta'al + Balad headed by Jabareen. Abu Shehadeh withdrew. Odeh is no longer chair. One caveat: run-1 yashar.md says "~786k votes, ~16%". Arab48 gives 786,109, while Wikipedia gives 825,023 (16.20%). This is a snapshot-timing difference. |
| Arab lists "19" as "a record" (dossiers) | CONFIRMED as reported ("historic high", Haaretz and others) | |
| Threshold failures | Not in the BRIEF | Hendel's Reservists 2.63% and Winter's Amcha Yisrael 1.60% were wasted. Together that is about 4.2% of the vote, mostly right and centre-right. |

**Net:** run 1's seat table was right for the latest snapshot. Its errors were:
- the percentage-counted label (85% instead of about 90%);
- treating preliminary figures as settled, ignoring the uncounted double envelopes and the 4 Nov final date;
- framing the arithmetic only as "anti-Netanyahu needs Arabs", without the unity and Likud-inclusive paths, Yisrael Beiteinu's veto, or the exact abstention numbers for a minority government.
