# The Democrats (הדמוקרטים / الديمقراطيون) — Stage-A dossier

Prepared 28 Oct 2026 by the Democrats movement agent. Quotes are marked **[Q]** (verbatim, original language kept) or **[P]** (paraphrase). Every claim has a source. Items marked **[GK]** come from general background knowledge and were not re-checked online this session. Treat them as needing verification.

## 1. Identity

- **Leader:** Yair Golan (יאיר גולן). He is a former IDF Deputy Chief of Staff, was a Meretz MK in 2020–22 and Deputy Economy Minister in the Bennett-Lapid government, won the Labor leadership on 28 May 2024 with about 95% of the vote, and has chaired the merged party since July 2024. Sources: [Wikipedia – Yair Golan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yair_Golan), [Wikipedia – The Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Democrats_(Israel)).
- **List:** Labor and Meretz merged. Golan announced the party on 18 Mar 2024, the merger was agreed on 30 Jun 2024 and approved on 12 Jul 2024, and members ratified it on 31 May 2026. Labor and Meretz remain separate legal and budgetary entities. Meretz is guaranteed slots 6, 8 and 14. Slots 12, 13, 18, 23 and 27 are reserved for rural and minority (Arab, Druze, Circassian) candidates. Soumaya/Somaya Bashir (Arab) is about 10th. Ali Salalha is 18th and Ghaleb Salamaneh 23rd. Naama Lazimi is #2. Gaby Lasky (#6) and Michal Rozin (#8) are ex-Meretz. Protest leaders on the list include Moran Zer Katzenstein, Omri Ronen, Ami Dror, Moshe Radman and Nava Rozolyo. Sources: [Wikipedia – Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Democrats_(Israel)), [Wikipedia – 2026 primaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_The_Democrats_primaries), [JPost 20 Jul 2026](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-903068).
- **2026 seats:** about 8, up from 4 Labor MKs. Source: [JPost 28 Oct 2026](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-913064).
- **Base:** secular, liberal Ashkenazi middle class (Tel Aviv, Gush Dan, kibbutzim), veterans of the 2023–26 anti-government protests, and a small but symbolically central Arab and Druze vote.
- **Ideology:**
  1. Zionist left and social democracy. The party calls itself "the political home of the liberal-democratic camp."
  2. Separation from the Palestinians and two states, framed as the way to keep Israel "Jewish and democratic." The party fights annexation and Jewish terror.
  3. Liberal democracy (judiciary, LGBTQ, religion and state), a welfare state, and an explicit Jewish-Arab civic partnership.

Haaretz, May 2026: Golan [Q] "דוחה לעתיד את מימוש חזון שתי המדינות" (defers the two-state vision to the future) ([Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/elections/2026-05-19/ty-article-magazine/.premium/0000019e-3517-d2ac-a1fe-b75fa6180000)). Meretz's line is sharper than the campaign line.
2. **Arab partnership was hedged before the election and is maximal after it.** On 6 Oct he would only say Ra'am could join and dodged on the Joint List: [Q] "לא ארקוד לפי חלילו של נתניהו" ([Walla](https://www.walla.co.il/news/politics/383956431)). ## 4. Positions on the statement topics

**(a) Jewish state and the Nation-State Law.**
- The movement fully embraces "Jewish and democratic" ("יהודית ודמוקרטית"). It uses the phrase itself as the reason to halt annexation ([Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel-elections/2026/Article-53c6ffa50321f91027.htm)), and the platform speaks of a "solid Jewish majority."
- It opposes the Nation-State Law as written: amend it "in the spirit of the Declaration of Independence" (platform) or repeal it (founding position). The key gap is that the law lacks the word equality.
- A statement that says "Jewish and democratic" with no end-goal clause is easy for the Democrats to sign. It must not endorse the Nation-State Law, and it should ideally reference the Declaration of Independence and equality.

**(b) Terrorism, Hamas, Oct 7, Gaza, hostages.**
- Condemns Hamas unreservedly and wants Hamas rule toppled and Hamas disarmed. Golan's personal Oct 7 record anchors this.
- Insists on symmetry: [Q, X post] "יש מפלגה אחת שתדע לטפל בטרור. אנחנו נטפל בטרור הפלסטיני והיהודי, ביד ברזל" (one party knows how to handle terror: we will handle Palestinian and Jewish terror with an iron fist) ([X](https://x.com/YairGolan1/status/2113124013216260364)).
- The party backed a hostage deal and an end to the war ([JDN](https://www.jdn.co.il/?p=2218352)), and has said Hamas and Netanyahu keep each other alive ([Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1175506)).
- Day after in Gaza: a technocratic Palestinian government, the PA's role, the Trump plan.
- It wants a state commission of inquiry into Oct 7 immediately.

**(c) Arab citizens.**
- Crime: the NIS 1bn per year plan and a demand for the Public Security Ministry (§2).
- Budgets: renew and fully implement the five-year plan (Takadum / Decision 550 line) and reverse the diversions to policing.
- Representation: Arab and Druze candidates in reserved slots and a call for Arab parties in government. Golan said [Q] "We are working internally to ensure appropriate Arab representation."
- Kaminitz law, planning, and the Negev unrecognized villages: **no specific 2026 statement found.**
  - Track record: the Bennett-Lapid coalition the movement sat in agreed to amend Kaminitz and freeze enforcement ([Algemeiner](https://www.algemeiner.com/2021/06/08/coalition-agreements-revealed-as-israel-prepares-for-new-government/)).
  - Meretz has historically backed recognizing the villages [GK].
  - Inference: the movement would readily sign Kaminitz amendment, planning and recognition commitments.
- Equality frame, in Arabic: "شراكة يهودية-عربية حقيقية قائمة على المساواة والتأثير" (a real Jewish-Arab partnership based on equality and influence) ([Bokra](https://bokra.net/Article-1604610)).

**(d) West Bank.**
- Against annexation, Jewish terror and outposts:
  - 26 Jul 2026, Arabic-language message to Arab society: [Q] "הטרור היהודי מבעיר את השטחים" (Jewish terror is setting the territories ablaze). He promised [Q] "לפרק את המאחזים" (to dismantle the outposts) and [Q] "לטפל ביד ברזל בטרור היהודי באמצעות השב"כ והמשטרה" (to deal with Jewish terror with an iron fist through the Shin Bet and police) ([INN](https://www.inn.co.il/news/702473)).
  - [Q] "יותר מ-100 מאחזים בלתי חוקיים שהתרבו בתקופת הממשלה הזאת כפטריות אחרי הגשם" (more than 100 illegal outposts that multiplied under this government like mushrooms after rain) ([ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/hkzz56nsmx)).
  - After the Taybeh attack on European diplomats (17 Oct 2026): [Q] "Jewish terror must be fought with an iron fist, exactly as Palestinian terror is fought… Administrative detention must be reinstated against dangerous Jewish terror activists… terror hubs must be dismantled, illegal outposts evacuated, rioters arrested… There is no immunity for Jewish terrorists" ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/9vrtb37za)).
- Settlements in general: [P] he says the illegal outposts should be removed, but no one thinks all settlements should be dismantled (podcast, via search summary). Past positions: keep most settlement blocs, with land swaps ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yair_Golan)).
- Palestinian state: the long-term goal is a demilitarized state, but timing and borders are deferred. Meretz wants it stated more explicitly.
- He wants authority over the West Bank returned from Smotrich to the IDF and the Defense Ministry.

**(e) Other coalition-relevant items.**
- State commission of inquiry into Oct 7: a must.

- Repair of relations with the US ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912335)).

## 5. Terminology sensitivities

| Term | Democrats' usage and limits |
|---|---|
| **כיבוש / "occupation"** | Meretz and the base use it freely. The founding identity is "the only Zionist party opposing the occupation." Golan's 2026 campaign documents avoid the word and say "השטחים" (the territories), "יו"ש" (Judea and Samaria, abbreviated) or "the West Bank." The Democrats can sign "territories under military control" ("שטחים בשליטה צבאית") and would welcome it. They **cannot sign wording that denies occupation or asserts sovereignty or a right to annex.** |
| **יהודה ושומרון** | Used descriptively, also by Golan ("ברחבי יהודה ושומרון"). It is acceptable as a geographic name, but should not be the only term in the text. Meretz prefers "הגדה המערבית" / "השטחים". |
| **טרור יהודי / Jewish terror** | **Must-use.** It is the movement's signature term. A softer phrase ("violent extremists," "hilltop youth," "אלימות מתנחלים" (settler violence) only) would be a visible loss. "Jewish terror" or "Jewish terrorism" must appear, ideally alongside Palestinian terror ("exactly as"). |
| **מאחזים בלתי חוקיים** | **Must-use**, with "evacuation" ("פינוי" / "פירוק"). Weaker verbs such as "regulate" or "legalize" are unacceptable. Legalizing outposts ("הסדרה") is a red flag. |
| **"Jewish and democratic state"** | Fully embraced. They prefer adding equality or the Declaration of Independence. They will not endorse the "Nation-State Law" by name. |
| **Hamas = terrorist organization; Oct 7 "massacre" ("טבח")** | Fully embraced. Golan uses "massacre" as the red line, as in the Abu Shehadeh case. |
| **"Palestinian people," "Palestinian state"** | Comfortable with both. Golan's plan names a "Palestinian state" (demilitarized) and a "Palestinian government." Silence about end goals is acceptable to Golan but costly with Meretz voters. |

## 6. Negotiation profile

**MUST-HAVES**
1. A government without Netanyahu, Likud's current leadership, Otzma, RZP or the Haredi parties.
2. The Arab parties are legitimate partners, at least through outside support plus real policy gains. The Democrats will not sign anything that frames Arab votes as illegitimate or "non-Zionist votes don't count." Golan has staked his post-election identity on this.
3. Explicit naming of **Jewish terror**, its enforcement (Shin Bet, police, administrative detention "according to law") and **evacuation of illegal outposts**.
4. No annexation, and no legalization of outposts.
5. A state commission of inquiry into Oct 7.
6. Equal draft legislation.
7. A national emergency plan on crime in Arab society. They want the Public Security Ministry.
8. Renewal of the Arab-society five-year plan.

**Nice-to-haves**
- Amending the Nation-State Law with an equality clause, or at least a Declaration of Independence reference.
- The word "occupation," or "territories under military control."
- Restoring West Bank powers to the IDF.
- PA security cooperation and reform.
- A Kaminitz amendment and recognition of Negev villages.
- Civil marriage and Shabbat transport.
- Judicial safeguards and term limits.

**Possible concessions**
- Silence on end goals: no "two states" language for four years. Golan has already deferred two states; Meretz will grumble.
- Accepting "Jewish and democratic" without an explicit repeal of the Nation-State Law.
- Settlements as such, not outposts: no evacuation, no freeze clause.
- Ministerial seniority, if they get Public Security or a social portfolio.
- Accepting Ra'am plus outside support from the Joint List rather than full Joint List membership.

**Deal-breakers**
- Any annexation or sovereignty clause, or any outpost legalization.
- Omitting Jewish terror, or framing settler violence as "isolated incidents."
- Excluding Arab citizens or Arab parties in principle, including a "Zionist majority" requirement on key votes.
- Including Haredi parties or Netanyahu.
- Dropping the Oct 7 commission.
- Language that legitimizes support for the Oct 7 massacre. The flip side is that the Democrats also need the statement to condemn Hamas explicitly.

**What they must show voters**
1. That Golan's "65 vs. 55" partnership gamble produced a government that includes Arab citizens.
2. A visible, enforceable crackdown on Jewish terror and outposts.
3. That Golan, not Ben-Gvir's successor, runs the crime fight.
4. That Golan delivered the Oct 7 commission.

**Internal nuances**
- **Meretz:** Lasky, Rozin and the activist base want occupation, Palestinian-state and equality language.
- **Labor and centrists:** Golan, Lazimi and the protest leaders are comfortable with security framing and with deferring end goals.
- **The Abu Shehadeh vote** showed Golan will take a hard line against perceived support for terror even at the cost of Arab votes. Kariv's dissent shows internal pressure the other way.
