## 1. Identity and latest result

- **Leader:** Yair Golan, born 1962. He is a former IDF Deputy Chief of Staff and Deputy Minister of Economy (2021–22) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yair_Golan)).
- **List (top 10):** Golan, Lazimi, Kariv, Rayten, Fink, Lasky (Meretz), Ronen, Rozin (Meretz), Radman, Bashir ([JPost 20 Jul](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-903068)).
- **Latest result:** **8 seats, 6.52%**, at 89.8% of votes counted (14:32 on 28 Oct) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)). Labor had 4 in the outgoing Knesset.
  - **All lists:** Likud 26 · Yashar 21 · Joint List 12 · Together 12 · Democrats 8 · Ra'am 7 · Yisrael Beiteinu 7 · UTJ 7 · Otzma 7 · Shas 7 · RZP–Zehut 6.
  - **Blocs:** Netanyahu 53, change bloc 48, Arab parties 19. Walla at 88% had the same blocs ([Walla](https://www.walla.co.il/news/israel/383959177)).
  - **Still open:** double envelopes (about 600–650k) were still being counted. Official results are due 4 Nov.
- **Arithmetic that defines us:** without our 8 seats the Zionist change bloc has 40. No change government exists without the Democrats. We are also the smallest, most left-wing and most vetoed partner.
- **Faction:** no Arab MK is in the elected eight (row 5 above). This is a credibility gap in our partnership message.
- **Election-day context:** a knife-carrying man was arrested near Golan at a polling station, and activists in Holon were attacked. The party blames Netanyahu's incitement ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)).

## 2. Ideological foundations (lifelong)

- **Labor heritage:** Rabin and territorial compromise. The old platform spoke of "שתי מדינות לאום" (two nation-states) ([Zo Haderekh](https://zoha.org.il/145596/)).
- **Meretz heritage:** [Q] "הקמת מדינה פלסטינית עצמאית לצידה של ישראל היא אינטרס לאומי, ביטחוני ומדיני ראשון במעלה" (an independent Palestinian state beside Israel is a national, security and diplomatic interest of the first order) ([Zo Haderekh](https://zoha.org.il/145596/)). Also full civil equality and secularism.
- **Merger (2024):** two states, repeal of the Nation-State Law, no occupation or settlement of Gaza, and the PA integral to the "day after" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Democrats_(Israel))).
- **Golan's moral-courage brand:**
  - the 2016 "processes" speech;
  - the 2022 Burqa remarks ([Q] "עושים פוגרום… הם תתי אדם") ([Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/bydkim4hf));
  - the May 2025 "babies as a hobby" remark, later walked back ([Arab News/AP](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2601502)).

  The right still uses all three against him.
- **Security credential:** on 7 Oct he went into the Gaza envelope as a civilian and rescued survivors. He called the day "the worst defeat of the State of Israel ever" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yair_Golan)).
- **Precedent:** Labor and Meretz sat in the 2021–22 government that relied on Ra'am. Its deals froze enforcement against illegal building until 2024 and suspended Negev demolitions for nine months ([Algemeiner](https://www.algemeiner.com/2021/06/08/coalition-agreements-revealed-as-israel-prepares-for-new-government/)).

## 7. Negotiating position for run 2

**Leverage.** The Zionist change bloc is 48 with us and 40 without us. Eisenkot cannot form a change government without our 8. In run 1 we behaved like the junior partner grateful to be in. We are not.

**Real must-haves (walk-away items)**
1. **Portfolio:** Public Security for Golan (our signature crime plan), or Defense, plus Education for Lazimi. Justice would be a fallback. A government that gives Golan transport or energy, as Bennett suggested on 8 Oct, would be humiliating, and our voters would read it as Bennett's veto succeeding.
   - Run 1 put the Arab-crime plan under a Ra'am deputy minister "in the ministry responsible for policing". That works for us **only if that minister is ours**.
2. **Arab partnership as a value, not a necessary evil.** The text must not single out the Arab parties as second-class: no "Zionist majority" clause and no Yisrael Beiteinu reservation phrased as a public boycott in the signed document. We want Ra'am as a **full coalition member** with a minister, not only a deputy minister. If Bennett and Liberman refuse, the floor is run 1's parliamentary agreement **plus** Ra'am in coalition management and the monitoring committee.
3. **Jewish terror, outposts and no annexation.** Keep clauses 10, 13 and 14 as written in run 1, with these additions:
   - re-establish the police's Judea and Samaria district;
   - administrative detention for Jewish terror suspects;
   - **no new settlements and no expansion beyond approved plans**, written into clause 13 itself rather than an annex reservation;
   - no budget money beyond what the law requires.
4. **Judiciary.** Run 1's preamble ("any change to the judicial system… only with broad consent") would let Bennett block repeal of the 2023–26 overhaul laws. We need an explicit carve-out: **restoring** the pre-overhaul status and the independence of the gatekeepers is not a "change requiring broad consent".
5. A 7 Oct state commission at the first meeting (already in run 1), and a Service Law with no exemption quotas (already in clause 6a).

**Deal-breakers**
- Any annexation or sovereignty language, or outpost legalization beyond decisions already taken.
- A Haredi party, Likud under Netanyahu, or any current minister in the government.
- A text or public side-letter that delegitimizes Arab votes ("we will not rely on…" written into the signed document).
- A Golan veto accepted in writing.

**Our best alternative**
- **Opposition** against a Likud-without-Netanyahu unity government (Yashar + Together + Yisrael Beiteinu + Likud). We would become the main left opposition alongside the Joint List, consistent with our brand, but powerless.
- **New elections:** risky. We polled 9–10 and got 8, and Eisenkot's big-party campaign is eating our voters.
- **Abstaining or supporting from outside:** not credible for a Zionist party that wants Public Security.

Our alternative is worse than a decent deal but better than a humiliating one. **We will not sign a deal in which Golan gets a non-security portfolio *and* Ra'am is held at arm's length.**

**What we could trade**
- "Occupation" and "Palestinian state" stay out of the joint text and the TV reading. We already accept "military control", clause 15's silence and clause 3's reservation.
- Accept Eisenkot as PM despite the campaign spat.
- Accept clause 4 (Jewish and democratic) without repeal of the Nation-State Law.
- Defer religion and state (civil marriage, Shabbat transport) to the coalition agreement, in exchange for a minimum such as Shabbat public transport pilots in willing cities.
- Give up Justice if we get Public Security and Education.
- Accept Ra'am outside the cabinet if Ra'am gets a deputy minister in *our* ministry and a full place in coalition management.

**What rivals and voters will attack us for**
- **Right and Likud:** "babies as a hobby", "subhumans", "a government with supporters of terror", "Golan in charge of the police".
- **Bennett and Liberman:** they will brand Golan "controversial" and try to sideline him.
- **Joint List and Balad:** we voted to disqualify Abu Shehadeh; we sit with Liberman; we accept "military control" and continued settlement construction; Golan "dodged" the Joint List.
- **Meretz base and Haaretz left:** no "occupation", no Palestinian state, Ra'am only outside, and Golan accepting a lesser ministry.
- **Next morning:** if we cave on the portfolio, "Bennett decides who Golan is". If we hold out and there is no government, "Golan blocked the change government".
