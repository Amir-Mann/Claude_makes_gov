## 2. Ideological foundations (lifelong)

1. **State of all its citizens:** Balad's founding thesis, written by Bishara, that Israel should be transformed from a Jewish state into
   «دولة جميع مواطنيها» / "מדינת כל אזרחיה", with Arab citizens recognised as a **national minority** with cultural and educational
   autonomy. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balad_(political_party) ; https://en.idi.org.il/israeli-elections-and-parties/parties/balad/
2. **Palestinian Arab national identity:** Balad defines itself as "a Palestinian Arab nationalist party" (en.wikipedia).
   Its programme calls for:
   - full withdrawal to 1967;
   - a sovereign Palestinian state with East Jerusalem as its capital;
   - the right of return;
   - de-recognition of the Jewish Agency and JNF;
   - separation of religion and state;
   - **an end to drafting Arab citizens into military or civilian service** (he.wikipedia, 2019 platform).
3. **No legitimisation of Zionist governments:**
   - It has opposed every state budget since its founding (en.wikipedia).
   - It never sat in a coalition.
   - **Sept 2019:** it refused to recommend Gantz. The letter said Gantz "supports the annexation of Jerusalem and the Golan", "threaten[s]
     a war in Gaza" and will not annul "the racist Nation-State Law". https://www.newarab.com/news/palestinian-balad-party-refuses-endorse-equally-racist-gantz
   - **March 2020:** it went along with the 15-MK recommendation of Gantz, "out of responsibility to voters and to keep the list united". This is
     the only exception, and its extent is contested (row 21).
   - **May 2021:** Abu Shehadeh recommended no one, while 5 of the 6 Joint List MKs recommended Lapid. https://maki.org.il/en/?p=27570
   - **Sept 2022:** Balad left the Joint List because it refused any recommendation of a Zionist candidate, then failed the threshold.
     https://www.timesofisrael.com/balad-leader-accuses-lapid-or-orchestrating-split-with-joint-list/
4. **Permanent target of disqualification:**
   - CEC bans in 2003, 2009, 2019 (with Ra'am) and 2022, all overturned by the Supreme Court.
   - In 2026 the list was banned and the ban overturned unanimously.
   - Its chair was removed in effect: the CEC voted 31–4, with every Zionist opposition party in favour.
   - ToI liveblog (23 Sep 2026); https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/2/israels-supreme-court-overturns-election-panel-ban-on-arab-parties
5. **Defining controversies:**
   - Bishara's 2007 departure under treason-related charges (en.wikipedia).
   - Basel Ghattas's 2017 conviction (ToI).
   - Zoabi's 2014 remark that the kidnappers of the three teens "were not terrorists" (en.wikipedia).
   - Abu Shehadeh's 8 Oct 2023 article.

## 3. 2026 platform, campaign red lines and promises about partners

- "Stop the war, put an end to the occupation and achieve a just and comprehensive peace"
- "an independent, sovereign Palestinian state on the borders of 4 June 1967 with East Jerusalem as its capital"
- "a just solution to the refugee issue and their right of return in accordance with international resolutions"
- "We struggle to end the occupation, stop the genocide and establish the Palestinian state"
- "We fight annexation, settlement, displacement and settler violence"
- Repeal of "the 'Nation-State Law', the 'Kaminitz Law', the 'Nakba Law' and the 'NGO Law'"
- "recognition of the Palestinian Arab public as an indigenous national group"
- "We reject military and 'civil' service schemes, all forms of conscription"
- "We work to halt the home demolitions resulting from the planning siege"
- "building parliamentary understandings as equals, based on respect for the rights of the indigenous minority"
- The platform never uses "terrorism", "Hamas" or "hostages".

## 5. Positions on the framework topics

**(a) Jewish state / Nation-State Law.**
- «זה אוקסימורון. זה לא תקין וזה לא עובד» (ynet, Jun 2026, about "Jewish and democratic").
- The Nation-State Law is «قانون القومية العنصري» and must be replaced by «قانون أساس المساواة» (Al-Araby, 8 Oct 2026).
- Balad cannot affirm "Jewish and democratic", nor the Declaration of Independence as *its* founding text.
- It can live with a clause that a *government* describes existing law, as long as Balad is explicitly not endorsing it.

**(b) Terrorism, Hamas, 7 Oct, Gaza, hostages.**
- Abu Shehadeh's 8 Oct 2023 article called the attack "an important historical event from a military, political and strategic
  perspective" (as quoted by the petitioners; ynetnews yrsnwoll2).
- In court, Oct 2026:
  - "The article contains things that were wrong… I would not say them today" (JPost).
  - Asked for any article calling Hamas a terror organisation: «אין מאמר כזה» (ynet).
- He said he "oppose[s] armed struggle" (ToI).
- JPost says he "later condemned the massacre" (P).
- Balad's 2026 vocabulary for Gaza is «الإبادة» (genocide) and «تفتك بأهلنا في غزة».
- The platform never uses "terrorism" or "Hamas".
- **Implication:** Balad will not call Hamas "a terrorist organisation" in its own voice. It will not endorse "disarming Hamas" as a
  government aim it signs, and it will not commit to deciding who governs Gaza. At most it can condemn "every deliberate harm to civilians,
  Jews and Arabs alike". Naming 7 Oct is possible only alongside Gaza, and even that is costly after the court episode.
- Hostages: no 2026 Balad statement found. Balad's frame is a comprehensive ceasefire and a prisoner exchange (UNVERIFIED this session).

**(c) Arab citizens.**
- Crime: «הפכנו להיות אחת החברות הכי מסוכנות בעולם» (ynet).
- Balad wants civilian policing and state accountability. It rejects any Shin Bet role (Arab leadership position, not re-verified for
  2026).
- Planning and housing: «الهدم» (demolition) is a core grievance. The platform says "halt the home demolitions"; repeal of Kaminitz.
- Negev: recognition of unrecognised villages (platform, under displacement and demolition).
- Representation: recognition as an "indigenous national group"; an end to «التحريض» and disqualification.
- **Service:** "We reject military and 'civil' service schemes, all forms of conscription."

**(d) West Bank.**
- «الاحتلال» ("occupation") is the mandatory term. Balad wants a state on 4 June 1967 lines with East Jerusalem as its capital, and
  opposes annexation, settlement and settler violence.
- It considers *all* settlements illegal, not only outposts.
- "Military control" is acceptable only as a description *by others*.

**(e) Other topics.**
- 7 Oct commission: no Balad statement found. It would not oppose one, provided it is not turned against Arab citizens (inference).
- Haredi draft: Balad opposes conscription generally. It has no stake in Haredi service except that "Service for All" drags Arab citizens
  in.
- Judiciary: it opposes the overhaul, but is now bitter at the Supreme Court and the AG, who backed removing its chair.
- Religion and state: full separation.

## 7. Negotiating position for run 2

**Starting point.**
- The Arab public turned out at about 80% "to bring down this government". Balad's 3 seats are now arithmetically decisive (section 1).
- The same Zionist parties that ask for our abstention all voted to disqualify our chair three weeks before the election.
- Balad's whole history is refusal: 2019, 2021, 2022. The 2020 exception is remembered as a failure, because Gantz went to Netanyahu.

**Real must-haves** (for even an abstention):
1. **No Balad signature** on "Jewish and democratic", on Hamas-as-terror or "disarm Hamas", on the Service Law, or on anything that reads
   as a loyalty test. In the run-1 Annex 1 we only "note" listed clauses. That structure is acceptable; the listed items must stay
   limited.
2. **Public de-delegitimisation.** The PM-designate must state publicly that the Arab parties are legitimate partners in parliament.
   He must also commit that the government will not initiate or support new disqualification petitions or "loyalty" legislation, and will
   support reform of Section 7A of the Basic Law: The Knesset (the provision under which lists and candidates are disqualified).
   Eisenkot's 22 Sep line is the wound.
3. **Arab citizens are fully out of the Service Law.** The run-1 clause 6a still says the duty "applies to all" and creates an Arab
   civil-national track. The platform rejects "all forms of conscription", so Balad votes **against** that law whatever its
   protections. Its Annex 1 must say so explicitly.
4. **Chapter D money and the demolition freeze as written:** Kaminitz freeze, Negev no-demolition, at least NIS 1bn a year for crime
   with **no Shin Bet role in civilian policing**, the Decision 550 successor. Each needs a date.
5. **No annexation, outposts evacuated, settler terror named, al-Aqsa status quo.** These are already in run-1 clauses 10, 13, 14 and
   15a, and Balad keeps them as gains.
6. **"Political horizon" language:** at minimum, a reference to ending the occupation as the Joint List's reserved position, plus the
   Palestinian people's right to self-determination as a reserved position under clause 3. Balad will not abstain on a text that
   pretends there is no occupation.

**Real deal-breakers.**
- Any wording that puts Balad's own name under clause 4, 6a or 9, or makes Balad's leader say "Hamas terror" or "our fallen".
- Budgets that fund settlement or war beyond existing law. Even without them, Balad's tradition is to vote **against** budgets.
  The most it can offer is **not to be present** for budget votes (absence, not abstention), and only if Chapter D is funded in that
  budget.
- A government whose senior partners (Bennett, Lieberman) publicly say they will "not count on" us while counting on us.
  Run-1 Annex 2 records exactly that for Yisrael Beiteinu. Balad can survive silence; it cannot survive being humiliated in public.

**Best alternative to this deal (BATNA).**
- Opposition is Balad's natural home and costs it little with its base.
- A Netanyahu–Eisenkot unity government, or a Likud-without-Netanyahu government, keeps Balad in opposition with clean hands.
- New elections are risky: Netanyahu's camp would campaign on Arabs having "stolen" the election. But the Joint List would likely hold
  11–13 seats.
- **The real cost of walking away:** being blamed by Ra'am, Hadash and our own voters for Netanyahu's return. "Balad again let the
  right back in", as after the 2022 split, is the attack we fear most.

**What we could trade.**
- Accept Annex 1 (no support for no-confidence or dissolution) while Chapter D is implemented.
- Abstain on investiture, not vote for it, and only as a unified Joint List decision. An abstention is not a recommendation.
- Accept "territories held under military control" as the government's description (clause 12), with our reservation "occupation"
  recorded.
- Accept a symmetric condemnation of harm to civilians, "Jews and Arabs alike", in the framework. Balad does not repeat it on TV.
- Not demand "state of all its citizens" or Nation-State Law repeal in the text, if the "no endorsement" disclaimer in clause 4 stays.

**What rivals and voters will attack us for.**
- *From the right:* "terror supporters" (the 8 Oct article) and "Arabs stole the election".
- *From Ra'am:* "Balad shouts, we deliver". If Ra'am signs and Balad is the spoiler, Abbas wins the Arab street.
- *From our base and the national camp (Follow-Up Committee, students):* "You held up a government that disqualified our chair, demands
  'Jewish and democratic', and wants to disarm the resistance and draft our youth."
- *From Hadash and Ta'al:* that Balad is again putting the list's unity at risk, as in 2022.

**Posture toward the run-1 texts.**
- **Framework (`final/statement_en.md`): accept Annex 1 only, with demands.**
  - Annex 1 must add explicit reservations: rejection of clauses 4, 6a and 9; "occupation" as our term for clause 12; all settlements are
    illegal.
  - Clause 20's deputy Speaker for the Joint List is welcome.
  - Clause 16's Shin Bet role must be dropped, or Balad must be recorded as opposing it.
  - Run-1 Annex 2 says Balad "abstains on budgets". Replace this with "is absent from budget votes conditional on clause 13 and Chapter D;
    otherwise votes against". A formal abstention on a Zionist budget breaks a 30-year line.
  - Strike Yisrael Beiteinu's "will not count on the Joint List's votes, and says so publicly", or Balad's Annex 1 lapses.
- **TV statement (`final/tv_statement.md`): reject for Balad as written.** Balad's leader will not read it in the first person:
  - "I condemn the massacre Hamas committed": after «אין מאמר כזה» in court, our base would read this as capitulation;
  - "to ensure Hamas does not rule Gaza": not ours to decide;
  - "our fallen";
  - "a small extremist minority": settler violence is state-backed;
  - "as the Declaration of Independence promised".

  Abu Shehadeh is not an MK, and he would not stand on a stage with leaders who voted to disqualify him. If a joint appearance is
  essential, Balad offers its own two lines: on bringing down the government and ending the war, and on equality, demolitions and crime.
